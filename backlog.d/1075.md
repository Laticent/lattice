# cards-stack: per-card overflow is unguarded (slide-level probe misses card-level clip)

## Summary

`cards-stack compact` can clip an individual **card's** content (text spilling out of its card box into the inter-card gap) **without any overflow signal**, because the overflow detector (`lib/core/overflow-probe.js`) probes the section and the bounded content cells in `CLIP_CELL_SELECTOR` (`.cell-stage, .panel-right, .compare-right`) — not the individual cards. A card that overflows its own box but stays within the section reports zero slide-level overflow.

Surfaced by the adversarial-trio review of #1070, where the generated anti-pattern slide ("When NOT to reach for X") switched to `cards-stack compact`.

## Why it matters now

#1070 made **58 component galleries'** anti-pattern slides render as `cards-stack compact`, and **7 components already sit at the 4-card hard ceiling** (`policy-recommendation`, `citation-card`, `inventory`, `cards-grid`, `agenda`, `verdict-grid`, `pricing`) — zero headroom. Two silent-failure paths:

1. A future manifest adds a long 4th anti-pattern → that card clips with no warning.
2. A future retune of `cards-stack`'s `compact` density / `--fs-body-compact` / capacity / pill rules → up to 58 anti-pattern slides shift or clip at once, for components whose authors never knew they were downstream consumers.

The only current guard is `component-galleries.test.js`, which asserts **page count only** — an overflowing card still counts as 1 slide, so it's blind to this.

## Repro

Not reachable by today's content (current 4-item max is ~116 words, renders clean). Triggers at ~4 cards × ~50-word bodies (~200 words total): the first card's last line spills below its card box into the gap before card 2, **with no overflow warning printed**. (Red-team artifact: a `max-antipatterns.md` built from the repo's own longest real bodies.)

## Proposed direction (not yet scoped)

- Either add the cards-stack card element to the probed clip-cell contract (so a clipped card surfaces as section-equivalent overflow, like `.cell-stage`), **or** add a targeted integration guard that renders a max-content anti-pattern slide and asserts no card-level clip.
- This touches shared overflow machinery (`overflow-probe.js`) and/or the cards-stack CSS clip contract — real blast radius, so it wants its own PR + maker-checker, not a drive-by.

## Not a regression in #1070

Every currently-shipping anti-pattern and closing-`index` slide renders cleanly in both themes (verified). This is a **latent** guard gap, not a live defect.
