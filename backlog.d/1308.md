## Step 1 — the sweep cannot detect a regression in anything that ships

**The problem, measured.** `tools/slice-equivalence.mjs` imports `lib/diagnostics` and `lib/engine` only. The repair that actually fixes the preview — `supplyablePosition`, `positionIsTrustworthy`, `deckSectionFor`, `DECK_DERIVED_FACTS` — lives in `docs/src/lib/single-slide-render.ts` and is never executed. Every slice is rendered with **no supplied position**, so the sweep faithfully reproduces the pre-#1272 behavior on all 1201 slides.

Change `positionIsTrustworthy` to `return false` — every Studio slide back to "1 of 1", the originally reported bug fully restored — and `npm run equiv:check` **moves 0.0 points and passes.**

And most of the rate is neutralizer:

| regime | rate |
|---|---|
| blessed | **91.9%** (1104/1201) |
| minus the `pagination` neutralizer | **11.0%** (132/1201) |
| minus the `rail` neutralizer | **67.5%** (811/1201) |

~81 of the 91.9 points are differences agreed to be ignored because their repairs already ship.

**The work.** Port `positionIsTrustworthy` and `deckSectionFor` into `lib/diagnostics/` — both are pure string functions — have the corpus walk supply `page`, and drop `pagination` and `rail` from `PROTOTYPE_NEUTRALIZERS`. Re-bless. The rate stops being mostly-neutralizer and starts *falling* when a shipped repair breaks.

This also pulls duplicated logic toward one owner (HARD RULE #1 pressure that already exists in that file).

**Watch for:** the neutralizer split is deliberate and load-bearing — `SHIPPED_NEUTRALIZERS` (the overlay) and `PROTOTYPE_NEUTRALIZERS` (the sweep) hide *different* things on purpose, and `test/unit/diagnostics/slice-equivalence-core.test.js` fails if they drift toward each other. Step 1 changes the sweep's set; keep the asymmetry meaningful or retire the pair deliberately.
