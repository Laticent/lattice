# theming: the chart categorical cycle needs an ON-FILL ink — text on a tint has no curated value

## Summary

Give the chart categorical cycle a second curated ink, solved against the row/cell **tint** (`--chart-cat-N-fill`) rather than the slide surface, so the two remaining render-time `color-mix(… 65%, var(--text-heading))` calls can go.

## Why this exists

Split out of #1536 (PR #1573), which took the ink half of Track 4 and hit a finding that changed its scope. #1536 read the three render-time mixes as one workaround copied three times. They are not — they paint on **two different surfaces**:

| call site | surface | status after #1536 |
|---|---|---|
| `matrix-grid` row label (`td:first-child`) | slide (`--bg` / `--bg-alt`) | **mix deleted**, curated ink taken raw |
| `quadrant` `.quadrant-label` | the region **tint** (`--chart-cat-N-fill`) | mix kept |
| `matrix-grid` `.cell.cell-filled` | the row **tint** | mix kept |

The curated `--chart-catN-ink` is solved against `--bg` and `--bg-alt`. A tint is a much closer surface, so the solve does not transfer. Measured across 15 palettes × 2 modes × 8 slots, taking the curated ink raw on the tint puts **77 of 240** combinations below AA, worst **3.33:1** (`concrete` light, slot 5) — materially worse than the 12-of-216 the quadrant comment already recorded for the graphical ink. So the two tint call sites keep their mix, and #1536 closes only partly.

No gate would have caught it: `check-viz-render` gates black SVG paint, and #1536's own gate is deliberately scoped to the slide surfaces.

## What this needs

- A per-slot ink solved against that slot's own tint — `--chart-catN-ink-on-fill`, or whatever name fits the contract. Note the surface **varies per slot**, unlike the existing tier where all slots share `--bg`/`--bg-alt`, so `solveInkArm`'s per-arm shape does not fit directly; `solveInk(seed, tint, tint)` per slot is the obvious call, and the anti-collapse pass is meaningless here (each slot sits on its own background) — worth confirming rather than assuming.
- The tint itself is derived: `light-dark(color-mix(in oklab, hue 24%, var(--bg)), color-mix(in oklab, hue 40%, black))`. The generator must resolve it the same way the CSS does, or the solve is against a surface that does not exist.
- Delete the two remaining mixes at `quadrant.styles.css` and `matrix-grid.styles.css`.
- Gate it the way #1536 gates the slide-surface tier: committed block equals a fresh solve, **plus** a direct AA assertion — against the tint this time. Scope it explicitly in the source, since mis-scoping is exactly what this card exists to correct.
- Re-measure fidelity: #1536's mix costs up to 18.9° of hue and a third of the chroma, and that cost is still being paid at these two call sites.

## Open question

Whether `.quadrant-dot-label` should join. It currently uses `--quadrant-label-ink` (`light-dark(black, white)`), deliberately palette-blind because those item names sit on tinted cells — #1536 decided explicitly to leave it. An on-fill ink is precisely the thing that would let it carry category color safely, so the decision is worth revisiting **once the tier exists**, not before.
