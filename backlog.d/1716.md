# fix(studio): tell the author when an export squares a `corners: rounded` deck

## Summary

`corners: rounded` is an explicit one-line directive. When the chosen export format cannot carry it (`lib/core/corner-export-capability.mjs` — `jpeg`, `pdf`, `pptx`), the engine squares the slide. The **CLI says so**:

```
Corners: squared on 6 slides — a .pdf cannot carry a transparent corner.
```

The **Studio says nothing at all.** An author writes the directive, exports the primary artifact — a PDF — receives none of what they asked for, and gets no signal. Raised by the Munger inversion lens during #1711's review as *"the most predictable future bug report in the change."*

## What I tried, and why it isn't in #1711

I implemented it there and **reverted it**, because it didn't work and I'd rather not ship a no-op that looks like a feature.

The obvious channel is `onStatus`, which is already threaded through `exportPdf`, `exportPptx`, `renderPdfBlob`, and `rasterizeDeckImages`. I added a `noteSquaredCorners(sections, cornerTarget, onStatus)` helper and wired it into the PDF and PPTX lanes. Verified on the real Studio that the helper runs and calls `onStatus` with the right message:

```
[nsc] {"target":"pdf","hasOnStatus":true,"n":2,"cls":["title dark corners-rounded","dark corners-rounded content form"]}
```

But the message **never reaches the human**. `onStatus` is a transient progress line, and the very next statement is the render loop's `onStatus('Rendering slide 1 of 2…')` — microseconds later. Polling the dialog every 60ms during a real export never caught it; React does not even paint the intermediate state. Emitted, never rendered.

*(The attempt also surfaced a genuine bug on the way: `sectionsOf` returns a **NodeList**, not an Array, so an innocent `.filter` threw and took the entire PDF export down. Worth knowing for whoever picks this up.)*

## The right channel

`ShareSheet` (`docs/src/components/studio/ShareSheet.tsx`) already has a persistent `notify` toast, and its own comment at ~line 94-103 says exactly why it is the right one: *"The reason goes IN the toast, which persists."* That is where an export-time override belongs — alongside "PDF ready.", not racing it.

The open design question is how the export reports back. Either the export functions return whether corners were squared (changes their return shape), or `ShareSheet` derives it from `source` + the chosen format — but note #1676 deliberately established that Studio surfaces **measure** the rendered corner rather than re-deriving it from front matter, and a per-slide `_class: corners-rounded` opt-in makes a source regex wrong. Measuring is the honest route.

## Swimlane

`engineering/decisions/2026-08-17-corner-export-capability.md` — the capability rule. This card is its notification gap.

## Done when

Exporting a deck with at least one rounded slide to PDF, PPTX, or JPEG from the Studio raises a persistent toast naming what happened and what to do instead (e.g. *"Corners squared — a .pdf can't carry a transparent corner. Export PNG or WebP to keep them."*). It does **not** fire for a square deck, nor for a rounded deck going to PNG/WebP — the notice appears only when an explicit directive was actually overridden. Verified on the real Share sheet, not in jsdom (HARD RULE #23).
