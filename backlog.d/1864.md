# theming: the categorical fill ramp is not separable — ΔE 0.013 to NORMAL vision, against a 0.15 floor

## Summary

The 12-slot categorical fill ramp does not function as a discrimination channel — **not under colour-vision deficiency, and not under normal vision either.** Adjacent `--cat-N-fill` pairs measure ΔE **0.013** on `indaco` (range **0.007–0.024** across five brand themes) against the project's own **0.15** separability floor.

`node tools/cvd-audit.js --type deuteranopia` reports **633 collapsed token pairs across 32 themes** — 26 on `indaco` alone.

This is the root finding behind the texture investigation (#1861, #1862, #1863) and it is upstream of all of them.

## Why this is the load-bearing issue

Texture is documented as **redundant** encoding — a second channel reinforcing a first. But if the fills carry ΔE 0.013, texture is not reinforcing anything; it is being asked to carry **100% of the discrimination load**.

That inversion explains every tension measured in the texture work and none of them are fixable while it holds:

- The texture ink must be loud enough to be the *only* signal, which is why it fights slide labels (2.59:1 on `indaco` dark, 2.48:1 on the a11y palettes) instead of whispering under them.
- Tile ink coverage spans 9.3%–50.0% (5.4x), which only matters visually because the tile is doing all the work.
- Small marks get nothing: a 1% pie wedge is 0.74 tile periods and cannot resolve a pattern at all — so exactly where hue already failed, texture also fails.

**Fix the ramp and texture goes back to being what the docs say it is:** a quiet second channel that can afford to whisper, on marks large enough to carry it, with an ink that never competes with a label.

## Scope of the decision

This changes the rendered appearance of **every theme**, so it is a design decision, not a mechanical repair. The axes:

1. **How much separation is enough?** The 0.15 floor is already enforced elsewhere (`checkHljsSeparation` uses it for syntax palettes). Adopting it for the categorical ramp is the obvious position, but 12 slots at ΔE ≥ 0.15 inside one brand's hue family is a real constraint on how "of a piece" a palette can look.
2. **Where in the derivation does it land?** `lib/theme/derive.js` already binary-searches lightness to repair contrast (`ensureContrast` / `solveInk`). A separability solve is the same shape of problem against a different objective, and would apply to every derived theme automatically.
3. **What happens to the 32 committed themes?** A ramp change moves committed PDFs and every gallery — HARD RULE #8 isolation and the QUALITY BAR export sign-off both apply.
4. **Is 12 slots even reachable?** If a brand palette genuinely cannot separate 12 categories, the honest answer may be that the categorical cycle is shorter than 12 for chromatic themes, with texture as the documented remedy past that point — which would be a *principled* reason for texture rather than the current implicit one.
