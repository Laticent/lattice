# Let a component decline the coda: anchor, math and the canvas family host a band that does not belong to their composition

## Summary

Some layouts should be able to say they do not accept a trailing editorial band at all, instead of hosting one that fights their composition — and which components those are has to be decided by looking at each one, not inferred.

## Swimlane

`engineering/decisions/2026-08-24-universal-coda-cell.md` — the universal coda Cell. That work made the coda a real Cell docked by the section's structure, and deliberately made support **opt-out by default** so a new component gets it free. This card is the other half: the opt-out is currently all-or-nothing per trailing ELEMENT (`coda.claims`), and there is no way for a layout to say "I render neither beat, because my composition has no place for one."

## Done when

Every one of the 61 layouts either (a) renders a coda that reads as designed on its own gallery slide, verified visually, or (b) declares that it does not accept one — and the deck lint tells an author who writes a beat on such a slide, rather than the engine placing it somewhere unfortunate. The declaration is gated the way `coda.claims` is, so a layout cannot silently drift back.
