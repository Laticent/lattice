# spike: nothing keeps the gotchas taxonomy from rotting again — titles are the only routing signal and the generator only checks that a row exists

## Summary

Re-filing 145 gotcha entries into 14 honest topics cost a whole PR. Nothing stops the next 145 from drifting back, and the note that did the work says so.

## Swimlane

`engineering/decisions/2026-08-17-gotchas-topic-refile.md` § "Titles are still the only routing signal".

## The gap

`tools/build-gotchas-index.js` verifies that every `## ` entry in `engineering/gotchas/*.md` has a row in the generated index. It cannot verify that the row sits under a heading that describes it — and the refile note is direct that this is a judgment a machine cannot make from the text, and that "pretending otherwise would be a gate that cannot fail."

That is a fair reason not to have written a gate then. It is not a reason for the taxonomy to have no rot signal at all, because the failure is silent and compounding: `gotchas/ci.md` was six-sevenths Playground hydration entries before anyone noticed, and `marp.md` was 26/34 not-Marp. Both drifted one honest entry at a time.

## What is worth trying, cheapest first

- **A report, not a gate.** A nightly or on-demand `gotchas:audit` that prints per-topic vocabulary drift — an entry whose title and body share no distinctive term with its file's other entries, or that scores closer to a different topic. Report-only, no red build. This is the shape the note's objection allows: it surfaces a judgment call rather than asserting one.
- **The authoring hint.** The refile rule is a single sentence — *an entry belongs to the surface or subsystem whose behavior surprised you, not to what the fix touched* — and it lives only in a decision record. It belongs at the top of `engineering/gotchas/README` or each topic file's preamble, where someone adding an entry will read it.
- **A pre-registered next split.** `studio-playground.md`'s seam is already named in the note (preview frame vs. app shell); see the separate issue for it.

Recommend the second before the first: it is a five-line change and it is the only one that acts before the entry lands in the wrong file.

## Done when

Either (a) an on-demand audit exists, is registered in `engineering/capabilities.md`, and its output over the current 14 topics is recorded in the PR — including whichever entries it flags that a human then judges correctly filed; or (b) a decision record states that no signal is worth its false-positive rate here, with the audit's measured output as the evidence for that.
