# engine(masthead): `headline:` is a silent no-op on split-panel / split-compare, and a masthead with a bay centers beside the bay

## Summary

Three scope edges were documented and deliberately not fixed when masthead alignment landed, under HARD RULE #18's off-path rule. None was ticketed.

- **`split-panel` / `split-compare` have no `masthead-lede`**, so `headline:` sets the token but no paint site reads it — a **silent no-op** on their panel headings. An author writes a key, nothing happens, and nothing says so.
- **A masthead WITH a bay** (a `meta:` / `logo:` / `status:` tile) centers or right-aligns in the space *beside* the bay, not the full frame, so the title does not run under the meta. With a bay, `head-center` / `head-right` can therefore sit visibly inset from the stage-level framing. `head-left` is always exact; a bay-less masthead is exact.
- **Excluded layouts** (`quote` / `citation-card` / `math` / `redline` / `inventory`) follow the seam for their masthead heading, but their body (pull-quote, equation, …) keeps its own alignment — so `head-center` / `head-right` can reintroduce a heading-vs-body split for these few layouts.

The first is the one that reads as a bug to an author; the other two are honest constraints that should either be documented where an author meets them or made exact.
