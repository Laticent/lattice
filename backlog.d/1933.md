# studio(a11y): the toolbar dial is three independent `aria-pressed` toggles, and its segments miss the touch-target floor at 820px

## Summary

Three accessibility items were explicitly listed as "deliberately NOT done" when the toolbar was placed, each needing its own change. None was ticketed.

- **The dial is an exclusive 3-way choice exposed as three independent `aria-pressed` toggles.** A screen-reader user hears "not pressed" three times, with nothing conveying exclusivity or "2 of 3". APG's Radio Group is the right pattern and would collapse three tab stops to one — but it changes Space/Enter to arrow selection, so it is a keyboard-behavior change that needs its own spec update.
- **The real toolbar pattern** (roving `tabindex` + arrow keys, taking eight tab stops to one) is the better end state. A half-built toolbar is what the original change removed, so this should be done properly or not at all.
- **Touch target sizes.** Dial segments are 27px tall and abut with a **0px gap at 820** — a touch tier — and six 32×32 ghost buttons run at 38px centre-to-centre.

## Swimlane

`engineering/decisions/2026-08-16-studio-toolbar-placement.md` — see § "Deliberately NOT done (and why)".

## Done when

1. The dial announces as an exclusive choice — a screen reader conveys "2 of 3", not three unrelated toggles.
2. Keyboard traversal of the toolbar is one tab stop with arrow-key movement inside it, per APG.
3. Touch targets at 820 and 390 meet the floor with a real gap between adjacent segments.
4. Evidence per HARD RULE #23 from the real Studio — a screen-reader pass and `tools/screenshot.js` at 1440 / 820 / 390. A jsdom role assertion is not evidence that a screen reader says the right thing.
