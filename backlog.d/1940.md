# forms(rule): the spectrum bar is still a `border-top` suppressed ad hoc in seven places, and the `rule` Tile manifest describes a Tile that does not exist

## Summary

Split-frame edge ownership was settled for the split layouts and left the general case alone:

- The spectrum bar remains a **`border-top` on the `section`** for every other layout, **suppressed ad hoc in seven places**.
- The **`rule` Tile manifest still describes a Tile that does not exist.**

Promoting it to a real Form noun — an edge Cell the root Frame owns, with `suppresses` able to name it — is the follow-up that change did not attempt.

Two things make this worth a card rather than a comment: seven ad-hoc suppressions is a pattern that grows (the eighth layout will add a ninth), and a manifest describing a non-existent Tile is a false entry in a published catalog.

`engineering/decisions/2026-08-18-split-frame-edge-ownership.md` — see § "Still open".

1. The spectrum bar is an edge Cell owned by the root Frame, not a `border-top` on the `section`.
2. The seven ad-hoc suppressions collapse into `suppresses` naming the edge Cell.

4. No layout's rendered output moves — this is a structural change, so the gallery goldens are the guard.
