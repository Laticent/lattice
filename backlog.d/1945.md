# docs(components): 54 of 59 components never got the three agent-contract fields — the pilot's second slice was never taken

## Summary

The per-component agent contract landed as a **pilot on 5 components**. The remaining **54 of 59 enriched components** got the mechanical section reorder from the shared template but **not the three new content fields** — described at the time as "the next slice, pending sign-off on this pilot's shape".

Sign-off never happened, and the slice was never taken. The result is a catalog where the agent-contract fields exist on a handful of components and are absent from the rest, which is worse than either end state: an agent reading the catalog cannot tell "this component has no anti-patterns" from "this component was not enriched yet".

A second, genuinely open design question was raised by the inversion lens in the same PR and also left: **should the new fields feed the aggregated `dist/docs/components.json` picker catalog?**
