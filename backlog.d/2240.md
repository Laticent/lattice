## Read this before judging a result

**Most travel is a mark riding its own block, and the clearance column is what tells them apart.** A `ul::before` ring drawn on a list moves down when the heading above it grows — that is flow, not drift, and the tell is that the clearance to the content does not change. Judge a mark against what it is positioned against: a `section::before` is section-relative and owes the canvas a fixed position; a `ul::before` owes its list. Several of the ten movers above are very likely this case, and recording them as **holds** with that reasoning is a correct outcome, not a wasted sweep.
