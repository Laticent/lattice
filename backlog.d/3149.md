# narration: precompress clips in idle time, so a narrated export assembles almost at once

## Swimlane

`engineering/decisions/2026-08-04-shared-deck-narration-audio.md` §9, with `engineering/decisions/2026-11-11-voice-synthesis-spine.md` §9.2 and §9.7.

## Done when

- Idle precompression writes compressed copies to their own keyspace, keyed by clip and bitrate, and never replaces what playback reads. A test asserts this.
- Changing the audio quality makes the old copies miss, and they recompute lazily.
- Eviction drops a compressed copy before any original.
- A 300-sentence export over a precompressed store finishes with no inline encode, measured.
