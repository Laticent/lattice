# Audit and optimize the fit-everywhere work (#2988) after it lands, without weakening fit

## Summary

After #2988 ("fit everywhere") lands, the ⚡ optimization desk and the 〰️ jank desk audit and optimize everything it added: engine code, CSS, the editor's measured split, bundle weight, tests and docs. Every gain is measured, and **nothing may weaken the fit work**: every deck fits, splits and looks exactly as it did on the day #2988 merged, or better.

## Swimlane

`engineering/decisions/2026-10-28-fit-everywhere-model.md` (the fit model #2988 implements). Desks: `/desk-optimize` and `/desk-jank`.

#2988 is 440 files, +20,361 / −3,750 lines over 58 commits (counted against its merge base, 2026-11-25):

Most of it is necessary: it changes how every layout fits. But a walk through three components found what the desks should look for:

- **Copied CSS blocks.** `list-tabular.styles.css` moved its narrow form from the deck's `data-family` stamp to container queries (a real improvement: it answers the width actually available), but wrote the same 8 rules twice, once at `23em` and again at `17em` for the `def` and `fit-name` variants, differing by one number.
- **Specificity escalation.** One selector repeats the class five times (`section.list-tabular.list-tabular.list-tabular.list-tabular.list-tabular`) to outrank the blocks above it; its own comment says so. That is a cascade fight, not a design.
- **A wide mechanical rename.** About 40 components changed only to rename the `cards:` register to `align:` (`--cards-align` → `--align-v` and so on). Behavior did not change. Check that nothing still reads the old names and that no compatibility shim outlived its need.
- **Engine weight.** The feature's logic sits in a few large files: `lib/core/fit-measure.js` (1,246 lines changed), `docs/src/lib/measured-split.ts` (534), `lib/core/carousel.js` (458), `lib/core/split-envelope.js` (330), `lib/core/passage-sentences.js` (212), `docs/src/lib/fluido/pack.ts`, `lib/core/auto-split.js`, `lib/core/resolve-cards.js` (renamed `resolve-align`), `lib/core/logo-wall-pack.js`.
- **Bundle weight.** Two route-budget raises came from this line of work: `lintCoreGz` to 174,022 B (#3512) and the Studio's `eagerJsGz` to 644,780 B (#3516), plus the `route-budget.d/2988-measured-split.md` declaration (studio +1,460 B, playground +680 B, home +500 B).
- **Timeouts added as safety nets.** `docs/src/lib/within-ms.ts` (`LAZY_LOAD_MS` 8 s) and `MEASURE_DEADLINE_MS` (12 s) in `measured-split.ts`. Confirm each still guards a real failure, or remove it once its cause is fixed.
- **Test bulk.** `test/integration/parity` (+2,806 lines) and `test/unit/core` (+2,000) carry most of the tests, one file per layout or case. Look for shared fixtures and helpers that cut lines without losing a single case.

### The fit floor: what may not get worse

Measured on the day #2988 merges, recorded as the baseline, and held by every optimization PR:

1. **Page counts.** Every demo deck and gallery deck renders the same number of pages at every venue (`hall`, `story`, `square`, phone), PDF and the editor alike.
2. **Pixels.** The golden renders hold (`tools/pixel-check.js`); any changed page is shown to the owner as before and after.
3. **Jank.** `npm run check:jank` on every touched layout: no new drift, collision or crowding, and no worse than baseline at any venue.
4. **Tests.** Every test that exists at merge still passes. A test may be merged, shared or rewritten, never dropped. Each changed test shows its failing arm (HARD RULE #34).
5. **Fit verdicts.** `fit-readable` and the parity tier give the same verdict on every case.

A change that cannot show all five does not ship.

## Done when

Committed as the record every PR compares against.
2. **Audit**, written up in one note under `engineering/decisions/`: each finding with its file and line, its measured cost, and keep / merge / delete. Covering at least: duplicated CSS blocks, specificity escalation, leftover `cards`-era names or shims, engine hot paths (what the bench says is slow), dead or duplicated helpers across the fit files, the two timeouts, test duplication, and docs that say the same thing twice.
3. **Optimizations, one PR per finding or tight group**, each with before and after numbers from the baseline (HARD RULE #19), the five fit-floor checks, and a changeset where behavior a person sees could change (#10). The desks start with the cheapest wins that carry the least risk (the copied CSS blocks, the specificity escalation, leftover names) and work up to the engine.
4. **The route budget comes back down** where the optimizations free bytes: each freed kilobyte is taken out of the ceiling in the same PR, so the room cannot be spent again silently.
