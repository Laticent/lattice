# explore: alignment as a universal modifier (vertical top/center for tables, lists, etc.)

## Summary

Spun out of the frames-as-flex-cell-trees work. In the flex cell-tree, the body
cell is a bounded, deterministic box. Most content fills it; but some content
should **not stretch to fill it vertically** — a **table** especially. The decision
there: a table keeps its nature (it fills the available **width**, columns line up),
but we do **not** stretch it **vertically** — it should sit **top-aligned or
centered** in the leftover vertical space, not balloon to fill the stage.

Rather than bake "tables sit at the top" into the table component alone, generalize:
make **alignment a universal modifier** — `align-top` / `align-center` (and possibly
`align-bottom`, and maybe a horizontal axis) — applicable to any content that
shouldn't vertically stretch: **tables, lists, and similar fixed-shape blocks**.

This mirrors the existing Form `fill-center` / `fill-anchor` / `fill-optical`
direction on the *stage* (`lib/forms/cell/stage/stage.css`) but as a **content-level,
universal** modifier rather than a per-component or stage-only one.

## Done when

- A universal alignment modifier (`align-top` / `align-center` / …) exists and can be

- It composes with the bounded body cell (it positions content *within* the cell; it
  does not defeat the cell's clip).
- Reconciled with the existing stage-level `fill-*` values (one vocabulary, not two).
