# chart(state-chart): consolidate the third private list-walker variant onto the shared kernel

## Summary

A performance audit (red team + Munger inversion + independent checker, 2026-07-10) found that `journey.transform.js`'s `findOuterUL`/`splitTopLevelLI` were a verified byte-for-byte duplicate of `lib/components/chart/_chart-family/transform-utils.js`'s versions (already shared by radar/quadrant) and deduplicated them — journey now imports from `transform-utils.js` instead of carrying a private copy.

`state-chart.transform.js` carries a **third, genuinely different** variant (`findOuterList`/`splitTopLevelLis`, `lib/components/chart/state-chart/state-chart.transform.js:85-145`) — it's tag-generic (takes a `tag` parameter for both `<ul>` and `<ol>`) and uses `charCodeAt`/regex-based boundary checks instead of journey/transform-utils' fixed char comparisons. Because it's a real algorithmic variant (not a byte-identical copy), the audit's own checker recommended leaving it alone or tracking its three-way consolidation as a **separate, larger design decision** rather than folding it into the journey fix.

## Why this needs its own design pass

Unlike the journey fix (a pure, behavior-preserving dedup — proven byte-identical via a before/after render diff), unifying state-chart's variant would mean either:
- Generalizing `transform-utils.js`'s `findOuterUL`/`splitTopLevelLI` to also handle the tag-generic `<ul>`/`<ol>` case state-chart needs (risks regressing radar/quadrant/journey, which don't need that generality), or
- Accepting a 3-way split permanently (current state) with a comment cross-referencing why.

Either way it's a real design decision, not a mechanical dedup.
