- `state-chart` now holds its type floor through its own self-scale. The chart letterbox-scales itself to fit the stage, and that transform multiplied the CSS floor away with everything else — so a machine scaled to 0.72 rendered its edge labels at 7.9px against a declared floor of 11px. The declared floor is now raised by 1/k where the scale factor is known, so the effective size lands on the floor instead of under it: measured on the shipped gallery, 7.91px → 11.00px and 8.30px → 11.00px on the two hard-scaled slides. Charts that are scaled UP are untouched — the compensation only ever raises the floor, never caps type that is already comfortable.
