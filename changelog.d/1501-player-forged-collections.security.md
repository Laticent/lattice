- **A shared deck can no longer add fake slides to the exported player.** A slide that carried its own `<div class="lp-frame">` or a nested `<section data-lattice-slide>` made the `--player` export count extra slides, show a fragment of one slide when Next was clicked, and play the next slide's narration over it. The player now finds its slides only inside its own stage, and the exporter frames only top-level slides. Every player export's script, and so its CSP hash, changes; markup and styles do not.
- The browser check for forged player chrome (`npm run verify:narrated-player`) now runs every night, and its two checks that could not fail now assert what the player actually bound.
