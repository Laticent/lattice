- **Breaking (visual): a palette's own tokens now win the cascade in an exported deck.** `lattice-emulator.js` built the deck stylesheet with the palette FIRST and the engine bundle second, so the engine's `:root` defaults landed later at equal specificity and overrode the theme — the opposite of what every theme's own `@import 'lattice';` declares, and the opposite of what the Studio, the docs Playground and the engine's own Mermaid token reader have always done. **675 declarations across 28 tokens, on all 32 shipped palettes**, resolved to the engine's value and painted nothing their author wrote, so a deck looked one way in the Playground and another in the PDF it exported. The two sheets are engine-first, palette-last on every path now.
- **What moves:** the twelve `--hljs-*` syntax colors plus `--code-text` (code panels), the eight `--diagram-*` state tokens (gantt, kanban, Mermaid), `--seq-500` (sequential ramps and word-cloud spectrums), and a short tail of `--on-accent` / `--on-dark-*` / `--seq-pole-*`. The status trio and `--chart-state-*` are **not** in that list: they reach the page through the `:root:root` bump landed separately, whichever order the sheets are concatenated in.
- **Swept across every palette in both modes before landing:** 64 of 64 theme-modes change at least one slide, and no palette is unaffected. The shape is narrow — 36 of the 38 distinct theme-mode states change exactly one slide, the `code` panel, because every palette curates twelve syntax colors and none of them had ever reached an export. `cuoio` changes more (6 of 10 sample slides in dark mode), being the palette with the most declarations of its own.
- **Nine tokens stop resolving two different ways in one render.** `--diagram-active`, `--diagram-critical`, `--diagram-done`, `--diagram-note`, `--diagram-today`, their `-mark` companions and `--fail` are read by both the CSS and the Mermaid token map. The baked SVG took the palette's value while the CSS framing it took the engine's, so a gantt bar and the rule around it came from one token name and two different colors.
- **Fixed: `journey` actor dots were unreadable on `carbone`** — 1.91:1, a shipping AA failure. The dot paints `--on-accent` over a categorical mark, so the engine's white won the cascade and landed on carbone's bright cyan; carbone's own dark ink now paints, at 4.89:1. On `atelier` the same surface moved the other way (4.57 → 3.91), so its light `--on-accent` is lifted `#F0EDE6` → `#FFFEFB` — imperceptible on accent surfaces, where it goes 15.42:1 → 17.28:1.
- `tools/palette-sweep.js` finds the palette region in an exported deck by an explicit `/* lattice:palette-end */` sentinel rather than by the next stylesheet banner, which no longer follows it.
