- Charts draw larger on every slide. `.chart-body` no longer carries a 32px block padding, so a chart gets the whole stage instead of the stage minus 64px — a quadrant gains 17.8–20.0% in both dimensions, and every SVG-bodied chart gains with it.
- Fixed two silent clips the padding was causing. A chart body pinned to natural height (`progress`, `kanban`, `timeline-list`, `roadmap`) counted that padding as part of its own height, so it pushed itself out of the stage and lost the overflow to `overflow: clip`. `check-chart-fit` goes from three clipped slides to one, and the survivor shrinks from 55.5px to 10.9px.
- Paint that legitimately falls outside a chart's box keeps its room, now as an `overflow-clip-margin` on the stage rather than as padding — so it costs no layout height. This is what `kanban`'s card elevation shadow needs; it sits outside the card's border box and was being cut at the stage edge.
- Fixed `state-chart`'s start and terminal markers being cut on a small canvas. The node column reserved their room with a container-relative padding while the markers are drawn at fixed pixel offsets, so on a tight box (the `curved` variant, 39.6px against a 46px requirement) the markers painted outside their own box. Both paddings now take a floor that matches the marker geometry.
- **Breaking (theme authors):** `--chart-inset-top` and `--chart-inset-bottom` are retired. A theme or deck that set them to tune a chart's vertical inset no longer has an effect; the stage owns the outer inset on both axes now. The painted `.canvas` panel's block berth moved to the new `--chart-panel-y`, which is the token to set instead — it takes one length for both seams or two for an asymmetric top and bottom.
