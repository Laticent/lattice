- Accessibility palettes, Studio and docs Playground: a slide marked `_class: dark` in an `a11y-*` deck, and the Confidential/WIP-style status marker on every title, closing and dark-divider slide, rendered the palette's light-tuned status colors on a dark field — 1.25:1 at worst, invisible. Each of `a11y-achromatopsia`, `a11y-deuteranopia`, `a11y-protanopia` and `a11y-tritanopia` now curates a dark-canvas `--pass` / `--warn` / `--fail`, clearing AA on the surface `redline` actually paints them on and holding the color-vision-deficiency separation of its light trio. - New gate: a palette that overrides one of `base.tokens.css`'s `light-dark()` defaults with a value that resolves the same in both schemes now fails `npm test`, rather than shipping a dark-mode value nobody chose.
