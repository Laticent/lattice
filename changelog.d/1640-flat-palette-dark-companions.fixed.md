- Accessibility palettes: a slide marked `_class: dark` in an `a11y-*` deck no longer falls back to the generic status trio, which collapses under exactly the color-vision deficiency the palette exists to survive (ΔE 0.019–0.092 under simulation, and two of the three at identical luminance for an achromat). Each of `a11y-achromatopsia`, `a11y-deuteranopia`, `a11y-protanopia` and `a11y-tritanopia` now curates its own dark-canvas `--pass` / `--warn` / `--fail`, holding AA on the surface `redline` actually paints them on and keeping the deficiency separation of its light trio.
- Eleven palettes (`ardesia`, `atelier`, `brina`, `burgundy`, `carta`, `crepuscolo`, `cuoio`, `indaco`, `laguna`, `magnolia`, `mustard`) now declare the sequential-ramp anchor `--seq-500` as a `light-dark()` pair instead of a single light-tuned value, so the ten-stop ramp — and the `word-cloud spectrum` word fills that read from it — stay legible on a dark canvas whichever sheet wins the cascade.
- New gate: a palette that overrides one of the engine's `light-dark()` defaults with a flat value now fails `npm test`, rather than shipping a dark-mode value nobody chose.
