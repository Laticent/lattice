- Slide layering is now a model instead of a habit. A slide is six named planes (`lib/base/base.tokens.css`): `--z-canvas` and `--z-atmosphere` sink below the words, content rests at the natural flow, and `--z-chrome`, `--z-mark` and `--z-alarm` rise above it. Every layering decision at slide scale names one. This executes the plane model the Form manifests have always declared: every Cell and Tile carries a `z`, and until now nothing read it.
- Every `section` is a stacking context unconditionally. It used to be gated on `.form`, so a sovereign frame such as `title`, `math` or `divider` formed none at all (`image` and `scene` declared one for themselves) and its `z-index` values escaped to the page root, where they were ordered against *other slides*.
- **Fixed:** the section-number watermark painted *under* a finish backdrop rather than above it — its manifest declares the atmosphere plane while its CSS said `z-index: -1`. On patterned finishes the texture ruled straight across the ghost numeral.
- Running chrome (header, footer, progress rail) shared one plane with body content under a `finish:`, so which covered which was decided by DOM order. Chrome now sits above content by definition, on `--z-chrome`, replacing the literal `z-index: 3` and its `section.finish > :is(header, footer)` companion — that companion existed only to undo the blanket rule this change removes.
- Status stamps move from `z-index: 100` to `--z-mark` (50) and the authoring tabs from `50` to `--z-alarm` (40), preserving the order the engine has always shipped: a classification marking outranks an engine annotation on a delivered artifact.
- The deck logo sits on `--z-chrome` with the rest of the frame chrome. (It was briefly pulled off the scale to dodge an apparent PDF rendering defect; that defect turned out to be a bug in `pdftoppm`'s rasterizer rather than anything in the exported file, so the exemption is gone and the model has none.)
- `image`'s `statement` composition no longer prints a page number. The glyph full-bleeds over the photo there and measures 1.72:1 where the image lightens; every other `image` composition and all of `scene` keep theirs.
- Two new gates. `checkZPlanes` (`npm run build:check`) rejects any `z-index` outside the local `0–9` band that does not name a plane token, plus plane tokens nothing reads and tokens nothing defines. `test/integration/invariants/slide-planes.test.js` renders a corpus and asserts every real direct child of every section is on a named plane or in the content flow, and that the canvas and atmosphere planes are the right way round. The Form manifest ↔ CSS check (§4.3) is now an equality test — the manifest plane and the CSS plane must be the same plane — where it was only a monotonicity test that the watermark defect passed.
- **Removed:** `base.finish.css`'s blanket `section.finish > *:not(.backdrop)` rules, their `:where(…)` frame-chrome exclusion list, and the `checkFinishChromeExclusions` gate that kept the list honest. With planes there is nothing to exclude.
