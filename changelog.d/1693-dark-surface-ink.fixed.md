- **Fixed:** text on a title, closing, or divider bookend could render in light-canvas ink on the dark field — bold body text measured 1.61:1 against a 3:1 floor, effectively invisible. Those bookends painted `--surface-inverse` without declaring `color-scheme: dark`, so every `light-dark()` token on the slide resolved to its light side and anything the base layer had not named explicitly inherited the wrong ink. The scheme is now declared with the surface, and **re-asserted after the canvas registers**: `section.title` and `section.color-light` are the same specificity and the registers are emitted later, so a deck carrying `color-mode: light` (or a slide written `_class: title light`) otherwise kept the original bug at 1.61:1, with links at 1.20:1.
- The `split-panel` and `split-compare` dark rails are **not** fixed by that and deliberately keep explicit `--on-dark-*` inks: they are descendants, and `light-dark()` in a custom property resolves where the property is *declared* — on `section` — so a rail flipping its own `color-scheme` inherits ink that has already resolved to the light value. Their remaining un-named inks are pre-existing and logged, not addressed here.
- **Fixed:** on the CLI/export path, every theme's inline-code ink was being discarded. `--code-inline-fg` had an engine default of `var(--accent)`, and `lattice-emulator.js` composes a render as palette-then-bundle, so the engine's copy landed last and won at equal specificity. Eight themes deepen that ink so the chip clears AA on a *card* rather than only on the canvas; all eight were dropped, and the chip shipped `--accent` at 4.36:1 against a 4.5 floor. (On the packing path — browser runtime, Playground, Studio — `composeCss` inlines the bundle at the theme's `@import`, so the theme already won there.)
- `--code-inline-fg` is now a derived, required token (`lib/theme/derive.js`, `ink` group). It was ink falling back onto `--accent`, an area token held to the 3:1 graphical floor rather than a text contract — the same shape as the `--cat-N-ink` defect. It is repaired against **the chip's own wash** (`0.1·ink + 0.9·surface`, the composite the glyphs actually sit on) rather than the bare card; repairing against the card left five of fourteen palettes deriving a sub-AA chip, indaco among them at 4.38:1.
- **Fixed:** the `split-compare` rail was missing from the inline-code on-dark rebind list. Latent while the chip rode `--accent`, it became a measurable regression the moment themes' deepened (darker, card-tuned) ink started reaching the page — mustard 3.65:1 → 2.49:1, with six other themes alongside it.
- Measured on the rendered DOM with `tools/check-slide-contrast.js`: `examples/accent-finishes.md` goes from 14 sub-AA text runs to **0** in `indaco`, and to 0 in `a11y-base`, `indaco-dark`, `mustard`, `onyx` and `cuoio`.
- The `bookend-ink` integration invariant now sweeps the ordinary inline vocabulary — plain body, bold, italic, links, inline code — across all three dark bookends **and all seven canvas registers**, in standards mode rather than the quirks-mode document `setContent` produces by default. Verified by mutation: removing the register re-assertion reproduces the reported 1.61:1 and 1.20:1 exactly.
