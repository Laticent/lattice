- The Theme Studio's live WCAG meter (`lib/theme/contrast.js`) now measures de-emphasis SEPARATION, not only contrast. `--text-muted` and `--text-secondary` each get a row asserting they stay perceptibly apart from `--text-body` in OKLab (floor 0.030, the same number `checkMutedTierFloors` holds committed palettes to, pinned equal by a test). Until now `deriveTheme` could emit either ink byte-identical to body from plausible essentials and the panel showed six green rows and an "AA verified" badge. Separation rows are a distinct row `kind` carrying `distance` — never a ΔE in the `ratio` field — and the Fabricate panel renders them as `ΔE 0.007 · FAIL` rather than a fabricated ratio and a WCAG tier.
- `tools/cvd-audit.js` gained an ACHROMATOPSIA arm. The audit loops all four simulated conditions instead of the three Machado matrices (`CVD_TYPES` is unchanged — achromatopsia is a monochromacy), with a per-condition collapse floor: 0.065 for the monochromacy against the dichromacies' 0.15, because only lightness survives. Reusing 0.15 reports 1229 collapses — 1.91x the worst dichromacy arm, with seven palettes where every group reads ✗. The normal-vision half of the induced test keeps 0.15 for every condition; lowering it too would add 996 pairs a sighted reader already cannot separate. The new arm finds a `--pass`/`--warn`/`--fail` collapse on 25 of 32 palettes (`concrete` at ΔE 0.000); `a11y-achromatopsia` is the only palette clean everywhere. No palette value was re-tuned.
- `node tools/cvd-audit.js <palette> --strict` now spans all four conditions, so it can exit non-zero where it exited 0. Gate a palette against the condition it is named for with `--strict --type <condition>`. Nothing in CI runs this tool.
