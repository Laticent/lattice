- **Breaking (visual):** every palette's status trio (`--pass` / `--warn` / `--fail`) has been respaced so the three signals carry three distinct WEIGHTS against the canvas, not just three hues. Under achromatopsia only lightness survives, and 162 of the 192 committed pairs sat under the 0.11 floor `a11y-achromatopsia` was built to reach — worst `concrete`/light, where `--pass` and `--warn` rendered the identical gray `#464646`. All 192 clear it now (worst 0.1173). Hue is held and chroma yields only where the sRGB gamut clips, so each palette keeps its character; on a light canvas the trio reads deeper and on a dark canvas brighter. A deck that hard-codes one of these hex values instead of reading the token will now differ from the palette around it.
- Moving the status inks away from their canvas moved their COMPOSED readings with them: the frozen `KNOWN_SUB_THRESHOLD` baseline drops from 108 entries to 70 — 38 composed pairs (status pills, redline bands, stance badges) cleared their contrast bar outright and 53 improved.
- `--state-{pass,warn,fail,info}-fill` now mixes its hue 50% into black on a dark canvas rather than 58%, so `--text-heading` keeps AA on top of a chart bar or kanban card whatever the palette's status hue. Dark-mode state fills read slightly deeper.
- `cuoio`'s `--text-label` is the saddle-gold accent again in both modes. It had drifted onto the body ink (light `#6F604F` against body `#6b5d4f` — no hue distinction at all), so kickers, table headers and the `list-tabular` meta column rendered as body-colored text instead of accent-colored.
- A theme fabricated in the Studio now gets a monotone de-emphasis ladder: `--text-body` louder than `--text-secondary`, louder than `--text-muted`, in BOTH modes. `deriveTheme` previously emitted muted and secondary byte-identical on the default `dusk` starter in light mode, and — on all four starters in dark mode — emitted `--text-muted` LOUDER than `--text-body`, making the de-emphasis tier the most prominent ink on the slide.
- `build:check` now fails a palette whose `--text-secondary` or `--text-label` sits within OKLab 0.030 of `--text-body`, alongside the `--text-muted` row it already carried. A quiet tier has to be quieter and a label has to be a label; a contrast floor measured against the CANVAS cannot see two inks converging on each other.
- `node tools/cvd-audit.js` accepts `--themes-dir <path>` to audit palettes outside `themes/`.
- **Known limitation:** the respaced trio reaches the Theme Studio and the docs Playground, but NOT a rendered PDF. On the export path `lib/base/base.tokens.css` still wins the cascade over the palette for `--pass` / `--warn` / `--fail` (#1527), so an exported deck paints the engine default — which is itself collapsed under monochromacy (0.0580 light, 0.0033 dark). Respacing that default is coupled to the #1527 cascade flip and is specified in the design note.
