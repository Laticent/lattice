- **Breaking: Present splits into a Stage and a console.** Projecting the deck used
  to mean projecting the presenter's instruments with it — the Exit button, the lens
  picker, the staging pills, the slide counter and the progress rail were all
  unconditional, so there was no state of the product in which an audience saw only
  the deck. **Stage** (in the Present bar, or `S`) now opens a chrome-free window
  carrying just the deck, auto-placed on the external screen; the Present overlay
  stays on the laptop as the presenter's console. Captions, the Guide pointer and
  the progress rail follow the deck to the Stage, because they are aimed at the room;
  the transport, the lens, the slide grid and the notes stay with the presenter.
  With no Stage open, the console hosts the captions and the rail exactly as before.
- **Breaking: the second presenter window is removed**, along with the labels
  "Presenter screen", "Presenter view" and the "PRESENTER" chip. It was a second
  presenter cockpit rather than an audience surface, which is why naming it was
  impossible. What it carried is now on the console: a **next-slide preview**, a
  **speaker-notes panel** and a **talk clock** with an arm-then-confirm reset (all
  `lg` and up). Zooming a slide privately is the console's own pinch / ⌘-wheel /
  middle-drag, which no longer needs a second window to stay private.
- **Changed: the Stage takes `F` for full screen.** A popup has no user activation
  of its own, so a browser may decline to fullscreen it on open; when that happens
  the console says which key does it rather than leaving a windowed deck unexplained.
