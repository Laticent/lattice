- **Added: `npm run mutate:stage-window`** — a committed mutation battery over the Stage
  window, in the same shape as `mutate:guide`. It injects the defect each test cell is named for
  and reports every cell that survives its own, failing on a survivor and on a mutation that did
  not apply. The stage-console split shipped eight gates that were green for a reason other than
  the one they were named for; this keeps that from being re-established quietly. On-demand, not
  a CI gate.
