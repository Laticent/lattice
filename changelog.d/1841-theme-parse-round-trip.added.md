- Theme CSS now reads back into an ordered, selector-aware declaration record and writes back out unchanged (`lib/theme/parse.js`). A theme edited by hand keeps its own formatting, comments and blank lines: only the declaration you changed is rewritten, and every other byte is copied through.
- `serializeTheme` now emits custom properties that sit outside the token contract instead of dropping them. Measured across the 32 shipped themes, 48 such names in 19 files were silently deleted by a read-then-write, among them the whole `--cat-N-texture` channel the accessibility palettes use to carry categorical distinction without color, `--chart-catN-ink`, and `--hljs-params` / `--hljs-tag`. Dropping them also broke names that *were* in the contract: `--spectrum` is built out of `--brand-*` operands, so losing the operands left it resolving invalid inside a `background:` shorthand and taking the whole declaration down with it.
