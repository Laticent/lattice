- The nightly golden bless reaches `main` again. Its showcase step no longer exits 1 on a gallery whose PDF the bless has not rendered yet: `rasterize-showcase.mjs` keeps that component's WebP until the PDF lands, for any gallery that moved, was renamed or is newly flagged, not just the chart kinds' move (#2997). A showcase or re-bless problem now holds auto-merge and turns the night red after the bless PR is updated, instead of stopping the update.
- The nightly bless renders each golden once. `regression-gate.mjs --promote` keeps the fresh render of whatever drifted (a deck, or one mood of a gallery), which is byte-identical to a re-bless, so the second render of every drifted golden is gone. `--jobs N` (or `auto`, one per core) checks galleries and decks side by side; the bless runs `--jobs auto --promote`.
- First renders of goldens with no PDF yet run one process per core, galleries first, and stop starting at the night's deadline instead of at a fixed count of 40. The fixed count, with alphabetical order, picked the same 40 `examples/` decks every night while the bless PR waited, so the 28 moved chart galleries were never rendered.
- `tools/lib/pool.js` is the one bounded-concurrency helper for `tools/`; `fit-harness.js` and `check-overflow-corpus.js` use it in place of their own copies.
