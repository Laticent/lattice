- **Added: the additive trio — `seam-census`, `blast-radius` and `contradictions`
  roster agents.** A generative counterpart to the adversarial trio, which audits
  claims you already have and cannot produce them. The three ask what is in a system
  that nobody has named: the joins and what each produces that neither side does
  alone; what dies when a piece is deleted, walked over real import edges and ranked
  by user-visible capability; and which forced tradeoffs the system refuses, at what
  price, plus the ones it does not. Each card requires a kill list and forbids
  sourcing a load-bearing claim from prose rather than source. Documented in
  `engineering/orchestration.md` as its own section — deliberately **not** a rung on
  the verification ladder, and never mandatory. "The trio" on its own still means the
  adversarial trio; the additive trio must be named in full.
