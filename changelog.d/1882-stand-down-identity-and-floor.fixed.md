- Nightly alarm stand-downs now identify their rolling issue by the filing identity, not by a title prefix anyone can type. Keyed on the title alone, a stand-down selected — and closed — an issue it never opened, stamping it "Recovered … measured green" about something nobody measured.
- Stand-downs act only on `main`. A `workflow_dispatch` from any branch could previously close the live alarm using evidence gathered on that branch, and record it as `main@<sha>` for a commit that was never on `main`.
- The two alarms that close outright now require a non-zero measurement (`islands`, `cases`). A scan that examined nothing produced the same "healthy" output as a scan that examined everything, so an ordinary refactor that emptied the corpus would have closed the thread on a measurement that never happened.
- `perf-nightly`'s narrow closer discriminates on the thread's latest firing instead of its create-time body. The body describes the first firing forever, so a harness-failure night followed by a real regression and one clean night closed a live regression.
