- **`<ins>` and `<del>` now announce where they start and stop.** A tracked amendment used to reach a listener as the old wording and the new wording run together in one sentence, with nothing marking which was which — a clause that reads as the opposite of what it means. Every `<ins>` / `<del>` (and, inside `redline` only, the `<s>` that `~~text~~` produces) now carries a visually-hidden edge label either side of it, on every render path: the CLI/PDF export, the Playground, the Studio, and the `lattice-runtime` bundle the marp-vscode preview loads. Nothing about the rendered slide changes — measured at 0 changed pixels across the redline gallery, and the labels stay out of the PDF's extractable text, so copying a verbatim clause is unaffected.
- The `redline` component's own summary line rendered its example `<ins>`/`<del>` as live empty elements, so the words "ins" and "del" were eaten out of the sentence on the gallery cover ("inline / tracking the amendment"). Backticked, matching how every other component's docs refer to a tag.
