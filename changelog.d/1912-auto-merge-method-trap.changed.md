- `engineering/workflow.md` § Merging now documents the auto-merge method trap, and the conclusion is that **an agent does not arm auto-merge at all**. On both paths anyone has watched, an explicitly requested squash was not what got stored: `enable_pr_auto_merge` with `mergeMethod: SQUASH` returned `(method: MERGE)` on #1870 — confirmed independently by the REST API and by GitHub's own webhook — and `gh pr merge --auto --squash` stores `"merge"` on PR #1613 today. The method is GraphQL-only, so arming belongs to the human from the PR page; an agent's job stops at green, rebased, card posted. The section carries the read-back that confirms what was actually stored, and says to disable auto-merge immediately if it is not `squash`.
