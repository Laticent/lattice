- **Seven new chart components: `bar`, `stacked-bar`, `line`, `waterfall`, `scatter`, `slope`, `bullet`.** The chart family shipped fourteen components and not one of them plotted a value against an axis — `progress` looked like a bar chart but encoded a percentage with no scale, `quadrant` looked like a scatter but its axes carried no units, and `gantt`'s only axis was time. A deck that needed "revenue by quarter", "where the budget went" or "actual against plan" had nothing to say it with. Each new member is admitted because it makes a claim no existing component can make, and each authors from the family's ordinary bullet-list series DSL.
- **A shared Cartesian substrate, `_chart-family/cartesian.js`.** One series parser, one nice-number tick generator, one gutter convention, one gridline weight and one rectangular fill recipe, so the seven read as one deck rather than seven products. Its paint side is `chart-family.css` § Cartesian chrome.
- Magnitude suffixes are now **scale, not decoration**: `1.2M` is 1 200 000, so one axis can carry `800k` and `1.2M` honestly. `%` is deliberately not a magnitude. The axis also speaks the author's own affix — a deck written in `$4.2M` gets an axis reading `$0 · $2M · $4M`, not a bare number with the unit stranded in the title.
