- Editing a Mermaid fence updates the diagram about three times faster: a keystroke inside a fence now reaches the redrawn diagram in ~60ms where it took 209ms, and arriving at a diagram slide takes ~99ms where it took 240ms. The fixed 150ms debounce in front of every diagram render is gone — it was 72% of the wait, and larger than a full render for any diagram up to about 64 nodes. Renders now coalesce on COMPLETION instead: at most one in flight, and if the source moved while it ran, it runs again with the latest text. That adapts to the diagram rather than guessing at it — a small graph updates at render speed while a large one throttles itself to one render per render instead of queueing one per keystroke.
- A diagram that does not parse *while you are still typing* no longer blanks the slot and shows you raw source on every keystroke. The previous drawing stays on screen, and the error box appears once you stop (about 450ms of quiet). A `mermaid.parse` check in front of the render is what makes this cheap — it rejects a half-written diagram in ~9ms where drawing one costs 22–239ms.
