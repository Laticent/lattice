- **Studio boot no longer flickers at the hand-off.** The pre-paint shell stands in for two things that become ready at different times — the app's chrome and the live preview — and both used to wait on the preview. The app's finished chrome therefore sat under an opaque cover, muted to 62%, for 672ms on a fast connection and 3463ms on a modeled 1200kbps link, then cross-faded to full brightness; that cross-fade was the flicker. The hand-off is now two stages: the chrome is uncovered in the same paint that draws it (dead time 0ms on both), and the Nacre loader keeps covering the preview until the engine's first render, as before.
- **The Studio's top bands no longer shift as the page loads.** JetBrains Mono backs the EDIT and PREVIEW sub-bars but was not preloaded, so it arrived ~500ms after the two faces that were and moved the Reader-view pill and everything right of it 2.781px sideways. It is now preloaded (+31KB, covering every mono weight the site uses) and all three families resolve together. The preload moves the fetch to parse time rather than removing `font-display: swap`, so on a link slow enough that the file has not landed when the shell paints, the fallback still paints first.
