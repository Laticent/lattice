- **The Studio's boot no longer holds a finished app behind a dimmed stand-in.** The pre-paint shell stands in for two things that become ready at different times — the app's chrome and the live preview — and both used to wait on the preview. The app's finished chrome therefore sat under an opaque cover, muted to 62%, for 672ms on a fast connection and 3463ms on a modeled 1200kbps link, then cross-faded to full brightness; that cross-fade was the flicker. The hand-off is now two stages: the chrome is uncovered in the same paint that draws it (0ms on both), and the Nacre loader keeps covering the preview until the engine's first render, as before. The hand-off is still a visible step — the stand-in is deliberately muted to 62% and the app's chrome is not — but it is one frame at the moment the app becomes live, rather than a cross-fade seconds later.
- **The Studio's chrome no longer moves when the web fonts land.** The pre-paint shell painted in `system-ui`, which is a poor metric match for Outfit — 24% wider at weight 600 — and `font-display: swap` then re-laid the text out when the woff2 arrived. Measured on an iPad Air 4 viewport, cold, at 1200kbps: the deck title moved **-42.27px**, carrying the deck pill and everything right of it, plus 14.38px on the Reader-view pill. Three changes remove it. JetBrains Mono is now preloaded alongside the two faces that already were (+31KB, every mono weight the site uses). Metric-adjusted fallback faces (`Outfit Fallback`, `JetBrains Mono Fallback`, `Playfair Fallback`, via `size-adjust` and the three metric overrides) join every site-chrome font stack, so the pre-webfont text measures close to the real thing. And all twelve faces move from `font-display: swap` to `optional`, which is what actually removes the reflow: the browser gives the font ~100ms and, if it is not ready, keeps the fallback for that load and never swaps. Now measured at 0 movement above 0.01px, in both orientations, cold and warm, at 1200/400/150kbps.
- **The cost, stated plainly:** a first cold visit on a slow link renders the chrome in the metric-matched fallback rather than in Outfit — measured as a 2px difference on the deck title and 1px on the wordmark, against the 42.27px jump it replaces. The font still downloads and caches, so the next navigation gets the real face with no shift either way.
