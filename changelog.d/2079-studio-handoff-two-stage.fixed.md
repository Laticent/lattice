- **Studio boot no longer flickers at the hand-off.** The pre-paint shell stands in for two things that become ready at different times — the app's chrome and the live preview — and both used to wait on the preview. The app's finished chrome therefore sat under an opaque cover, muted to 62%, for 672ms on a fast connection and 3463ms on a modeled 1200kbps link, then cross-faded to full brightness; that cross-fade was the flicker. The hand-off is now two stages: the chrome is uncovered in the same paint that draws it (dead time 0ms on both), and the Nacre loader keeps covering the preview until the engine's first render, as before.
- **The Studio's top bands shift less as the page loads.** JetBrains Mono backs the EDIT and PREVIEW sub-bars but was not preloaded, so it arrived several hundred ms after the two faces that were, and moved the Reader-view pill and everything right of it 2.781px sideways. It is now preloaded (+31KB, covering every mono weight the site uses), which pulls it ~400–600ms earlier and roughly halves the observed shifts. It does not remove them: the preload moves the fetch to parse time and leaves `font-display: swap` in place, so the shell can still paint in the fallback and swap. Removing them outright needs metric-adjusted fallback faces, a site-wide font-stack change that is not in this release.
