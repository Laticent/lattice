- Exporting a deck with diagrams no longer risks baking a blank space into the PDF where a diagram should be. The export used to wait a fixed four seconds for diagrams to finish and then capture the page regardless — and because the stylesheet hides the fence's source text once a diagram is on its way, a deck that needed longer produced a blank region in the downloaded file. It now waits for diagrams to stop making *progress* instead: it gives up on a diagram that is stuck, never on one that is merely slow.
