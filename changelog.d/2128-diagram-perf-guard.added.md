- A regression guard for Mermaid diagram scheduling, in two tiers. A unit-speed metamorphic test asserts fourteen relations over the shipped policy — a costlier diagram never redraws more often than a cheaper one, a slower cadence never redraws fewer times, the expensive path is indistinguishable from the build before it, the wait after the last keystroke is never longer than that build's — and it runs on every push with no browser. A nightly Playwright pair drives the real Studio and checks that the runtime actually schedules the way the policy says, asserting on render and parse counts rather than milliseconds, because counts survive a change of machine and timings do not.
