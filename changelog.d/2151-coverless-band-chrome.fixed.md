- `split-panel pullquote`'s coverless split pages place their wayfinding marks instead of
  stacking them. `pullquote` leads with the quotation and carries no `<h2>`, so its run is
  `lat-split-native` body pages with no footer cell — and both marks dock at SECTION level,
  which on this layout IS the panel flex container. At portrait that put the forward pointer in
  the bottom band, over the k-of-N rail (42.1×6.5px) and the running footer's ink
  (206.6×24.5px); at `square` the container is a row, so the same item became a third COLUMN,
  squeezed the right panel from 450.9px to 252.7px and ran 53px past the slide edge. The
  pointer is now positioned in the band rather than laid out in the panel row, the band it
  lands in is reserved, and the k-of-N rail takes its ink from whichever field holds the
  corner — so the marks read on all five variants against all six palettes, and the panels keep
  their full bleed at every size. This lifts the deferral shipped alongside the variant
  enrollment: these runs carried the rail but not the pointer, and now carry both.
