- A coverless `split-panel` split page carries its k-of-N rail but **not** a forward pointer, for now. The pointer is a flex item of whichever box its layout puts it in, and a coverless native page has no footer cell — so on this component it lands in the panel row itself and becomes a third column, squeezing the content panel from 450.9px to 252.7px at `square` and running off the slide. Placing it properly needs its own change (absolute placement, a reserved band, and rail ink keyed to whichever field holds the corner), so these runs split without it rather than shipping it broken.
