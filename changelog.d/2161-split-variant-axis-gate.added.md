- `split-panel`'s coverless-split rules are now gated on the variant axis: a rule keyed on a variant no test fixture renders fails the suite until the variant is covered or carries a written exemption, and an exemption that no longer describes anything fails too. The k-of-N rail's ink also gets a palette matrix (`test/unit/css/split-panel-rail-ink.test.js`) measuring every segment's composited contrast against the field that geometrically holds the corner, rather than resting on a sweep recorded as prose.
- **Known residual, unchanged by this release:** on a coverless `split-panel` split page an over-stuffed member can still print under the forward-pointer pill (measured 396.1×51.5px at portrait with three members at 32 words), and the engine does not always say so. The cause is that `overflow-probe.js` measures a clipping cell as `scrollHeight - clientHeight` where `clientHeight` includes padding, so a chrome band reserved with `padding-bottom` is a blind zone of exactly that height — a shared-kernel issue rather than a component one, and fixed there rather than here.
