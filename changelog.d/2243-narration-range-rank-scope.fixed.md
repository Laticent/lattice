- **The range and rank rules no longer claim tokens that are not ranges or ranks.** Both spellings of a range multiplier (`2-3x`, `2-3×`) now read the same way. Both shipped too wide inside this same change set and a pre-merge review caught them on real decks. A hyphenated legal citation was renumbered — `§22-1201` narrated as "section twenty-two to one thousand two hundred one", a section the slide does not show, on seven decks including the legal component's own gallery. A hyphen now needs a unit (currency, magnitude, percent, multiplier) to read as a range; an en dash still reads as one on its own, so `$1.2–1.4B`, `50-60%` and `2-3x` are unaffected while `2026-09`, `555-1234` and `9-5` are left alone. Separately, an all-digit hex color read as a rank (`#000000` → "number zero"), so the two hex lengths — exactly three or six digits — are refused.
- **A very large integer reads its digits rather than an exponent's letters.** `toFixed(0)` is itself exponential at or above 10²¹, so the fallback spoke "one e + two one".
- **The direction dedup stops at a sentence boundary.** "We are up. +18% YoY." is two sentences and the second one's sign is not a duplicate — the caption track and the plain-text render disagreed on exactly that input.
- **A `:::tint` suffix no longer hides a state transition.** A tinted transition matched neither the narrator nor its suppression filter, so a chart read half its transitions by name and the other half as raw `accept => 3:::state-pass-hue` glyphs. (The tint is Lattice’s own state-chart channel, written *after* the closing backtick — an earlier attempt at this looked for it inside the backticks, which is Mermaid’s unrelated `:::className` shape, and was therefore a no-op on every deck that ships one.)
- **An unresolved transition is read rather than dropped.** The suppression filter removed every transition-shaped line, including one whose target does not exist — which the slide still renders as `typo => 9 (unresolved)`. It is spoken again.
- **Journey mood mirrors the chart's own clamp**, so narration cannot state a score the picture does not plot: `:99` reads five, `:0` reads one, `:4x` reads four, and a bare `@` is ignored instead of producing an empty actor.
