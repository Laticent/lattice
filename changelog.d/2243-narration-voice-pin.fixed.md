- **Read-aloud no longer changes voice — or language — partway through a deck.** Narration re-resolved the speaking rung and the voice preference on *every sentence*, and the cloud and on-device rungs read different preferences, which may hold voices in different languages. So the on-device model finishing its download mid-deck, a key expiring, or a second Studio tab writing the preference would switch the voice between one sentence and the next. A read now pins its `{rung, voice, speed}` once and keeps it.
- **A clip can no longer be cached under the wrong voice.** The audio cache key was built from one read of the voice preference while the synthesis request took another, so a clip synthesized in one voice could be stored under another's key — and persisted to IndexedDB, where it replayed on later visits.
