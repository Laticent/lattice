- **A finish composites against the surface the slide paints in every mode, not only under `dark`.** #2293 re-pointed `--fin-canvas` at a frame's own canvas, but scoped the accent-cover half to `.dark` because that is the mode it had broken. A cover paints `var(--accent)` in every mode, so the light, `color-light`, `color-system` and print rows carried the same mismatch all along — and the cross **pinned them by name**, which certifies a defect rather than recording it. The pin is gone and the pinned set is empty.
- **`lat-split-cover` joins the list, and it was wrong in all six modes.** It needs no entry in `base.modifiers.css`'s dark exemption — it is declared ~2,200 lines after that rule and already wins at equal specificity — but nothing about source order makes `--fin-canvas` follow it, so the shared split cover every plain-pagination layout gets had never been named.
- **Print gets its own arm, keyed on the role stamp the de-flood is keyed on.** `section.print[data-split-role="cover"]` repaints a split cover's accent field with the bookends' framed panel, so a printed cover is `rgb(236,236,236)` while `--fin-canvas` was white. The mirror carries no frame class for the same reason the painter does not: the stamp lands on every cover the splitter emits.
- **Two canvases nothing had ever measured.** `section.split-panel-cover:is([data-split-mods~="cat-N"])` paints `var(--panel-fill)` and the `image`/`scene` gallery, spotlight and statement compositions paint the matte (`var(--bg-alt)` through `--img-matte`/`--scene-matte`). Both are gated on an **attribute**, and every derivation in the cross walked `ClassSelector` nodes — so 40 imagery rows and the whole category-tint family were invisible to it. Measured, indaco: `image gallery` is `rgb(242,245,250)` on a light deck, `rgb(0,40,71)` under dark and `rgb(245,245,245)` under print, against a `--fin-canvas` of white in all three.
- **The cross grew a subject-attribute axis**, derived from the built bundle the way the modifier list is: any attribute selector in a section-subject painting rule becomes an axis value, crossed against every canvas mode and register with and without `dark`. It takes the cross from 48,636 cells to 60,263 — 11,627 rows, +24%, +1.7s. It refuses an attribute it cannot turn into a probe, proves each built probe matches the selector it came from in the browser, and proves the `[class…]` selectors it declines are reached by the class axis instead.
- **`print` and `dark` are not exclusive, and the print face had to learn it.** A stamped cover carrying both plus a register hands the surface to `section.dark.spectrum-off` `(0,2,1)` or `section.dark:is(.spectrum-edge-*)` `(0,3,1)` in `base.variants.css`, which out-orders the de-flood at equal specificity. The de-flood's mirror also carries that rule's own `:not(.divider)`, because those three edge values do not repaint a divider — without it, `divider dark print spectrum-edge-left` composites a white ellipse over `rgb(236,236,236)`.
- Measured over 18 frames × 219 modifier classes × every register pair × every subject attribute × both DOM shapes — 60,263 cells per palette, Chromium 131, on **all 33 palettes**. `main` leaves **3,976–12,628** rows per palette compositing against a color the slide does not paint (12,628 on indaco); this leaves **0** on every one.
