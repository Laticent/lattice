- The nine shipped finishes are now generated from their recipes. `npm run build` runs `lib/finishes/finish-generate.js`, the same generator the Finish Studio uses, over every `lib/finishes/<name>/<name>.recipe.json` and writes each `section.finish-<name>` rule into `lib/base/base.finish.css`. Retuning a recipe now changes what decks render, and "Start from preset" in the Studio now reproduces the shipped finish. Measured against the old hand-written rules, the export (print) face matches on eight of nine finishes; nimbus's vignette differs by at most 3 of 255 levels. The screen face differs by at most 6 of 255 levels, on at most 0.02% of pixels.
- The finish vocabulary gains four terms the shipped finishes needed: a thin `rule` mark (atrium's margin rule), a `wash.hairline` strip (strata's top line), a corner-anchored glyph (`mark.anchor: "corner"` with `mark.inset`, as used by meridian, savile and gallery), and a hand-tuned screen face for the fold (`edge.rich`, ledger's). In the Studio, moving an anchored glyph turns it into a free one.
- A Studio-made finish's texture on screen is one accent step lighter (+2 instead of +3, matching the shipped finishes), its monogram mixes at 9% like the numeral, and its inset frame is sized in the slide's own units, as the shipped gallery frame always was.
