- **Breaking:** `list-steps` (its side-by-side row, including `phase`, `stage` and `tier`), `compare-prose` (both panes, except `decision`) and `cards-stack horizontal` now opt into the `cards:` register with a `center` default. Sparse cards size to their text and the row centers on the stage instead of stretching to fill it. A slide that ends in a key-insight or note still stretches. Add `cards: stretch` to the front matter, or `_class: … cards-stretch` on one slide, to restore the old look. `cards: center | stretch | top | spread` and `cards-*` now work on all three (#2317).
