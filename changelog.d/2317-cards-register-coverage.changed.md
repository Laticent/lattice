- **Breaking:** `list-steps` (its side-by-side row, including `phase`, `stage`, `tier` and `capsule`), `compare-prose` (both panes) and `cards-stack horizontal` now opt into the `cards:` register with a `center` default. Sparse cards size to their text and the row centers on the stage instead of stretching to fill it. Add `cards: stretch` to the front matter, or `_class: … cards-stretch` on one slide, to restore the old look. `cards: center | stretch | top | spread` and `cards-*` now work on all three (#2317).
