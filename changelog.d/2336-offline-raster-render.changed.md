- **Breaking:** the CLI's PDF, PPTX, PNG and image-set renders no longer fetch anything from the network. A remote image, video, font or stylesheet in a deck is left out and shows the browser's broken-image mark, as it already did in the Studio preview and the `.html` export; local images, `data:` URIs and the bundled fonts, Mermaid and KaTeX are unaffected. Pass `--allow-remote` to fetch them as before. No shipped deck is affected: the data-viz gallery renders byte-identical PDFs either way.
