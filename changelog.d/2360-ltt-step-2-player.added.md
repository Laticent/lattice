- **Added: a narrated HTML export now plays from its Lattice Timing Track.** The Studio's webpage
  export embeds the deck's LTT (engineering/ltt.md) as one packed block: every slide's arrival
  hold, every sentence, every breath. The player times every hold, silent sentence and breath,
  and its caption crawl, through `positionAt` — the function video export will use — so the
  player restates no timing rule of its own. The clips still ride in one block per slide, parsed
  only when that slide speaks. A narrated 7-slide export grows by 7–10 KB (3 KB gzipped),
  most of it the inlined timing code; a deck without narration is byte-identical.
- **Added: `positionAt`, `timeline` and `makeCursor` in `@laticent/ltt`,** with conformance
  fixtures that run against the source, the copy the exported player inlines, that copy after
  esbuild's and rolldown's minifiers, and the exported player itself on a fake clock. `makeCursor`
  moved from Cadenza, which still re-exports it. Cadenza also exports `ENGINE_HASH`, the timing
  engine's content hash, regenerated by `npm run engine-hash:build`.
