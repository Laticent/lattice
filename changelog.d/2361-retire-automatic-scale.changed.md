- `venue:` and `scale-l` / `scale-xl` / `scale-2xl` are fixed sizes, like desktop zoom. Every slide renders at the size the deck asks for on every surface — the PDF, the exported `.html`, the Studio preview and Present — and nothing steps a too-full slide, or the deck, down to fit. A slide too full for its venue clips, and three places name it: `lint:deck` (`capacity-scale`), the Studio's ring and clip notice, and the export's `⚠ OVERFLOW` line. (An automatic step-down, with a `↓ SCALE` report and a hidden whole-deck measuring frame in the Studio, was on `main` from #2378 to this change and never released.)
- The Studio's deck settings have a **Venue** menu (Look → Venue: Default, Laptop, Huddle, Conference, Hall) that writes `venue:`.
- Present has a **venue switch** that changes the room size for one showing without editing the deck.
- When a slide is too full for the deck's venue, the Studio shows a notice under the preview with two one-click fixes: **Split slide** (halves the slide's main list or table onto a second slide that repeats the heading) and **Use <next venue down>**.
- An ordered list that starts past 1 (`4. Report`) now keeps its number in layouts that draw their own counter (`list-steps` STEP badges, `q-and-a`, and the others); they used to restart at 01. A list the author started past 1 also keeps its numbers when the engine splits it across portrait pages.
- `lint:deck`'s venue budget (`capacity-scale`) is closer to what a real deck renders. It interpolates between measured word lengths, reads a variant's own row (`list takeaway` holds far more than a bare `list`), counts the height of a trailing `> …` callout, and now covers components with a venue row but no count budget (`compare-prose`). On a 74-slide talk it names 16 / 25 / 38 of the 17 / 31 / 47 pages that clip at huddle / conference / hall (was 9 / 18 / 29), with 3 / 4 / 1 false warnings (was 10 / 6 / 4), and it no longer claims a slide clips at the designed size when it does not. That includes the slides that clip at `venue: hall` because the venue lifts labels past the body.
- `tools/calibrate-capacity.js` takes `--variant <token>` and `--insight`, to measure a register's own row and the cost of a trailing callout.
