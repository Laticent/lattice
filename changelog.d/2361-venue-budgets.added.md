- Every component now states how many elements it holds at each `venue:` (laptop, huddle, conference, hall), or why it has no count budget. The numbers live in the component's manifest (`venueCapacity`), print on a "**By venue**" line in its `.docs.md` and in a `by venue` column of `dist/docs/components.pick.md` (never above the component's `capacity` max), and are the numbers `lint:deck`'s `capacity-scale` checks on components that have a `capacity` budget. Newly measured: checklist, compare-prose, cycle, decision, glossary, kanban, list-tabular, matrix-2x2, policy-recommendation, roadmap, split-compare, split-panel, stats, table and timeline-list.
- `build:check` now rejects a per-slide type-size change in engine CSS: a modifier may change spacing, chrome and color, never the size of a type role (`checkTypeSizeModifiers`, with a sanctioned allowlist). The rule and the audit of every modifier are in `engineering/typography.md` §7.
- **Changed:** `compact` on `cards-stack` and `q-and-a` no longer shrinks the text; it tightens spacing only, like `compact` everywhere else. A `q-and-a` slide now holds four pairs, or five with `compact` (was six), and a component gallery's "When NOT to reach for" slide splits over two or three slides when its anti-patterns run long.
