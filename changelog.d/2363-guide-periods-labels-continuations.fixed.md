- **Present's Guide follows three more ways a narrator names things.** A spoken period meets the
  slide's written one ("the third-quarter review for fiscal twenty-six" points at a `Q3 FY26`
  title). A sentence that says one word of a card's label points at that card ("We did look hard at
  the fix" → the `Why not fix it` card), when no other painted text on the slide says the word. And
  a sentence that opens with a pronoun and shares a word with what the sentence before it named stays
  there ("It costs more than the segment earns" keeps the same card). Asides that name nothing still
  hide the Guide.
