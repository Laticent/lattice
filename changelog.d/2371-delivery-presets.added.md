- **A deck can set how much its narrated Guide gestures, with the `delivery:` front-matter key.** The values are `restrained` (the default, for a boardroom), `expressive` (a sales room, a talk, a lunch-and-learn) and `somber` (bad news; no cursor). The Guide no longer gestures on every block change. At each slide it ranks what the narration names, favoring authored `_focus`, measured figures, emphasis, chart marks and a chart's extreme mark. It then spends the preset's budget on the top moments and holds the hand still through the rest. On the Q3 board fixture, gestures per slide dropped from a mean of 3.0 (max 5) to 1.35 (max 2) under `restrained`, and to 2.7 (max 4) under `expressive`. An expressive deck makes its top moment's gesture heavier. The linter flags an unknown value (`unknown-delivery`), and the Studio editor completes the key and its values.
