- **`_focus: mark N` and `_focus: series N` name one mark or one series on a chart.** `mark 3` spotlights the third bar, wedge, funnel band, heatmap cell or state in the order the list was written, and `series 2` spotlights the second line or radar polygon. The rest of the chart recedes to 40% (text recedes to 24%) so its shape stays readable as context, and a focused line gains weight. `_focusStyle: ring` edges the focused mark in the accent and keeps every other mark at full strength. Fifteen chart components now declare these axes, so the Studio editor completes them. Line dots now carry `data-series`, so a receded line's dots fade with it.
