- A narrated webpage export of a deck that sets `delivery:` now plays the Guide: while the deck presents itself, the element the narration names stays at full strength and the rest recedes, as in the Studio's Present, with the preset's depth and tempo. Pausing lifts the focus, and playing again replays the slide with its focus; with the captions off, the word being said lights on the slide. The Guide adds about 37 KB to the file. A deck without `delivery:` exports exactly as before.
- The Studio and the exported player now run one Guide kernel (`docs/src/components/studio/guide-kernel.ts`): the resolver, the salience plan, the focus, and the focus policy (the rest, the resume after a pause, the chart walk, the aside hold). `npm run guide-player:build` bundles it for the player, and `node tools/verify-guide-player.mjs` plays an export in Chromium and logs every focus.
