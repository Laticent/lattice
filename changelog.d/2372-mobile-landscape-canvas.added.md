- **`size: mobile-landscape`: a 19.5:9 canvas that fills a phone held sideways.** A deck on it keeps
  HD's type, spacing and content box, centered on a canvas 1560 px wide, so a video of the deck shows
  no side bars on a phone and every component lays out as it does at 16:9. `lattice video deck.md
  --size mobile-landscape` records it, and `--size NAME` renders any deck on another registered
  canvas without editing it. The Studio's slide-size picker offers it.
