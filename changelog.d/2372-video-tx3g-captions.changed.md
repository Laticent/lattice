- **`lattice video` muxes its captions as `tx3g`**, the 3GPP timed-text format QuickTime, iOS
  and macOS use for subtitles, in place of the WebVTT-in-MP4 (`wvtt`) track no player offered.
  Each caption shows exactly while its sentence plays and clears between sentences. The `.vtt`
  sidecar is unchanged. Whether QuickTime, PowerPoint and Keynote offer the track is not yet
  verified.
