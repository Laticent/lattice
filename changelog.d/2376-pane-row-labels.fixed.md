- `progress` and `matrix-grid` in a side pane give their row names room. The label column was a share of the pane while its type stays slide-sized, so in a 50% pane every three-word name set one word per line and both charts clipped at five rows. In a pane the column now takes its slide width, capped at 40% of the pane (progress) or 24% (matrix-grid, whose `Level 1` cells must still set on one line). Their side budgets return to 6 and 4. Slides without panes, and panes squarer than the slide, are unchanged. `examples/panes-row-labels.md`.
- A status pill in any pane is sized like a slide's. Its height was a share of the pane, so a 50% pane drew each pill 9px tall around 17px of type. progress's track takes slide units in a pane for the same reason.
- A `progress` readout wider than its bar (a small percent in a narrow track) runs on past the bar's end instead of losing its first digit to the fill's clip. A readout that fits is placed as before.
