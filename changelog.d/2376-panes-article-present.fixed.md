- The exported player's Read · Article view draws a chart pane styled, like a chart slide. Before, it drew black bars with labels several times too large. Each pane now reads as the slide it would be on its own, in the order written, so a KPI pane reads as figures, not a numbered list.
- The Studio's Present steps through a split panes slide. On a square, portrait or story deck, Next shows the second pane before it moves on, Previous comes back to it, and the presenter's next-slide tile shows it. The arrows now count pages, so a one-slide deck whose slide splits can still reach its second pane. Autoplay still advances a slide at a time, showing a split slide's first pane while it reads both.
- Present's frame now takes the deck's shape. It was always 16:9, which cut off everything below the heading on a portrait slide. On a portrait deck the presenter's next-slide tile keeps room for the speaker notes.
- The Studio frames a deck by the size the engine actually renders. `size: 4:3`, `size: Square` and `size: STORY` are not size names the engine knows, so they render at 16:9. The preview used to draw them at the shape the name suggests, leaving an empty band beside the slide.
- A panes slide's markers no longer show up as its speaker note in the Studio, and setting a note no longer deletes them (which turned the slide back into a single component).
- The Studio's slide strip labels a panes slide "panes" instead of "text".
