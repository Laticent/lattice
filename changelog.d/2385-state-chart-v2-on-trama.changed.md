- **Breaking:** the state chart moves to the flowchart's grammar (state chart v2). A transition is an arrow to a state's NAME, `- -approve-> Approved`, not `` `approve => 4` ``; a self-loop names its own state. `:::token` tints are gone: paint with a status word, a palette slot `:c1`…`:c8`, `=>` for the main path or `:dashed` / `:dotted`. A prose sub-bullet under a state now makes a composite state; write detail as a `>` blockquote. `lint:deck` names the old spellings with their fix (`state-chart-v1-transition`, `state-chart-v1-tint`), every deck in the repo is migrated, and the export now warns on each v1 slide. `node tools/migrate-state-chart-v1.js --write <deck>` migrates a deck of your own, and checks every rewrite against the new parser before it writes.
- The state chart lays out and draws on Trama, the graph-chart library the flowchart uses: lines are the router's elbows, which never cross a state; labels sit on their line; a chain still wraps into rows in reading order and needs no layout engine; typing in the Studio lays a large machine out in a worker. New: composite states (a sub-list of states), shape words on a state, an authored key, and `unnumbered` to drop the badges.
- **Breaking:** on both graph charts a `>` blockquote under a shape or state is HIDDEN DETAIL — shown on hover or tap in Present, Practice and Preview, and folded into the speaker notes — instead of a visible note card on the flowchart. A PDF does not show it; write text the page must carry as a caption.
- `curved` rounds the lines' corners generously on both charts (new on the flowchart).
- A graph chart's key shows words that paint the same tone as one entry (`On track · Done`), on the flowchart as it already did on the state chart.
- Status words fold case in both charts' grammar (`AT-RISK` is `at-risk`).
- **Breaking** (for hosts that read the figure): the state chart's figure carries `data-sc-model` (was `data-sc-transitions`) and marks a drawn chart `data-sc-drawn` (was `data-sc-svg`). `installStateChartLayout` moved from `state-chart.transform.js` to `state-chart.layout.js`, and the v1 helpers (`parseStateLi`, `TRANSITION_RE`) are gone.
- A line into a composite state enters its first state, and a line out leaves from each state inside it, in the roles, the narration and the `inline` rows; `inline` also names each composite. The `inline` chips draw their arrows as shapes instead of typed glyphs.
- A status wins over a palette slot on one state, as on the flowchart, so the key never names a status the chart does not show.
- A graph chart too dense for its slide, under half size with a fit that cannot settle, is drawn after two fit rounds instead of four: raising its type floor only shrinks it further, and two rounds are enough to measure that. A chart under half size whose fit does settle still reaches the type floor. A `--fluid` page with a 36-state machine took 29 s to load; with one round it took about 6.5 s, and the second round adds about 1.5 s to the type-floor watcher test. A stage collapsed to no height is no longer laid out until it has one.
- A chart finish (`chart-finish-pigment` / `-etching` / `-tone`) repaints a state chart's key with its tiles: the key is now the shared graph key, and its status swatches take the same finish as the states they key, in the default and `inline` variants alike.
