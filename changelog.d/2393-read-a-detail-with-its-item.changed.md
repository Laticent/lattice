- Chart narration now reads each item's `detail` sublist right after that item, as its own sentence, instead of as a list at the end of the slide. A stacked bar used to say all three years and then "Services priced per seat. Services moved to fixed-fee." with nothing saying which year each belonged to; it now says "FY24: Licenses, nineteen point one; Services, nine point eight. Services priced per seat. FY25: …". This covers bar, stacked-bar, line, slope and dumbbell, scatter, map, piechart, waterfall, funnel, radar and quadrant, and a journey task's nested note. A line chart reads a category's detail once, after the first spoken point in that category; a category no series speaks a point for (a gap, or a long line's summary) is named first, after the chart ("M5. Pricing change."). A radar reads an axis's detail after the sentence that names the axis. The Guide, in the Studio and in an exported player, keeps the item focused while its detail is read; on a line chart, a category's note keeps the point just read in focus instead of lifting it.
- The `radar quadrant` variant now reads an axis's detail sublist, with any HTML comment in it left unsaid. Its narrator used to drop the detail without a word.
