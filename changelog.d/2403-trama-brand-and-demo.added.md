- **Trama has its own mark and a live demo at `/trama`.** The mark is the weft: two
  right-angled terracotta threads woven over and under each other around the family hub,
  with one straight gold thread for the main path (`docs/public/trama-{mark,mark-min,lockup}.svg`,
  palette locked in `engineering/decisions/2026-07-18-sibling-brand-system.md`). The page runs
  the real kernel on every keystroke: type rows and watch `layout()` place and route them,
  pin the direction across or down, turn on the wrapping chain, and drag a box (or nudge it
  with the arrow keys) to watch `route()` weave the lines again, with all ten
  `geo.quality` counters on screen. Trama joins the site's Libraries menu.
