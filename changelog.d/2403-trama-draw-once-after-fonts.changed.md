- **Flowcharts draw once, in the deck's own fonts, and sooner.** On a cold page load Trama's pipeline used to lay every flowchart out three times, twice in fallback fonts it then threw away. It now waits for the page's fonts (at most 2 seconds), then draws each chart once: a 7-chart deck goes from 32 layouts to 13, and its last chart appears about 550 ms sooner. The CLI export of that deck takes about 0.6 s less.
- **A flowchart the fit shrinks hard keeps its 11 px type floor on its first draw.** The fit and the type floor are solved together, and a chart that needed many rounds to agree used to stop short after three when it had no earlier draw to start from (a chart pasted into the Studio, say, or now every cold load): the typing test deck's 17-shape chart painted its smallest text at 10.2 px. The third and later rounds now take a secant step, so such a chart settles in 4 layouts at the floor. Charts that settled before draw exactly as they did.
