- **Each `delivery:` is now its own style, not a louder or quieter copy of the others.** `restrained` focuses every part of a chart the narration names, one quiet crossfade at a time, with no ink and no cursor. `expressive` leads with the cursor and inks every act: it underlines the slide's claim, brackets the figure, traces a line, taps each bar or point, washes a detail and circles the highest or lowest. `somber` stays still until the slide's key beat, then makes one slow focus and holds it. Before, the three ran the same moves and differed only in how many they got, so `restrained`'s limit of two moments a slide left most of a chart dark.
- The Guide no longer guesses which part of a chart a sentence is about from its words. The chart narrator binds each sentence to the bar, point, stage, cell, row or state it names, and each chart component declares how that part is drawn (its manifest's new `scene`). A new test resolves every bound sentence on every chart gallery slide against the real render, and a score file per chart pins what each delivery does with it.
- The moment budget is retired: `restrained` and `expressive` focus every sentence that names a part of the slide.
- Vetrina has a new gesture, `trace`: a stroke drawn through a series' points in order, the hand riding it.
- A narrated webpage export of a `delivery:` deck carries each chart's bindings, so the sent file focuses the same parts the Studio does. The export still draws no cursor or ink, so a sent `expressive` deck shows its focus but not its ink. The Guide adds about 49 KB to such an export (37 KB before).
