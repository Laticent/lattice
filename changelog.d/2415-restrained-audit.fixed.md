- The Guide walks a chart from the first thing its narration names, not from the chart's planned moment. `restrained` (two moments a slide) used to leave every earlier sentence about the chart dark: a line's series summary and first point, a dumbbell's first row and a heatmap's first cell showed no focus, which on a phone read as the chart doing nothing. A chart that earns a moment is still one moment of the budget.
- On a line chart, the point being read keeps its category label on the axis at full strength while the other categories fade, so the focus is visible at phone size, not only a few-pixel dot.
- A table slide narrated as "SMB — Q3: 7.8%; Q4: 9.4%" now focuses the SMB row. The Guide could not match that shape to the row, so table slides focused nothing.
