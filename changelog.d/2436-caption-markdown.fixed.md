- A caption written with Markdown (`<!-- caption: … **It costs more.** -->`, or a front-matter `captions:` entry) now shows and speaks its words without the markup. Present's caption band, the exported player, `lattice video` and the `.vtt` used to show the asterisks. Bold, italics, code spans, strikethrough and links are all read as their text, and a `*` or `_` that is not emphasis, such as `2 * 3` or `snake_case`, is left alone.
