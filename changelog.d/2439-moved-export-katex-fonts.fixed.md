- **A moved `--html`, `--fluid`, `--read` or `--player` export sets its math in KaTeX again.** The CLI page linked KaTeX's stylesheet by its `file://` path in the exporting machine's `node_modules`, so a copy opened anywhere else lost all 20 KaTeX faces and showed the math in Times — a small integral, bare delimiters. A deck that renders math now carries the sheet inline with its faces as `data:` URIs (the player keeps only the faces the deck paints, subset to the glyphs it uses), and a deck without math carries no KaTeX at all. The PDF exports are byte-identical for a standard install (checked on the math and KPI galleries, light and dark); where KaTeX is installed outside Lattice's own package (a hoisted install), the PDF now draws its math as vector text instead of leaving it in the photo layer.
