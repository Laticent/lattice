- **Security: the vendored Mermaid is now 12.3.0, so diagram labels go through DOMPurify 3.4.17.** Mermaid 11.17.2 bundled DOMPurify 3.4.12, which five published advisories still cover (GHSA-cg2g-xh64-v4q7 critical, GHSA-mrv2-3pqr-j5jr high, three low or moderate). The CLI export's render page moves to `@mermaid-js/mermaid-cli` 12.2.0's, and ZenUML to `@mermaid-js/mermaid-zenuml` 1.0.1. ZenUML still bundles DOMPurify 3.3.1, because no ZenUML release carries 3.4 yet.
- **Diagrams keep the drawing they had.** Mermaid 12 changed its own defaults: every diagram drew in the new `neo` look, flowcharts and state diagrams laid out with elk, nodes got a 120-unit minimum width, mind maps turned into top-down trees, and swimlanes would have lost their lanes. The engine now states `look: classic`, `layout: dagre`, `minNodeWidth: 0`, a radial mind-map layout and the swimlane layout, so the diagram gallery renders as it did on 11.17. An author's own `%%{init}%%` still overrides each of these.
- **Block diagrams draw by hand on a sketch slide.** Mermaid 12 draws `block` through rough.js, so `mode: sketch` and `_class: sketch` now reach a block diagram's shapes, as they already did for flowcharts, state, class, ER, mind map, requirement and C4.
- **Use-case diagrams take the deck's font.** Mermaid 12's `usecase` diagram has its own font keys; the engine sets them like the other families'.
- **A theme pinned inside a diagram section is honored.** Mermaid 12 reads `%%{init: {"flowchart": {"theme": "forest"}}}%%` as a pin, and the engine now stands its palette down for it, as it already did for a top-level `theme`.
