- **Trama gains a radial layout, `radialLayoutKernel()`**: one node in the center, the rest on one ring around it (`solveStar`) or on two (`twoRings`), joined by straight bands, with a label placer that puts every label where it touches no node, band, other label or stage edge, and reports what it cannot place. Like the rest of Trama it knows nothing of what it lays out; a chart passes what its marks mean as plain numbers. It needs no dagre and no browser, and closes over nothing, so it could also ship as browser source. The paint paths, collision tests and label placer are general; the two solvers are hub-spoke's, calibrated to its linter's envelope.
- **`hub-spoke` now lays out through Trama.** Every hub-spoke chart draws the same bytes as before: 3,636 outputs (the demo deck, the gallery and 600 seeded slides per stage, in the monospace and the sketch face) hash identical before and after. `hub-spoke.transform.js` keeps what the marks mean and the drawing, and shrinks from 1,336 lines to 811. `_chart-family/arrowhead.js` is gone; its triangle lives in the kernel.
