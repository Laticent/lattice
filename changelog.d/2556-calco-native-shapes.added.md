- **Editable `.odp` and `.pptx`: cards, tags and rules are native shapes now, grouped with their text.** `--editable` (and the Studio's editable export) used to lift only the words; every box stayed pixels in the slide picture. Calco's reader now also lifts each plain box (a solid fill, solid borders, round corners, one outer shadow) and draws it as a real shape. A tag carries its own word inside it, a border that differs from its card's outline becomes a line along its edge, and a card is grouped with everything inside it (`draw:g` in the `.odp`, `p:grpSp` in the `.pptx`), so dragging a card moves its tag, its rule and its text together and leaves no ghost behind. A box that would cover something the picture keeps above it (an icon, a chart, a `::before` tag, list bullets, unread text) stays in the picture whole, and so do gradients, textures, sketch strokes and elliptical corners. On card-tags, 41 shapes come out; on the 58-slide jargon gallery, 370; each `.pptx` validates against the OOXML schema and the Open XML SDK with zero errors.
- **The /calco page and the composed PDF's html-to-image camera now draw CSS counters, as the Studio does.** Their captures passed html-to-image no property list, so a slide numbered with `counter()` would have captured as 0. All three captures now take one list from `lib/core/capture-style-properties.mjs`. The /calco demo slides write their numbers as text, so their exports do not change.
