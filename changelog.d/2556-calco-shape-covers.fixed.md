- Editable office export: cards under a `stamp-veil` or `stamp-mark` state stamp now stay part of the slide picture, tinted and under the DRAFT band as the browser draws them; before, the export drew untinted native cards over the stamp. The same holds for anything a slide draws over its cards with its own `::before`/`::after`. A slide whose colors, decorations or shadows have a CSS transition no longer leaves a box in the slide picture under its native shape, or fades it back in after the export.
