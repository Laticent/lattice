- The Studio's PowerPoint export in WebKit, the engine behind Safari, now keeps the deck's pictures. WebKit decodes a slide's photos only after the slide is first drawn, and the export shipped that first draw, so slides lost their photos while the toast still said "PowerPoint ready." The capture now warms each photo until WebKit has decoded it, then draws the slide, waiting up to 5 seconds a slide; if a photo still has not arrived, the toast says the export could not load every image. The image set, the chart PNG, the editable Office export and the PDF fallback lanes share the capture and get the same fix. Chromium and Firefox exports are byte-for-byte unchanged. Checked in Playwright's WebKit build; real Safari is not yet checked.
