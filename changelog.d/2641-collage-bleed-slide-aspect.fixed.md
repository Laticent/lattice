- **Fixed: a `collage bleed` slide on a 4:3, 4:5 or wide deck no longer crops a third of every photo.** The packer assumed a 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16 slide from the deck's orientation, so a deck of another shape had its grid stretched to fit and cropped heavily. It now packs for the slide's real shape: the CLI reads the deck's `size:` and the live preview reads the section's own box. On the demo photos a 4:3 deck drops from 33% to 4% cropped, a 4:5 deck from 24% to 7%, and a 1560x720 phone-landscape deck from 21% to 10% on its worst set. 16:9, square and 9:16 decks render byte-identical. A bleed collage also now weighs empty space more heavily when it picks a layout, so it crops less: over random sets of 4 to 6 photos, mean crop falls from 9-11% to 3.5-4.7% on six canvases. The bleed docs state the real figures in place of "a few percent". New demo: `examples/collage-canvases.md`.
