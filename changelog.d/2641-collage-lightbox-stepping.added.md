- **Added: step between photos in the `collage` lightbox.** With a photo open, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight (or PageUp and PageDown), Home and End, a horizontal swipe, or the two new arrow buttons move to the collage's other photos in written order, with a "2 / 5" counter. It works on the Playground, the Studio and the exported HTML player. A deck with a collage gains the two input rules (`keyAction`, `swipeAction`) in its player script; a deck without one is unchanged to the byte.
