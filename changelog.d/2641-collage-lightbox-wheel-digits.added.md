- **Added: the collage lightbox steps with a mouse wheel and jumps with the number keys.** A decisive plain-wheel flick moves to the next or previous photo, with the deck's own wheel gate and cooldown, so one flick is one step. Ctrl or cmd plus wheel still zooms, and a trackpad pinch never steps. While a photo is zoomed the wheel does not step. The keys 1 to 6 jump to that photo while one is open; a number past the set is ignored, and with no photo open the digits stay the deck's. The Studio, Playground and exported HTML player all take both, from the shared transport kernel (`createWheelGate`, new `digitAction`). A collage deck's exported player grows by about 1.5 KB; other decks are byte-identical.
