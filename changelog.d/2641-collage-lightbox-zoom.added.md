- **Added: zoom the open `collage` photo.** A two-finger pinch, ctrl (or cmd) and the wheel, a trackpad pinch, the `+` `-` `0` keys or a double click zoom the photo from 100% to 500%; a one-finger or mouse drag pans it, and a percentage pill returns it to fit. It works on the Playground, the Studio and the exported HTML player, and uses the one shared zoom rule (`createZoomGesture`) the Studio preview already uses. A deck with a collage gains that rule in its player script; other decks are unchanged to the byte.
