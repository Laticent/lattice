- **Fixed: a mouse drag across a `collage` photo in the Studio preview no longer opens the photo it ends on.** The Studio opened a photo on any click, and a browser still fires a click when a drag ends, so on a `bleed` slide (where photos cover the slide) a drag could open one. The Studio now ignores a click whose pointer travelled more than 8 px, as the exported player already did; both read the number from `lib/core/collage-tap.mjs`.
