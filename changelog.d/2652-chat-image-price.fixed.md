- The Studio chat's budget gate now prices a picture by the model that reads it. It charged every picture a flat 800 tokens (an attached photo a flat 1,500), which was too low for most vision models at attachment size and about 10x too low for GPT-4o mini. A 3:2 picture at 512 px is 212 tokens on GPT-5 mini, 250 on Claude, 1,290 on Gemini 2.5 and 8,500 on GPT-4o mini; at 1568 px it is 258 to 36,835, and a square costs up to 1.5x a 3:2 one. The figures come from a measured table of 192 vision models (`tools/probe-picture-tokens.mjs`), a `~…-latest` alias is priced as the model it points at, and a model with its own listed image price is charged that too. A hard-stop cap now holds on every model measured (192 of the 212 vision models); the other 20 could not be measured and are charged the default.
- Gemini and other Google models no longer get prompt-cache markers. Measured through OpenRouter on the chat agent's real three-round turn, a marked request cost more than an unmarked one on every Google model tried (7% to 83% more on a cold turn, 1.4 to 4.2 times as much on a repeat turn) because Google caches implicitly. Anthropic is unchanged. The Workspace "Reuse the cached prompt" switch is now offered only for Anthropic models, the only ones a marker does anything for. The same marker was sent by the Coach's per-finding Fix; it now follows the same rule. A hard-stop cap now holds for every spending action, not only the chat: `cloudBudgetBlock` waits (at most 4 seconds, stop mode with a cap only) for the price catalog when it has not loaded, the catalog is fetched as soon as a key exists (at connect, at the OAuth return, at Undo after Disconnect), concurrent requests share one fetch, and a hung fetch is abandoned after 15 seconds. Before, the first chat turn on a fresh browser profile, and the Coach's Fix and the refine and generate actions on a profile that had not opened the model tab, had no price and ran past the cap.
