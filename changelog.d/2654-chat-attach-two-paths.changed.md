- The chat's attach controls are now two buttons in the message box: the **paperclip** opens your phone's or computer's own picker straight away (photos, videos, take one now, or files, each sent where it belongs), and a **Library button** next to it lets you tap saved photos, videos and files to attach them, with a check on what's attached. The one popover that sat on top of the phone's picker, with its search box and extra rows, is gone. Taking a clip you picked from the Library off a message leaves it in the Library; one you added from the device is removed again. The message box now gives the text its own full-width line, with the buttons on a bar underneath, and the two attach buttons are larger and further apart so a tap on one cannot land on the other.
