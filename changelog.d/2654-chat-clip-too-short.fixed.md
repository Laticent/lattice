- A video clip shorter than 1.5 seconds is now refused in the chat with a plain message ("Record at least 2 seconds"), in both the device picker and the Library picker. A video model reads about one frame a second, so a clip that short gave it nothing to look at, and it answered with an invented description or said it could not see the video.
- The Architect is now told, in your message, that a clip came with it and is shown to it there. An iPhone clip is sent under the `video/mov` type that OpenRouter's guide lists.
- The message that explains why a video cannot be attached is now easy to read: full-strength text with a warning mark instead of small faded red text, and it says what to do. A clip from the Library, whose length is known, gets the length to trim to, for example "This video is 16.4 MB and the limit is 15 MB. Trim it to about 7 seconds or less, then add it again."; a clip from the device that is over the size limit is refused before it is read, so it is told to trim to a few seconds.
