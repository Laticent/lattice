- **The Segno bakeoff's ratios hold still across runs.** `npm run parser:bakeoff:segno` now times a row's columns in alternating ~150 µs slices and reports each ratio as the median of the per-slice ratios, timed back to back. Five runs of unchanged code read the shipped dispatcher's every-span ratio at 1.32x to 1.40x (a spread of 0.08); the old harness divided two fastest rounds and spread by 0.20 (1.34x to 1.54x). The ns columns are now each column's median slice, not its fastest round.
