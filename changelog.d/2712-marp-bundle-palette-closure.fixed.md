- `export-marp` now ships every palette the deck's palette extends, all the way up its chain. A Marp bundle used to carry only the palette and its `-dark` companion, so a palette that `@import`s a parent rendered without its colors in Marp: `atelier-dark` shipped without `atelier`, and `a11y-deuteranopia` without `a11y-base` or `onyx`. Five example decks were affected. The Studio's export already walked the chain; the CLI now reads the same declared graph (`THEME_EDGES`).
