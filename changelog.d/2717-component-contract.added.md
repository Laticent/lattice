- A component contract runs in `npm test`: every folder under `lib/components/` must have its manifest and stylesheet, its shared sheets wired into the build, every variant drawn in its gallery and known to `lint:deck`, coherent `variantAxes` and `capacity`, a label in the card-row corpus, and `cards` declared if the corpus says it has a card row. `stats`, `actors` and `kpi` are recorded as debt with a reason.
- Component CSS may not add physical `left`/`right` declarations: 44 components carry 402 today (shorthands, positions and case included), recorded per component with a ceiling that may only fall, so the layouts can mirror under `dir="rtl"` later.
- `npm run conformance:render` renders every gallery slide (all 75 components, 858 slides) and reports overflow, axe violations, the card-row trigger's hit and miss rate against the hand-labeled corpus, and WCAG AA contrast in every shipped palette, light and dark. It is report-only and not part of `npm test`.
- The conformance debt ledger checks an entry's date against git: debt may be added only in the commit that adds its clause, a ceiling may only fall, and a deleted-and-re-added entry counts as added later. A shallow clone cannot answer and answers nothing.
