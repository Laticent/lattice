- The contrast checker reads gradient backdrops. It used to score text only against the nearest flat `background-color`, so `topic`'s dark tab labels on its light bottom band scored 1.02:1 (they now score 10.5:1), and any slide that paints a band, rail or scrim as a `linear-gradient` layer was scored against the wrong color. It evaluates each linear gradient at the text's own position (size, position, repeat, angle, hard stops, OKLab for `color-mix` stops) and composites it over the background color; the gates that share it kept their pinned counts. Radial, conic and `url()` backdrops are unchanged.
