- **A hand-written deck can no longer stall `--player` export, and a hand-written quote spelling no longer ships a local path.** The player's local-file inliner now reads tags the way a browser does, in one pass: a 1 MB deck of unclosed quotes (`<a "`) ran past five minutes and now takes tens of milliseconds, and `style="background:url(&#34;file:///…&#34;)"` (any numeric spelling of a quote) is baked in instead of leaving the absolute path in the shared file. A `<img <src=…>`, a duplicate `src`, a `<plaintext>` tail and a `<script><!--<script>` nest are no longer read as pictures. Decks the engine writes export byte-identical.
