- Slide-comment metadata has one vocabulary and one reader. A new always-on `meta` plugin (`lib/plugins/meta/`) declares every comment key with its type; the engine, the linter, the notes extractor, the Studio and the packaged slide facts read the key list from it instead of five hand-kept copies. `meta` cannot be switched off, by a deck or by a host setting. No syntax changes: `<!-- _class: … -->` renders exactly as before, checked against the engine over 478 committed decks.
- The notes extractor treats `lat-meta`, `lat-pane`, `lat-panes` and the `<!-- stress-slide -->` marker as tooling, never as speaker notes.
- New lint rules for a record-shaped comment in a place that cannot be read: `meta-opener-bare`, `meta-not-own-line`, `meta-in-html-block`, `meta-block-in-container`. `legacy-directive-comment` exists and is off until the old form is cut.
