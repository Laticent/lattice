- **Breaking:** a background image may no longer build a URL from another value: `image-set(var(--header) 1x)` (and `src()`, `image()`, `cross-fade()` beside `var()`, `env()` or `attr()`) is dropped. That route turned a `header:` string into a URL of any scheme (measured: Chromium fetched `http://127.0.0.1/…`). A plain gradient that reads a token (`linear-gradient(var(--accent), red)`) is unchanged.
- A slide's `_backgroundImage: url(…)`, an `![bg](…)` image (on an `image` slide and on every other slide) and the deck `logo:` now follow one scheme allowlist (`lib/core/url-scheme.js`): relative paths, `https:`, `data:image/…`, `sample:` and `asset:` render; `javascript:`, `file:`, `http:`, `data:text/…`, `blob:` and protocol-relative `//host/x` are dropped, and the render carries a `meta/url-scheme` diagnostic (an `image`-slide `![bg]` is dropped silently). Before, a hostile scheme reached a real `background-image` or `<img src>` unchecked. A Windows drive path (`C:\logo.png`) and `http://localhost` are refused too.
