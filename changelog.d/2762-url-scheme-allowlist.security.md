- A slide's `_backgroundImage: url(…)` and the deck `logo:` now follow one scheme allowlist (`lib/core/url-scheme.js`): relative paths, `https:`, `data:image/…`, `sample:` and `asset:` render; `javascript:`, `file:`, `http:`, `data:text/…`, `blob:` and protocol-relative `//host/x` are dropped, and the render carries a `meta/url-scheme` diagnostic. Before, a hostile scheme reached a real `background-image` or `<img src>` unchecked.
