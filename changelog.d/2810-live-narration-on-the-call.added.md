- **The presenter's narration reaches everyone on the call.** While the host presents to everyone and is on the call, the narration their Present reads goes out with their microphone, so everyone on the call hears the presenter's voice from the presenter's own playback, with no second speaker in the room. It still goes out when the presenter mutes their microphone, which then silences only their voice. Only the presenter's browser makes the narration.
- **A presentation shows the deck as it was when it started.** While presenting to everyone, the host's and the followers' Present keep the deck from the moment it started, so an edit made meanwhile cannot shift slides under the people following or add to the narration the presenter pays for. Edits show when the presentation ends.
