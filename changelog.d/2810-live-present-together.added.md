- **Present to everyone in a live session.** The host can choose "Present to everyone" in the Live panel's session menu. Everyone who is not typing opens Present on the host's slide and follows along as the host moves; someone who is typing gets a Join button instead of being pulled away. A Present the room opened ignores the keyboard until you click into it, so a keystroke meant for the editor cannot jump slides or go fullscreen. Moving on your own stops following, and "Rejoin" brings you back. When the host stops, everyone's Present closes. Someone who already has Present open is offered to join rather than moved, and someone who joins the session mid-presentation joins it where it is.
