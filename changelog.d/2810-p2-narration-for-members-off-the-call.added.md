- Present together now plays the presenter's narration for a member who is not on the call. When the presenter presents to everyone with Voice on, a member's Present asks for each sentence's clip by the hash of its text, and the presenter sends back what it already holds, compressed to mp3. The member's Voice button reads "Tap to listen" until they tap it (the tap that unlocks sound on an iPhone), and a listening member's Present starts each slide the presenter reaches without ever moving them. No member calls a voice engine or uses a key of their own, and a presenter's key is never spent for a request: the host serves only clips it has already made, only for slides near the presenter, one at a time, at most 4 requests a second per member, and a member checks each clip's mp3 header (bitrate, sample rate and length) before decoding it, and a member keeps at most 96 clips (24 MB) in memory, dropped when the presentation ends. A member on the call with the presenter still hears it there, once.
- `tools/live-narration-check.mjs` runs this on two real browsers (Chromium, Firefox or WebKit) against a stub voice endpoint and checks that the guest decodes non-silent audio from the presenter and never calls the voice endpoint.
