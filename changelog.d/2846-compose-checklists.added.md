- **Compose edits a checklist.** A slide whose list items lead with a state marker (`- [x] done`, and the other five: `[ ]` `[-]` `[!]` `[?]` `[/]`) used to lock read-only, which held 40 of the 96 locked slides in the shipped decks. The serializer now keeps a list item's leading marker as written, the same way it does in a table cell, and a click on the marker chip flips `[ ]` and `[x]`. `[X]` (GFM's checked box, not one of the six) still locks the slide.
