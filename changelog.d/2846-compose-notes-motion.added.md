- Compose edits a note in place. Opening a `say:`, `describe:` or plain note shows an **Edit** button beside Remove; it opens the words in a field, and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter or leaving the field writes them. Only the words change: the comment's opener, closer and `say:` or `describe:` prefix keep their bytes, and a note that is opened and left is never written. A result that would end the comment early, read as a directive (`_class: …`), switch channel, or be empty is refused with the reason, and the words stay in the field.
- The Insert menu gains **Motion**. It opens the Library on its Motion scenes; a pick there adds the scene as a slide after the current one, the way it always has from the Library.
