- Compose keeps a heading that spans two lines. A setext heading (`Title`, then `line two`, underlined with `===` or `---`) was dropped whole when its slide was edited, because a heading could not hold a line break; it now keeps both lines, its level and its break, at the top level and inside a quote or list. Where the two-line form would not read back as the same heading, Compose writes one `#` line with a space for the break. A heading that ends in an escaped `\#` keeps its escape.
