- Compose now shows what an outside change changed. When an AI edit, a rename, an Inspector edit or an undo rewrites text while you are not typing, the changed line fades in with a tint and an accent rule on its leading edge, the exact new words get a stronger tint a moment later, and both fade out slowly. Where words were only deleted, a thin accent mark stands where they were. With reduced motion the tint holds and goes without a fade. A change out of view shows a "N changes above" or "below" chip that scrolls to it on a tap; nothing scrolls on its own. The paint is decorations only: it never changes the deck's bytes or the undo history.
