- `lint:deck` now judges a one-block prose slide (`big-number`, `citation-card`, `collage`, `image`, `redline`, and their registers) at a venue on a square, tall or strip deck by the words that canvas holds, not only on a wide one: a tall `big-number` holds 3 words at `venue: hall`, not 12. The rows are `venueCapacity.prose.families`, measured by `tools/calibrate-venue-prose.js`, and `check:venue-conformance` renders each slot at its row to hold it true. Count budgets are still wide-only.
