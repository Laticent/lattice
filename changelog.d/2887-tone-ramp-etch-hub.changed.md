- **Under `tone`, a heatmap that prints its values uses the same ramp band as one that does not.** The top step against an empty cell rises from 3.10:1 to 4.19:1 at its worst on light and from 1.67:1 to 2.64:1 on dark, because each value already picks its own ink. Under `etch`, a hub-spoke hub is the neutral whisper with a 2× edge instead of the page's heading ink, so a blocked or at-risk disc is the loudest mark on the slide.
