- **Tone is one ladder of shades on every chart.** A text-bearing mark (tower share, matrix-grid cell, journey actor dot, key swatch) used a second, paler ladder, so category 2 was a different shade on a tower than on a pie (closest pair of tower bodies 0.017 OKLab against 0.072). It now takes the same eight steps, and its text picks black or white from its own body. Tone outlines every mark in one ink, since the shades already tell the categories apart: a tone chart is one color plus status.
- **Under `tone`, a heatmap that prints its values uses the same ramp band as one that does not.** The top step against an empty cell rises from 3.10:1 to 4.19:1 at its worst on light and from 1.67:1 to 2.64:1 on dark, because each value already picks its own ink.
- **Under `etch`, the heatmap and map ramps are wider** (the step times 0.82 instead of 0.4), so the top step beats an empty cell by about 2.5:1 at worst instead of 1.5 to 2.3:1 on dark. A hub-spoke hub is the neutral whisper with a 2× edge instead of the page's heading ink, so a blocked or at-risk disc is the loudest mark on the slide. Demo: `examples/chart-finishes.md`.
