- Browsing a deck with panes in the Studio is faster and no longer jumps. Each slide of such a deck used to load its own ~1.3 MB stylesheet, because pane rules were composed only for the components on the slide shown. The Studio's slide frame now asks for a sheet that twins every pane component, so all of a deck's slides share one (1,306 KB to 1,489 KB raw, 144 to 154 KB gzipped, for the panes example deck). Click-to-paint on the production build, median of 3 passes over the 8-slide panes deck: revisiting a slide 91 to 65 ms in Chromium and 275 to 94 ms on an emulated iPhone (WebKit); the first visit to each slide 260 to 176 ms and 381 to 259 ms; a deck without panes takes 58 ms. Only the Studio's live frame opts in: the CLI, the player and the Studio's exports keep the sheet for exactly their own panes.
- A slide no longer paints under the previous slide's stylesheet. Moving into the first pane slide put its table at the wrong height for about 120 ms (260 px, then 306 px) until the new sheet finished parsing. The restyle path now waits for the new sheet to load, then writes the frame style, author CSS and body together; a render that started in the meantime wins.
