- The editable PPTX export (`--editable`) no longer hides a focused column's text. A `_focus: col N` column's tint and ring become one picture that spans every row, and the column's header and last cell were drawn under it, so they vanished. Each focused cell's text is now drawn above the picture. The default picture PPTX and the PDF were never affected.
- A highlighted card (`_focus: item N` with `_focusStyle: highlight`) exports to the editable PPTX as a native card with a blue inside edge, grouped with its title and body text. It used to be a flat picture, because Calco could not draw an inset `outline`.
