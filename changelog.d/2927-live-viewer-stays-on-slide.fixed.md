- **Fixed: in a Live session, adding a slide no longer moves other people to the wrong slide.** When a
  collaborator added a slide just before the one you were watching, your view jumped to the new blank
  slide, and the person who added it was pulled back to the slide they added it after. Each slide now
  keeps its identity through adds, moves, duplicates and deletes, so everyone stays where they were and
  the author lands on the slide they added.
