- **Changed: the Studio has one labeled "New slide" button instead of five ways to add a slide.** It floats
  over the preview's bottom-right corner, like Gmail's Compose, on a desktop, a tablet and a phone. When
  the preview is collapsed, it moves into the editor's toolbar. On a phone the edit pane floats one too,
  and it steps aside while you type. The gallery it opens is titled "New slide" too. The `+` in the
  strip's icon row, the editor's "Add" button and the phone menu's "Add slide" row are gone. The command
  palette entry is now "New slide…", and Compose keeps its "Add slide below". Tapping **New slide** splits it
  into **Before** and **After**, so you choose where the slide goes first; the gallery then says where your pick
  lands, and the button says "Added before …" or "Added after …" for a moment, as a plain message rather than a
  button (tapping it does nothing), instead of a pop-up notice.
- **Changed: slide actions in the strip confirm on the strip.** Duplicate, Delete and Move say what they did just
  above the slide strip, with an Undo button there, instead of in a pop-up notice.
