- **Studio:** every slide in the strip (the slide pills and the slide map) has a menu with New slide before, Duplicate, Move earlier, Move later and Delete. Open it from the ⋯ beside the current slide, a right-click, Shift+F10, or a tap on the slide you are already on. The command palette has the same five actions, and an empty deck's strip offers "+ Add your first slide".
- **Studio:** the slide strip is a keyboard surface. Tab reaches the current slide, and the arrows, Home and End walk the deck with focus following. While a strip slide has keyboard focus, Ctrl+D duplicates it, Ctrl+Shift+Left and Right move it, Ctrl+Enter and Ctrl+Shift+Enter add a slide after or before it, and Delete removes it (⌘ instead of Ctrl on a Mac). A screen reader hears what each action did, and "?" lists every shortcut. A focused thumbnail no longer drops keyboard focus to the page when the map scrolls it away.
- **Studio:** drag a slide in the strip to move it. With a mouse, press and move; on a touch screen, hold the slide, then move it. A copy follows the pointer, a bar shows where it lands, the strip scrolls at its ends, and one Undo takes the whole move back. Holding a slide and letting go without moving opens its menu.
- **Studio:** the four slide buttons that led the strip (Duplicate, Delete, Move earlier, Move later) are gone; every one of them is in the slide menu, and moving is also a drag. The strip's slides now start at its edge.
