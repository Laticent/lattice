- **Export to Marp no longer hangs on a line of nested quote or list markers with no comment after it** (`> > > … text`, or `-    -    … text`). The bundle step read each line's container prefix with a pattern whose work grew exponentially with the markers: about 16 bullets followed by four spaces took 11 seconds, and 20 never finished. A one-pass reader in `lib/core/marp-bundle-scope.js` reads the same prefixes in linear time.
