- **Raw HTML, long blank runs or many unclosed tags no longer slow a slide's render with the square of their size, anywhere on the chart render path.** The engine read a directive's value, a chart's trailing zeros, cell and carousel code spans, the slide heading, a chart's trailing footer and caption, the section open tag and its classes, SVG open tags and their `<title>` / `<desc>`, the hidden-slide mark, the `data-form` / `data-frame` strip, the pagination marker, raw-block tags and markdown emphasis with patterns that re-read the slide from every unclosed opener or every blank. One-pass readers in `lib/core/directive-scalar.mjs`, `lib/core/tag-scan.js`, `lib/transformers/chart-frame.js`, `lib/core/svg-a11y-names.js`, `lib/core/chart-rows.js` and their callers read the same structure in linear time, and what a slide renders is unchanged. A `paginate:` value followed by 200,000 blanks rendered in 42 s and now takes 0.01 s; a heatmap cell holding 20,000 unclosed `<code>` went from 1.5 s to 0.06 s.
