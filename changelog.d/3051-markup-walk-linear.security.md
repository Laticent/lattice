- **Security: a deck of unclosed raw HTML no longer stalls the Studio.** The markup walk that reads a slide's structure scanned its whole open-element stack for each tag, so a pasted slide of 32,000 unclosed `<div>` lines took 11 s to walk and 16,000 lines took 3.4 s to render. The walk now keeps a count of open elements per name and skips a scan whose target is not open: 32,000 lines walk in 24 ms, and the 16,000-line render takes 56 ms. Inputs that open a scope boundary first (`<object>`, `<button>`, an SVG `<foreignObject>`) and then thousands of unclosed tags are still slow, about a second at 16,000 lines; that is recorded for a follow-up.
