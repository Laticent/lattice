- The prose binding gate (`test/unit/core/prose-binding.test.js`) no longer holds every deck's documents to the end of its walk. It kept ~2.0 GB live by the last deck, and under a 1.5 GB heap the file died out of heap, which the dot reporter prints as a bare `'test failed'`. Each deck is now its own test, with its own jsdom window closed when it ends and an event-loop turn after it, so the live heap stays under 0.2 GB (179–192 MB, the largest heap after a collection between decks) and a failure names its deck. Its last test now holds a floor for each kind of binding (heading, paragraph, item, row) instead of one total that headings alone cleared, and fails if any slide did not render.
- The scene binding gate (`test/unit/core/scene-binding.test.js`) had the same one-window walk. It now parses each deck, and each gallery, in its own window with an event-loop turn after it: the largest heap after a collection falls from 925 MB to 152 MB. Its floor of 1,000 bound sentences, a fifth of the real 4,719, is now a floor for each of its 23 units, and it fails if a narrated slide did not render.
