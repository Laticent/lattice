- The prose binding gate (`test/unit/core/prose-binding.test.js`) no longer holds every deck's documents to the end of its walk. It kept ~2.0 GB live by the last deck, and under a 1.5 GB heap the file died out of heap, which the dot reporter prints as a bare `'test failed'`. Each deck is now its own test, with its own jsdom window closed when it ends and an event-loop turn after it, so the live heap peaks near 0.3 GB and a failure names its deck.
