- **Chat on a spend-capped OpenRouter key** (a gift link's key, or any key with a limit set at OpenRouter): when the key cannot cover the reply ceiling, the Studio asks again, once, for what the key can afford, so roughly the last quarter of a $1 gift card is no longer refused. When the key is spent, the chat says the key has used up its spending limit and offers signing in with your own OpenRouter account, instead of "your account is out of credits" or "rejected the connection". A long provider message no longer runs out of the chat's notice box.
