- **Gift links:** `lib/core/gift-link.mjs` turns a spend-capped OpenRouter key into a link of the form `<site>/gift#v1.<key>.<check>` and back. The key rides after the `#`, so the site never receives it; a 24-bit check catches a damaged or mistyped link, and an optional PIN locks the key against someone who only photographed the card. One codec serves the Studio and the admin CLI.
- **`/gift` route:** a gift link opens a small welcome page that stores the key in this browser (sealed, through the one key writer), removes it from the address bar, asks for the PIN when the link is locked, asks before replacing a key already there, and opens the Studio with chat ready on OpenRouter. On a phone it offers **Send to my laptop** through the phone's own share sheet, or copies the link.
- **Workspace › AI:** the OpenRouter key field also takes a whole gift link.
