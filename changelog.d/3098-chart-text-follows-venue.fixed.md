- Chart text now grows with `venue:` instead of shrinking. A chart drew its labels in viewBox units, so when the venue grew the title and chrome around it, its box got shorter and its labels got smaller: a bar chart's category labels printed 17.4 px at laptop and 13.0 px at hall. The chart frame now hands each kernel a venue factor (`lib/transformers/chart-text-venue.js`). The kernel draws on a canvas that many times smaller, so its labels print larger in the same box, and the room it reserves for them grows with them. The factor reads the slide's own title, eyebrow, subtitle and source line, because those decide how much box the venue takes. The frame keeps a venue drawing only when it costs no label the old drawing printed (no name cut to "…", no label dropped, no smaller unit), and otherwise steps the factor down. Bar, funnel and heatmap now hold 8 arcminutes of x-height at the back row at every venue (10.1 / 15.1 / 20.1 / 26.8 px); a bar chart's labels print 32.5 px at hall. Waterfall, stacked-bar, ep-curve, line, histogram and scatter grow too, short of that floor where the full size would cut a name. No chart prints smaller than before at any venue or slide size. Portrait, square and 4:3 slides, charts in panes, and bullet, slope, risk-matrix, tower and quadrant keep their previous drawing; `followups.d/3098-p1-chart-text-venue-remaining-kinds.md` lists every kind still short. At laptop the charts are unchanged byte for byte.
