- An `image` slide no longer lifts an author's raw `.lattice-bg` panel out of a quote, a list item or a plugin's drawing: a quoted raw panel used to draw as the slide's photo and hide the slide's own. The slide's photo is decided by who wrote the panel: the image plugin's panel carries a marker an author cannot write, so it is lifted from anywhere; any other panel stays where the author put it, on the engine path and the DOM mirror alike. The `image` layout's panel rules now style only the section's own panel (`section.image > .lattice-bg`), and the adaptive layout measures only that one. An unclosed raw `<div>` written before the photo now holds it, as a browser nests it. No shipped deck changes.
