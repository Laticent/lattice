- An `image` slide no longer lifts a `.lattice-bg` panel out of a quote, a list item or a plugin's drawing: an author's raw panel inside a quote used to draw as the slide's photo and hide the slide's own. Only a panel at the slide's top level, or in a paragraph of its own text, is the slide's photo, on the engine path and the DOM mirror alike; the `image` layout's panel rules now style only those (`section.image > .lattice-bg`). No shipped deck changes.
