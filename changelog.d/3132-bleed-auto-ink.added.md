- A `bleed` slide picks its words' ink from its photo: white words and a darkening scrim over a dark photo, dark words and a lightening veil over a light one, read from the band under the heading (the middle of the photo for a layout that centers its words) as the slide crops the photo. A slide whose own or carried class names a color mode keeps it, and a `print` deck keeps print's ink. The CLI's PDF, picture and HTML exports decide before the page renders, the Studio and the Playground in the preview, and Export-to-Marp writes the choice into the slide's class. No shipped deck changes; `examples/bleed-auto-ink.md` shows each case.
