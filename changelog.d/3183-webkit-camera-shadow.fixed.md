- A Studio export from Safari or an iPhone (PDF, images, PowerPoint, LibreOffice) now draws every card's shadow whole. The slide camera drew each slide on a canvas scaled 2x, and WebKit then cut a negative-spread shadow short: the image layout's lifted card lost its shadow from the left edge to about halfway along, and the `mat`, `device` and `float` picture frames got a hard gray block down their right side. On an engine with that defect the camera now has the slide drawn at the export's own size. Chromium and Firefox exports are pixel-identical to before.
