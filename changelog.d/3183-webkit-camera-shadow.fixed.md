- A Studio export from Safari on an iPhone or iPad (PDF, images, PowerPoint, LibreOffice) now draws every card's shadow whole. The slide camera drew each slide on a canvas scaled 2x, and WebKit then cut a negative-spread shadow short or dropped it: the image layout's lifted card lost its shadow from the left edge (Mobile Safari on the iOS Simulator drew none of its bottom edge), and the `mat`, `device` and `float` picture frames got a hard gray block down their right side. On an engine with that defect the camera now has the slide drawn at the export's own size. Measured in Mobile Safari on the iPhone and iPad simulators and in Playwright's WebKit; Safari on a Mac shares the engine but was not run. Chromium and Firefox exports are pixel-identical to before.
