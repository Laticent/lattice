- The collage lightbox no longer zooms toward the photo's top-left corner when you press `+` or `-` before the photo has loaded. A zoom key pressed in that window now waits, and applies about the photo's center once it has opened. A pinch, `ctrl` + wheel or double click in that window is ignored, because there is no photo yet to aim at. The Studio, the Playground and the exported HTML player all get the fix.
