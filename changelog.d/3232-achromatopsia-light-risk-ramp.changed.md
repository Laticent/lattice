- `a11y-achromatopsia`'s light face now orders its status fills by lightness, so a reader who sees no color reads severity as one ramp. Done (the risk matrix's low band) is the lightest fill, at risk (medium) sits in the middle, blocked (high) is the darkest, and the critical band steps further still. Before, medium was the lightest band (luminance 0.098 against low's 0.039) and critical sat 1.09:1 under high. Now the steps are 1.59, 1.47 and 1.20:1. Every status mark on that face changes with it: gantt bars, checklist discs, kpi pills, state tiles. A palette may now set `--risk-critical-shade-keep`, the share of the fail fill critical keeps on the light face (82% by default); achromatopsia keeps 40%. Every other palette renders the same as before.
