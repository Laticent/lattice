- `carbone`'s chart color cycle now starts with its blue, and its electric green moves to second. A chart paints a mark with no status in the first color, and green there sat so close to the on-track fill (0.075 OKLab on the light face, 0.053 on the dark) that an unstatused state tile or gantt bar read as on track. Blue sits 0.224 and 0.233 away. Everything that reads carbone's first chart color turns blue with it: single-series bars and lines, a chart's first group, the default icon tint, the default avatar ground and the first stop of a title or closing spectrum bar. The sequential ramps, a heatmap and a map choropleth, keep their green under every finish through two new optional tokens, `--ramp-hue` and `--ramp-ink`, so their range is unchanged (6.53:1 heatmap top step on light). Every color in the cycle is the same; only the first two swapped.
