- Compose undo and redo now keep a deck byte-exact when two neighboring slide separators are spelled differently (`---` followed by one newline, then `---` followed by a blank line). Deleting the slide between them used to drop Compose to a full rebuild, and inserting one beside such a separator could emit the deck one newline short. The narrow resync now keeps every newline the edit carried across a separator, and it falls back to the whole-source path whenever its split cannot spell back the edit.
