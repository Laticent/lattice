- **A Webpage export that cannot get started now says so, and names the step.** Share → Webpage first loads the engine, the theme, the math fonts and the deck's Library photos, renders the deck and loads the exporter. If one of those steps does not finish within 60 seconds, the export stops and the toast names the step (for example, "the step “load the theme” did not finish within 60 s"). Before this, an export whose theme or exporter never arrived stayed on "Rendering the deck…" forever with no message.
