- **The agent kit is organized by task, and now carries the quality canon.** Four folders, each answering a question a person actually has: `authoring/` (writing a deck), `components/` (which layout, and how), `skills/` (creating a new artifact from blank), `reference/` (machine records for tools). `BOOTSTRAP.md` routes by task and costs each path in tokens.
- **`authoring/deck-canon.md` is new and is the most important thing in the kit.** It is `DECK_CANON` — the ~925-token statement of how a boardroom deck argues (one idea per slide, narrative arc, rhythm, restraint, bookends) plus the **18 traps the deck reviewer flags, each with its fix** — which the Studio chat sends itself on *every turn* and which the kit had never shipped. The component files say how to author a layout correctly; this says whether the deck is worth showing. The short on-device canon ships beside it for very tight contexts.
- **The seven `design/skills/` guides ship verbatim** under `skills/` — deck, theme, component, chart-component, finish, lens, speaker-notes. Each is self-contained: the 10/10 bar for that artifact, a mental model, a numbered recipe, a copy-paste contract, what good and bad look like, a ship checklist, and common mistakes. A test pins them byte-for-byte against `design/skills/`, because unlike everything else in the kit they are hand-written and could drift.
- **`reference/studio-prompts.md`** carries the three generator canons the product sends its own model — `THEME_CANON`, `COMPONENT_CANON` and `FINISH_SYSTEM` — with an explicit note that the matching `skills/` file is fuller and wins on disagreement. A 2026-07-19 investigation found these prompts had silently drifted from the shared design canon in two confirmed places, so shipping them without stating precedence would invite a reader to follow the drifted one.
- The publish path filter now covers `design/skills/**` and `docs/src/components/studio/architect.ts`; without them, editing a skill or the finish prompt would not have republished the kit.
- **Known gap, recorded rather than half-answered:** there is no motion/Anima skill. Anima has four decision records, a player bundle and shipped examples, but no `design/skills/` file — so the kit cannot teach it either. That is its own piece of authoring work.
