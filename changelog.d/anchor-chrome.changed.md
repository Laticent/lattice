- Anchor slides (title, divider, topic, closing) now keep or drop each chrome band by one rule: a band stays unless the slide already does its job. The title drops the running `header:`, because it names the deck. Every anchor keeps the `footer:`, so a "Confidential" or copyright line now shows on every slide that opens a section, including `divider numbered`, which used to hide both header and footer. Topic slides no longer show a page number, because their track says where you are. Bring a band back on one slide with `with-header` or `with-pagination`, or drop one with `no-footer`.
- A divider's section marker (`2 / 4` and the marks) sits where the deck's section rail sits on the slides after it: bottom-right by default, bottom-center under `progress-rail: center`, and as marks alone, upright on the right edge, under `edge`. Its count is set in the footer's own type, and the footer, the count, the marks and the page number now share one center line on every slide; the rail's marks are spaced in the marker's rhythm. Under a bottom-right logo the marker steps aside as the rail does, and `divider with-pagination` keeps the number clear of it. A `divider qr` at `venue: conference` or `hall` keeps its full code size again.
- The Studio's starter text for a new title, divider or closing slide no longer blanks the header and footer with `_header: ''` / `_footer: ''`.
- `npm run lint:deck` now warns `empty-section` (it was a suggestion) when a divider follows another divider, which leaves the first section empty and makes the first rail a reader sees say `2 / 4`.
- The section rail no longer moves within a deck. The footer band reserves a fixed column for the page number, so the rail holds still when the count reaches two digits and on a slide without a page number (it moved 10 px and 50 px), and a divider's marker ends on the same line.
- The footer, the page number and a divider's section marker share one baseline on every slide; a title's, divider's or closing's sat about 5 px lower than a content slide's. An image in `footer:` now sits inline at the height of a capital letter (it was drawn at its own size, a hundred pixels tall on a divider, or clipped away with the words after it), and a `<sup>` in the footer no longer moves the page number or the rail.
