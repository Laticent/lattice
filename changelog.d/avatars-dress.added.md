- **Avatars can wear earrings, head coverings and a choice of top.** Four new traits, all opt-in, so a
  face you already drew does not change: `earrings=stud|hoop|drop`, `headwear=hijab|turban|cap` with
  `headwear-color` (the nine `top` colors), and `clothing=crew|vneck|collar|turtleneck|hoodie|blazer`
  (`crew` is the default). A hijab and a turban hide all hair; a cap hides the fringe and any volume above
  it (a bun, an afro, curls); a hijab covers the ears, so it hides earrings. The Avatars guide, cheat sheet and `avatars.docs.md`
  list them, and `examples/avatars-dress.md` shows each.
