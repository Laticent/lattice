- **`backdrop:` register — restrain any finish, per deck or per slide.** `backdrop: 40 clear` dims whatever finish the deck wears to 40% and clears it from behind the content; `<!-- _class: backdrop-spot-tr -->` shows it in one window on one slide. Works with built-in and fabricated finishes, on every render path including the CLI, and overrides a finish's own baked strength or mask. Strength steps are 20/40/60/80/full; masks are `clear`, `open` and `spot-<tl|t|tr|l|c|r|bl|b|br>`.
