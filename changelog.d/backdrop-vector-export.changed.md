- A finish's backdrop no longer comes out blotchy or grainy in an exported PDF. The writer used to store the glow and texture behind each slide as a low-resolution JPEG picture, which broke a faint glow into blocks and color patches; it now draws them: the glow as a smooth picture computed from the finish itself, the grid, weave, rings and dots as sharp lines, and a clear panel or spotlight as a smooth fade. The PDF also shows the finish's richer on-screen look instead of the flatter export look, keeps a `tint-edge` rail that a finish used to hide, and is usually smaller (a dark `loom` deck went from 455 to 165 KB). `--chrome-pdf` is unchanged.
