- The editable `.odp` and `.pptx` now keep each slide's own background (its fill, border rules, washes and ghost numerals) apart from its content. Slides that draw the same background share one master page (`.odp`) or layout (`.pptx`), so it is not a movable object on any slide. A flat-color background is a plain fill, not a picture. A slide that blends or blurs what is behind it keeps its own opaque picture. `lattice` prints how many slides did.
- The editable export names what each text box is: the slide title is a real title placeholder (Outline view, slide sorter and screen readers list it), the page number is an auto-updating field, and a header, footer or page number that repeats across slides is drawn once on the layout. Eyebrows and subtitles are named "Eyebrow" and "Subtitle" in the selection pane.
- The slide's content picture is locked against moving and resizing in the editable `.odp` (LibreOffice and Collabora honor it) and marked locked in the `.pptx` (PowerPoint's Lock; LibreOffice ignores it).
