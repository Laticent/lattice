- The editable `.odp` and `.pptx` now draw more of a slide as native shapes instead of pixels in the picture: pills with their dot, ring and icon (grouped as one object inside their card), accent bars, counter text such as "STEP 01", linear gradients (a real gradient fill with its stops and transparency), and sized one-color rules.
- Two outer shadows draw as the wider one and a frosted box keeps its translucent fill; the export line counts both (`approximated: 2 stacked shadows, 1 frosted fill`).
- What cannot be a shape (a photo, a chart, an icon) is now a picture of its own in the editable export, placed in order among the shapes and grouped with its card, instead of one slide-sized picture under everything. A card that holds an icon is a real card with the icon above it. A large photo is a JPEG cut to the photo, so a photo deck is smaller; a small photo crop stays a lossless PNG. The export line reports how many pictures and how many slides kept one whole-slide picture.
- An inline code span or mark is now a text highlight in the editable export, so it moves, edits and wraps with its words (its rounded corners and border are not carried). A finish's decorative monogram or numeral no longer appears twice.
