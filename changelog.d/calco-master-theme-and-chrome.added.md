- The editable `.pptx` now carries the deck's own theme instead of Office's: the theme colors are the deck's palette (text, ground, heading, card fill, the accent and the first five chart series colors, read from the theme's tokens under the slide's color scheme), and the theme fonts are the deck's heading and body faces. PowerPoint's color picker, theme fonts and new text boxes now match the deck.
- A slide added in PowerPoint now looks like the deck: every layout has a title placeholder in the deck's title type, and a body placeholder in its body type (a subtitle placeholder on the title look). Layouts are named "Title", "Content", "Content 2", … instead of "Layout 1", …
- The running header, footer and page number are drawn once on the slide master, so they are edited in one place in Slide Master view and appear on new slides. A slide on a background no other slide uses now shares them too, and a layout whose slides show none (the title slide) hides them.
- The page number now reaches the master and layouts in Lattice's own style (letter-spaced, translucent). It used to stay a separate text box on every slide, so a slide added in PowerPoint had no number.
- Existing slides look the same as before in the `.pptx` and `.odp` (checked pixel for pixel in LibreOffice on four decks).
