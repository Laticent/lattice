- Editable `.pptx` and `.odp` exports now wrap text and honor card padding. A paragraph that wrapped in the browser is one wrapping paragraph (a multiline title is one title, not several), and a card's heading and body are one text body inside the card's own shape, with the card's padding as its insets. Code, `<br>` lines, titles' placeholders, header, footer and page number keep their pinned boxes. A card keeps the browser's height, so text in a wider face can run past its bottom edge. Measured in LibreOffice; PowerPoint and Google Slides are not yet verified.
