- `@callout{…}` now draws on nineteen charts, not only the heatmap: `bar`, `stacked-bar`, `line`, `histogram`, `ep-curve`, `waterfall`, `bullet`, `scatter`, `slope`, `tower`, `piechart`, `funnel`, `map`, `quadrant`, `radar`, `gantt`, `hub-spoke`, `risk-matrix` and `journey`. A note names its mark with `for=` (a category, a stage, a task, an axis, a region, a point), the mark is ringed and carries a numbered marker, and the words sit in a lane beside the chart, or under it as a group that sets side by side and wraps to a new row when the width runs out, whichever leaves the plot larger at the slide's shape. On a `journey` the task's chip is ringed and the notes read in a list under the board (the board is browser-measured CSS layout, so there is no side to choose), one list per stage on a portrait deck. A slide with no callout renders as before. The rule, the per-chart naming and the limits are in `lib/plugins/callout/callout.docs.md`.
- On these charts a callout's `detail="…"` joins its mark's hover card and the speaker note, the chart's description lists the notes (and any it could not draw), the narrator says each one after the chart, and `lint:deck` warns on a `for=` that names no mark, on a `key` legend (not drawn yet) and on a note past the sixth.
- Not yet: `flowchart` and `state-chart` (the browser lays them out), and `radar` inside a split pane.
