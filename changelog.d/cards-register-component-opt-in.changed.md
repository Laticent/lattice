- **Sketch `list principles` row rules no longer read as the heading rule.** Under `class: sketch` the hand-drawn row rules now use the same lighter ink (55%) and the same inset to the text column as the plain rule, so the full-strength masthead rule stays the one line that marks the page.
- **The `cards:` register now reaches the rest of the card catalog.** `decision`, `pricing`, `statute-stack`, `split-compare`, `team-profile`, `matrix-2x2`, `q-and-a grid`, the default (column) `cards-stack`, `split-panel`, `regulatory-update`, `inventory cards`, `agenda cards` and `citation-card` all follow `cards: center | top | spread | stretch` and `_class: cards-*`. **Changed default:** at wide, the default `cards-stack`, `decision`, `pricing` and `statute-stack` now size their cards to their text and center them, as `cards-grid` already did — `cards: stretch` (deck-wide) or `_class: cards-stretch` (one slide) restores the old fill exactly — and `regulatory-update` centers the rows it used to pack at the top (`cards: top` restores that). `matrix-2x2`, `q-and-a grid` and `split-compare` keep their filled layouts by default, and `split-panel` keeps its spread evidence list and its filled `proof` grid.
- A component manifest's `cards` field takes a new `byClass` override for variants that are different shapes (`split-panel`: `"byClass": { "proof": "stretch" }`).
- **Changed:** `inventory cards` spaces its tiles with the same card gutter as the other card grids (`--sp-md`, 24px at 1280 wide) instead of 8px; a grid with a pull-quote below it keeps the tight gutter it needs to fit.
- **Fixed:** a `banner-tag` card sized to its content (`compare-prose` since the register, and now `decision`) no longer spills its body text out under the banner.
