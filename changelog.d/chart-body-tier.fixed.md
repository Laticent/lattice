- **Fixed:** on a dark canvas a chart's legend swatch could paint a different color from the mark it labelled — a pie wedge and its own key entry came out `oklab(0.521…)` and `oklab(0.561…)`. The family's 82% "vivid solid" wash had no token, so the recipe was retyped at ten call sites and five of them drifted onto `var(--bg)` instead of `--chart-cat-base`. Invisible on light, where the two resolve to the same color; on dark, `--bg` is the theme's canvas and the mix neutralizes warm hues toward brown. `funnel`, `piechart`, `radar`, `map`, `quadrant` and `stacked-bar` all render truer hues on dark now; light and print are pixel-identical.
- **Added:** `--chart-cat-N-body` — the 82% level as a token, beside `-fill` and `-ink`, so the recipe has one definition and cannot drift again. Charts on the mark contract read it as `--mark-body`.
