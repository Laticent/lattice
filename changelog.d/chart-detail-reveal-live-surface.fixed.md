- **Fixed:** a chart's authored per-mark detail no longer flashes up and vanishes as you sweep the pointer onto a mark, on the Playground, the Studio preview or Present. Radix renders the popover inside its own positioning wrapper, which kept `pointer-events:auto` while the card inside it was `none`; the reveal fires mid-gesture, the wrapper is placed at that cursor point, and the continuing motion carries the pointer into it, so the preview frame gets `pointerleave` and the layer dismisses. A hover that arrives and stops was never affected, which is why this read as an intermittent glitch. Measured on the real Playground: 7 of 9 heatmap cells closed the instant they opened.
- **Fixed:** a heatmap cell's `` `# prose` `` annotation now reaches the hover card. The reveal layer builds the card from the `<li>`s in the mark's detail template; the cell annotation went in as bare text, so the card fell back to the compact value-only tooltip and the words were visible only in the PDF speaker note.
