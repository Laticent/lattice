- **Fixed:** a chart's authored per-mark detail no longer opens and immediately shuts on the Playground, the Studio preview or Present. Radix renders the popover inside its own positioning wrapper, which kept `pointer-events:auto` while the card inside it was `none` — so the wrapper landed on the mark the pointer was still inside, the preview frame got `pointerleave`, and the reveal layer read that as a dismiss. Measured on the real Playground: 7 of 9 heatmap cells closed the instant they opened.
- **Fixed:** a heatmap cell's `` `# prose` `` annotation now reaches the hover card. The reveal layer builds the card from the `<li>`s in the mark's detail template; the cell annotation went in as bare text, so the card fell back to the compact value-only tooltip and the words were visible only in the PDF speaker note.
- **Changed:** the reveal's lift and 3D tilt are off for a heatmap. A matrix reads by row and column, and both flourishes move the tiles out of alignment with the labels beside them — the same refusal the gantt already makes for its time axis. The active cell is still marked, by the dim on its neighbors and the heavier `.chart-mark-active` stroke.
