- **Added:** `chart-finish:` takes a second word, the intensity: `muted` (the default) or `vivid`, in either order (`chart-finish: pigment vivid`). Muted status fills are calm enough for a deck full of status charts; vivid is richer and stops short of neon. Both keep near-black text. A slide's `_class: chart-finish-vivid` changes only the intensity. The editor completes the second word, and `unknown-chart-finish` names a typo or a second word on the same axis.
