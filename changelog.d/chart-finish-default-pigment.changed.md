- **Breaking:** `chart-finish:` has no `off`, and a deck that names no finish now wears `pigment`. Every `<section>` carries a finish class, including a deck with no front matter and a chart inside a pane. `chart-finish: off` reports `unknown-chart-finish` and renders `pigment`; the per-slide `chart-finish-off` token is gone (a slide that wants the default writes `chart-finish-pigment`). The Studio's "As designed" option is gone, and choosing Pigment removes the key.
- **Fixed:** two things the default would otherwise have shipped to every chart deck. A heatmap's top step against its empty cell kept about half its contrast under pigment (3.54:1 fell to 1.92:1 light and 3.03:1 to 1.51:1 dark on indaco); pigment now carries the member's own span. A progress bar went near-black on a dark slide under pigment, because its readout sits on its own plate and the bar did not need the text level; it now wears the same body as any other mark.
- **Fixed:** a gantt task, milestone or key chip with no status, a state-chart tile with no status, and a progress bar with no status kept their own gradient beside flat sibling bars under a finish. They wear the neutral finish now.
- **Added:** `tools/chart-finish-reach.mjs`, a diagnostic that renders the chart gallery per theme and mode and names every mark a finish does not reach, every reached mark that keeps a gradient, and each finish's heatmap range against the unfinished paint. `--check` gates the default finish at 0.9×. Six charts (`flowchart`, `kanban`, `risk-matrix`, `slope`, `map`, `journey`) are listed as not reached yet.
- The Playground snapshot no longer keeps every finish rule on every slide: a bar slide's snapshot is 53K units, not 100K.
- **Fixed:** the ink a text-bearing mark picks (pills, status bodies, tower and hub-spoke values) switched on OKLCH lightness, which is not luminance for a saturated hue: a red body took black text at 4.31:1 where white gives 4.87:1, and a narrow band returned a near-invisible mid-grey (1.003:1 on `rgb(210 40 70)`). It switches on CIE luminance now and is never worse than 4.58:1, checked over 140,608 bodies and in Chromium 131, Firefox 142 and WebKit 26. The tower's own ink uses the same expression.
- **Fixed:** the `--player` export dropped every finish rule written `light-dark(...) !important`, because the flag landed inside the `var()` fallback and the engine discards the declaration. A finished deck showed the shipped paint under the finish's ink: tower share values read 2.45:1 on onyx against 8.33:1 in the plain HTML export. Both exports now agree on every theme.
- **Fixed:** under a finish the radar's scale ticks fell as low as 1.60:1 (they sit on the overlap of every series), and a state tile's index numeral fell from 5.95:1 to 4.31:1 on cuoio. Ticks take the heading ink, tone's polygon alpha runs 0.32 down to 0.104 (was 0.6 down to 0.24), and an unstated state tile's numeral picks its ink from its body. No text run on any chart, theme or mode is under its threshold where the unfinished paint cleared it, on all three finishes.
- **Added:** `tools/chart-finish-reach.mjs` also measures every text run (arm `RUNS`) and compares each finish with a frozen baseline in `test/fixtures/chart-finish-baseline.json`, so the reference survives a member converging its own paint.
- A runtime `<section>` nested inside a slide is content, not a slide, and is no longer stamped. A deck with a pigment heatmap keeps the theme's own cell inks, and `lint:deck` says `chart-finish-off` was retired instead of calling it unknown.
- **Changed:** under every finish, a chart mark that carries text (a gantt bar, a state or flowchart tile, a status pill, a tower share, a journey badge) wears the chart family's curated text tier, and its text is `--text-heading`: a tint on light, a deep fill on dark, at least 5.46:1 on every palette and 13:1 at the median. Pigment is solid on the marks without text (bars, wedges, dots, lines). A key follows the tier of the marks it keys.
- **Changed:** the finish now reaches flowchart nodes, risk-matrix bands, line and ep-curve strokes, radar and bubble edges, and the neutral hub-spoke and journey marks.
- **Changed:** every theme's chart colors are now curated SOLIDS: one flat color per category, status and the new neutral, with one text ink per mode (`--chart-on-solid`). Text on every solid clears 7:1 and every solid clears 3:1 against the slide, on every palette in both modes (`test/unit/palette/chart-solids.test.js`). `tools/curate-chart-solids.js` seeded the values by moving only lightness; 174 seeds changed, and ardesia's slots 3 and 4 and onyx's slot 6 were hand-tuned to keep adjacent categories apart.
- **Changed:** pigment paints every chart mark with its concept's flat solid, text on it or not, and every label on a mark reads `--chart-solid-ink`. No chart grades, mixes or filters its fill any more: the shared gradient wash (`--chart-fill-top-*`, `--palette-fill-*`) is retired, the pie, quadrant, radar, gantt, state-chart, progress, timeline and key-spine gradients are flat, journey's drop-shadow filters are gone, `--chart-cat-N-body` is the solid itself, and a mark's leading accent is its own solid. Unstated marks paint the new neutral solid. The status pill is a solid with the solid ink. The flowchart key names its chart, so its status swatches follow the nodes.
