- A roadmap's status discs and its key now take the one status level under the `etch` and `tone` finishes, as a gantt bar does. They stayed solid green, amber and red there, the loudest status marks in the class. `pigment`, the default, is unchanged.
- The Playground repaints your last slide on reload after you move the divider, even when the reload comes soon after the page last saved that slide. A second save within 500 ms used to be skipped even when the pane had changed size, so the stored slide kept the old pane width and the next load showed an empty pane until the engine rendered.
- Inline code on a kanban card clears 4.5:1 again under the `etch` and `tone` finishes. Their card wash on a light slide is now capped at 8%; at 12–14% the code fell to 4.07:1 on carbone, cuoio, indaco and laguna.
- Under `etch`, a slope chart no longer looks like `tone`: a status or signal line keeps its status color at etch's heavier line weight, and each dot takes etch's pale wash with an ink edge. A word cloud under `etch` keeps tone's steps, now a documented exception.
