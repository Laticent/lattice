- The chart finish register resolves to four named looks — **folio** (the newspaper page), **plate** (the bounded illustration plate), **relief** (material, depth across the mark) and **survey** (the surveyor's sheet) — specified as executable token blocks in `engineering/decisions/2026-09-07-chart-design-language/finishes.spec.js`. A finish is 16 lines of tokens with zero per-member rules, so a new chart inherits all four for free.
