- **Breaking (visual):** `funnel` and `timeline-list` now draw every mark in **one hue** instead of cycling six. A funnel's stage label sits beside its own band and a timeline dot has its title directly under it, so hue had no job to do — nothing to bind a mark to a name (no legend rail, no leader line) and nothing to separate (the marks do not touch). The single hue is slot 1, the family's single-series slot, which both components already declared as their default. Decks using either member re-render with a flat palette; no authoring change is needed.
- A funnel's a11y and print bands take **one texture** (`latt-a11y-chart-tex-1`) to match, so grayscale no longer claims six categories where color says one.
