- `tools/chart-hue-jobs.js` measures whether a chart member's categorical colour does a job — binding a mark to a name across a gap, or separating marks that position does not — and reports the verdict beside what the member actually spends.
- Chart design language: the G0 grouping test is replaced by that two-jobs test. G0 could not tell a `bar row` from a `funnel` (both name the mark in a gutter, one spends five hues and the other one), and the measurement flags `funnel` and `timeline-list` as spending categorical hue with no job, and `roadmap` and `state-chart` as having a job and under-using it.
