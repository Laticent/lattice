- `lint:deck` warns with `chart-list-split-quote` when a `>` written at the left margin sits between two items of a chart's list. The quote ends the list, so every row after it dropped out of the chart without a word. The fix follows what the chart reads: on a flowchart or state chart it indents the quote under the item above; on the charts whose hover detail is a nested bullet it rewrites the quote as that bullet.
- `lint:deck` warns with `chart-row-quote-unread` when a `>` is indented under a row of a chart that does not read one yet (bar, line, pie, funnel, waterfall, map, scatter, quadrant, radar, slope, stacked-bar, bullet). The quote hid the row's value, so the bar or wedge was dropped or misread. Its fix rewrites the quote as a nested bullet, which those charts show as the row's hover detail.
