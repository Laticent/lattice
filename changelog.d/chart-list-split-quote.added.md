- `lint:deck` warns with `chart-list-split-quote` when a `>` written at the left margin sits between two items of a chart's list. The quote ends the list, so every row after it dropped out of the chart without a word. On a chart that reads a row's quote (flowchart, state chart, and the 13 detail charts: bar, line, stacked bar, slope, bullet, funnel, pie, waterfall, map, scatter, quadrant, radar, word cloud) the fix indents a one-line quote under the item above; a quote of several lines is left for you to indent, and elsewhere the warning asks you to move the quote above or below the list.
- `lint:deck` warns with `chart-row-quote-unread` when a `>` sits under a radar series or a quadrant group. Those rows draw no mark of their own, so there is no card to show the quote in.
