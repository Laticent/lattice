- The chart census now measures OCCLUSION — whether a member's marks overlap each other — with a shape-accurate same-class test, which is what tells `radar` apart from `quadrant`: both paint a `<radialGradient>`, but radar's polygons layer and quadrant's zones tile. `chart-mark-separation.js` also composites `stop-opacity` instead of reading `stop-color` alone, so an ALPHA ramp is measured as what the reader sees rather than forced to a self-range of zero.
