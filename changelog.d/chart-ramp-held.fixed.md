- Chart design language: a ramp's fill is SCALED by a finish, not replaced and not held. An earlier revision held it, on the reasoning that a choropleth's own steps already sit at the discrimination floor — but holding it made `map` render identically under all three finishes, which is the defect a finish exists to avoid. A finish now sets the band's floor and span; the datum keeps its position inside it, so neighbouring steps keep their gap.
