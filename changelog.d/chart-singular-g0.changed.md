- Chart design language: a directly-labelled mark may own a hue *because* it is labelled, not despite it — the label is what keeps a per-mark palette from becoming confetti. The test is G0 (does the mark carry its own name, or share a label axis with its siblings?), the cap is G1 (past six singulars, collapse to one group rather than cycling), and RULE G settles a figure that has two categorical systems competing for one palette.
