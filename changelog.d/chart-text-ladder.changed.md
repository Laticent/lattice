- On a page read on a phone (portrait, square, story, mobile), chart text prints at one size per role in every chart kind. Key entries are 13pt on the phone; names on the drawing, values, area names and notes are 12pt; axis names, ticks, axis titles, status words and marker numerals are 11pt; and items in a roadmap, kanban or journey read at the deck's body size. Before, ticks and category names printed at 5 to 7pt. Inside the chart the text is in one face (the body face, with tabular figures). Landscape slides and charts in panes are unchanged.
- On those pages a word cloud sizes its words in five steps from 11pt to 40pt, scaled up together until the cloud fills its box, keeps its display face, and shows at most 25 words, reporting the rest. Radar small multiples run two to a row, and a risk-matrix cell keeps its markers at full size.
- On those pages a chart's layout follows its larger text: the drawing takes the shape of its box, axis titles and gutters grow with their labels, and a crowded category axis staggers its names over two rows instead of dropping or cutting them (waterfall, line, risk-matrix). A funnel's name and value columns widen, a quadrant places its names at the size they print, and a risk matrix's grid fills its box.
- On those pages bar, quadrant, risk-matrix, line, scatter, ep-curve, bullet, funnel, hub-spoke, piechart, tower, radar, map, stacked-bar, heatmap, histogram, waterfall and gantt are laid out by one shared chart form kernel: each draws once on a canvas the shape of its box, and its plot, axes, key and notes each take the room their text needs, measured with the theme's own glyph widths. A two-line category name sets whole, a row chart's names never shorten, and a grouped key sits under or beside the plot, whichever leaves it larger.
- A quadrant whose names cannot sit clear of their dots numbers its dots and lists the names in a key; a risk matrix's register runs in one to three columns or as a running line, and a crowded cell's markers shrink to the smallest disc that holds their 11pt numbers. Authored notes on these charts print at 12pt.
