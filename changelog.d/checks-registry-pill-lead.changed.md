- **Breaking:** verdict-grid and pricing no longer draw a `.badge` span. A criterion is a check inside a pill (`.lat-pill > .lat-pill-lead > .lat-state`) and a pricing feature a check then its words (`li > .lat-state`), so CSS that targets `.badge` there matches nothing. Restyle the check with the `checks-*` modifiers, or target `.lat-state`.
- **Breaking:** three modules moved into the checks plugin, and their old paths are gone: `lib/core/state-marks.js` is `lib/plugins/checks/shared/registry.js`, `lib/core/check-compose.js` is `lib/plugins/checks/shared/compose.js`, and `lib/core/leading-marker.js` (where `readLeadingMarker` lives) is `lib/plugins/checks/shared/leading.js`. `STATE_WORDS` is gone from `lib/core/segno-slots.js`, with the core `state` slot it spelled.
- The six check answers (`[x] [-] [!] [?] [ ] [/]`) now live in one registry table, `CHECKS` in `lib/plugins/checks/shared/registry.js`; the stylesheet, the layout word maps and the Studio's marker picker are pinned to it by test. No rendered output changes.
- A pill can hold content a component hands it: `pillHtml` / `pillElement` take an optional `lead`, drawn before the label with a `0.35em` gap, and `pillHtmlFrom` / `pillElementFrom` build a pill from parts. Narration reads a check that leads a pill in the layout's own words ("Residency: yes"), as it reads a badge.
- verdict-grid's criteria are now a check inside a pill, and pricing's features a check then the label on one row, composed by one kernel on both render paths; a long pricing feature wraps under its own text.
- **obligation-matrix `pills` now shows words:** each marker cell is a pill with its check and the key's word (Applies, Partial, Exempt…), and a label set renames the pills, the key and the narration together. Under `heat`, every row's cells keep their burden ground.
- Narration speaks an obligation-matrix label set's words, not the defaults, and a key entry says its word once.
- **New: the checks plugin and `@check{…}`.** The six answers are now owned by an embedded plugin, `checks`. The brackets stay as shortcuts; the full form names the answer, `` `@check{pass}` ``, by id (`pass warn fail unknown todo skip`) or spoken word (`yes partly no open na`), and with a label draws the check and its words as one pill: `` `@check{yes, label=Residency}` ``. The Studio autocompletes the six answers and `label=`; lint explains an answer it does not know, and counts a full-form criterion's words like its shortcut's.
- The ten chart statuses join the check registry as its second set, so a status and a check that share a drawing are held to it by test. No chart renders differently.
- The website's plugin pages gain Checks, and six catalog facts that a comma had silently cut short now read in full (for example "Loaded: Always. Part of every deck, so it has no off switch").
- `lint:deck` warns (`check-label-code`) when a `verdict-grid` or `pricing` criterion that starts with a check holds inline code: the label beside the check is plain text, so `` [x] `@check{fail}` text `` would show "@check{fail} text".
