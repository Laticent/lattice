- **A `closing index` see-also list that does not fit now splits** into an accent cover and its rows, instead of running past the footer. On a phone square, the closing slides of 40 galleries clipped this way. Only the `index` variant splits; any other closing stays one slide, however long. A manifest scopes a split to one variant with the new `split.onlyVariant` key.
- **A split cover's title is set in the display face**, like every other heading. It used to inherit the body face, so on a theme whose display and body faces differ (indaco: Playfair and Outfit) a slide's title read in two typefaces across its split run.
