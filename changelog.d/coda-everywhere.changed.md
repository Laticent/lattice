- **Breaking:** the title, closing, divider and topic slides take no key insight or note. A `> …` on one used to render as a Key Insight panel; it is no longer shown as one, and `lint:deck` flags it (`block-unsupported`). Move the takeaway to the content slide it sums up.
- The universal coda reaches 71 of 80 layouts. A trailing `> …` key insight, a below-note and an italic source line (the annotation) now also work on `stats`, `split-panel`, `split-compare`, `citation-card`, `quote` and every chart, `diagram` and `video`. `math`, `image` and the three QR posters keep their trailing paragraph; `quote`, `math`, `redline` and the posters keep their blockquote, and `inventory`, `policy-recommendation` and `split-compare` draw it as their own insight. `authoring.blocks` publishes the new set.
- A chart's caption is its coda's note: the one paragraph after the data still renders as the caption inside the figure, and a key insight before it stays the slide's panel. `diagram` and `video` caption the same way, under the figure.
- On `quote`, the first paragraph after the quotation is still the attribution; a second paragraph is now the note.
- `split-panel` and `split-compare` take `panel-narrow` and `panel-wide`, which scale each variant's own panel width (0.8× and 1.3×, at most 62%). With neither, widths are unchanged.
- In the copy-and-go Marp kit, the VS Code preview now says across the top when Lattice's runtime is not running (the preview's default *Strict* security), instead of showing raw slides with no explanation. The kit's README and `Sample-Deck.md` now say which slides need the runtime, and teach the settings block (`<script type="application/lattice-settings">`) for deck-wide settings such as `claim:` and `class: dark`, which Marp drops from the front matter.
- Fixed: a `video` slide with a key insight or a note no longer pushes its card off the slide (its manifest declared the wrong dock).
- Fixed: in a deck Marp renders, the last slide lost its key insight and note. The kit's `<script>` and `<link>` lines at the end of a deck land inside the last slide, after its closing beats, and the coda stopped at them.
- Fixed: a `journey` with a key insight or a caption no longer pushes its mood key off the slide. Its actor key and mood key now share one row at the foot of the board.
- When a slide clips, the export now names the smallest `claim` that makes it fit, checked by rendering the slide again: `claim-quiet`, then `claim-hero`, or one deck-wide `claim: quiet` when three or more slides clip and it fits them all (a deck-wide `claim: hero` is never suggested: it drops the header and footer on every slide). Also `npm run fit:grace -- deck.md` and `lint:deck --fit`. The export skips it under CI (the `CI` environment variable); `--no-grace` or `LATTICE_FIT_GRACE=off` skip it anywhere.
- `lint:deck` judges a slide that carries a claim by what the layout holds under it: thirteen list and card layouts (`checklist`, `table`, `agenda`, `cards-grid` and others) hold more items under `claim-quiet` or `claim-hero`, measured by rendering. A slide past the bare budget is told the smallest claim that fits it ("Or add `claim-quiet`, which fits 10.").
- On `stats`, the note keeps the row's centering (or `headline: left`), and on a split page it keeps its compact size, now that the coda draws it.
- On `big-number` and `quote`, the below-note and the source line center under the number or the quotation, as they do on `stats`.
- `video` with a key insight AND a caption: the poster gets shorter to leave the caption its room, instead of running over it (`video` and `video companion`). `qr` and `gallery` need a claim for both beats, and the export names it.
- `lattice … --no-grace` skips the overflow suggestion and its re-render. The suggestion now only answers a slide the export itself reported, keeps a layout set by a running `class:`, and renders with the deck's own images and stylesheet.
- `lint:deck` warns again when `insight-*` sits on a `citation-card` whose only blockquote is the cited text, and reads a deck's `claim:` even with a trailing comment or as `class: claim-hero`.
- `split-panel pullquote` keeps its quotation in the dark panel wherever the author writes it, and the line after it as the cite; a later `> …` is the key insight and a later paragraph the note. A manifest can now declare per-variant coda anatomy (`coda.variants`).
- On a `topic` slide, a line of text after the lede (a `> …` or a second paragraph) and a stray `#` heading are now readable on the dark band; they rendered dark on dark.
- The overflow suggestion now renders with the export's own `--size`, `--packages`, plugin switches, `--no-split` and `--css`, reads a deck saved with a byte-order mark, ignores directives shown inside a code fence, names the right slide when the deck hides some, and suggests no claim on a slide marked `claim-framed`.
- `lint:deck` warns (`quote-note`) when a `quote`'s attribution runs onto a second line that reads as a role: that line now renders as the slide's note. Put the role on the attribution line.
