- The universal coda reaches 75 of 80 layouts. A trailing `> …` key insight, a below-note and an italic source line (the annotation) now also work on the title, closing, divider and topic slides, `stats`, `split-panel`, `split-compare`, `citation-card`, `quote` and every chart, `diagram` and `video`. Only `math`, `image` and the three QR posters keep their trailing paragraph; `quote`, `math`, `redline` and the posters keep their blockquote, and `inventory`, `policy-recommendation` and `split-compare` draw it as their own insight. `authoring.blocks` publishes the new set.
- A chart's caption is its coda's note: the one paragraph after the data still renders as the caption inside the figure, and a key insight before it stays the slide's panel. `diagram` and `video` caption the same way, under the figure.
- On `quote`, the first paragraph after the quotation is still the attribution; a second paragraph is now the note.
- `split-panel` and `split-compare` take `panel-narrow` and `panel-wide`, which scale each variant's own panel width (0.8× and 1.3×, at most 62%). With neither, widths are unchanged.
- In the copy-and-go Marp kit, the VS Code preview now says across the top when Lattice's runtime is not running (the preview's default *Strict* security), instead of showing raw slides with no explanation. The kit's README and `Sample-Deck.md` now say which slides need the runtime, and teach the settings block (`<script type="application/lattice-settings">`) for deck-wide settings such as `claim:` and `class: dark`, which Marp drops from the front matter.
- Fixed: a `video` slide with a key insight or a note no longer pushes its card off the slide (its manifest declared the wrong dock).
- Fixed: in a deck Marp renders, the last slide lost its key insight and note. The kit's `<script>` and `<link>` lines at the end of a deck land inside the last slide, after its closing beats, and the coda stopped at them.
