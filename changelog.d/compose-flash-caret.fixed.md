- Compose: a click into a slide now keeps its caret, so the words you type next go where you clicked. Two cases sent them elsewhere, mid-paragraph in another slide:
  - **After an undo or redo.** Clicking another slide and typing within about three seconds sent the typing back to the slide you came from. The undo's highlight cleared itself with a change that ignored a caret move the editor had not read yet, and put the old caret back. The highlight now reads the caret first. A Markdown paste that finishes after a click gets the same fix, so it lands where the caret is now.
  - **On the first click after Compose opens, on a busy page (seen in Safari's engine).** The editor library put its own caret back before the browser reported the click, so the first words landed where the caret was when Compose opened, often the top of the deck. A click that focuses Compose now has its caret read first.
