- **Compose names each slide's layout and changes it from the slide bar.** The pill's first button is the slide's component (`cards-grid ▾`), and every other slide shows the same button quietly on its line, so the column of slides reads as a list of layouts. The menu shows a live preview of the slide, its looks, and the layouts its manifest names as alternatives with when to use each. Picks apply at once; closing keeps them and offers one Undo. A layout that cannot read the slide's text says so and asks before replacing it.
- **Changed:** Undo and Redo moved from each slide's pill in Compose to the editor's edit bar, beside Compose / Source, Reshape and Fix all (on a phone they stay in the header). They act on the whole deck. The pill now holds the slide's layout, Add, Insert and Settings, then the styles of the block under the caret; on a narrow pane the styles take a second row.
- **Find and replace** in the editor's edit bar, for Compose and Source: counts matches, steps through them, replaces one or all (one undo step), with match case. ⌘F opens it on the selected text; on a phone it is in the drawer. In Compose, a slide that can only be edited in Source is left out.
- **Changed:** the edit bar never runs past the editor pane. As the pane narrows, Version history and Slide settings, then Reshape and Fix all, move into a new More (⋯) menu before the Compose and Source words drop to icons; Undo and Redo always stay.
- **Fixed:** changing a slide's layout, or placing a picture on it, no longer drops other settings that share the `_class` comment (`<!-- _class: stats⏎_paginate: false -->` lost `_paginate`). Compose now leaves such a slide's class to Source and says so.
- **Fixed:** `cards-grid three` and `four` are one pick-one group, so choosing one replaces the other instead of writing `cards-grid three four` (in Compose's layout menu and in Slide settings' Reshape).
