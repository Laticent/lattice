- Compose edits links by pointing. Select words and press **Link** in the selection bar, or choose **Insert → Link**, to give them a web or email address. Click a link to see its address, change it, open it in a new tab, or remove it. Compose refuses an address a slide cannot carry, such as a `javascript:` or `data:` address, and says why. It writes an address the way the engine reads it back, and adds the scheme an author leaves off: `https://` for `example.com`, `mailto:` for `ada@example.com`, `http://` for `localhost:3000`.
- A click on a checklist item's state chip now opens all six answers (Yes, Partly, No, Unknown, Open, Does not apply). It used to switch only between done and open, and one click on a `[!]`, `[?]`, `[-]` or `[/]` item silently turned it into `[x]`.
- The Insert menu adds **Check** (`@check{pass}`, with a selected word as its label) and each inline kind an installed plugin declares with a slot, started from that slot. Compose could already edit both, but adding one meant typing it.
