- Compose draws a pill as the slide draws it, in the deck theme's own colors, as one object with no `{SHIPPED, c2}` text and no caret inside it. Tap or click it to change its label, shape, color and size from one editor (a popover on desktop and tablet, a sheet on the phone), so authors no longer type the grammar. Each pick rewrites only the word that was changed (a pill written `{BETA, c4, tag}` keeps its order and spelling, and every other pill is untouched), and opening a deck never rewrites it. "Done" puts the cursor after the pill; "Edit as text" turns it back into text, and it is drawn again when the cursor leaves. The "+ Pill" button on the slide bar inserts a new pill and opens its editor. A pill stays as text on a slide edited in Markdown, and nothing is drawn in a deck set to `inline-code: literal`.
- The slide bar has Undo and Redo buttons for the whole deck, because a phone has no undo gesture that sees Compose's history. On a narrow pane the bar wraps to two rows (history and the insert doors, then the Format group) instead of covering the slide's end caps.
- `editPill` in `lib/core/inline-pills.js` is the matching writer: it changes one word of a pill using Segno's own item offsets, keeps the author's casing and order for every word it does not touch, and returns null (writes nothing) unless the pill reads back as the old fields plus the requested change. A property test runs it over every readable pill in the shipped decks and galleries.
