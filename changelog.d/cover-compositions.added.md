- **Cover compositions.** The `cover:` register takes a second word that sets how the heading block sits on the front and back cover: `masthead` (a heavy rule over the title), `ledger` (small capitals over a double rule), `column` (an accent column at the right edge, clear of the header and footer), `label` (a gallery wall label, low on the left), `stage` (a stage-size centered title), `frame` (the title in a keyline) and `poster` (a giant title set left). Write it with or without a surface, in either order (`cover: light column`). Each is also a `title` and `closing` variant (`_class: title cover-poster`), and `cover-plain` keeps one slide centered. Every composition reads on both surfaces in both color modes. `plain`, the default, stamps nothing, so existing decks render as before. `lint:deck` warns when a `stage` or `poster` heading runs past 60 characters (`cover-heading-long`). See `lib/base/base.registers.docs.md` § cover and `examples/cover-compositions.md`.
- **`closing qr` and `divider qr` follow `headline:` for the eyebrow too.** On the wide QR layout the eyebrow stayed centered while a left-set heading (`headline: left`, or a left cover composition) moved left; the heading column now follows the same alignment. Unset, it stays centered.
