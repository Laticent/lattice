- **Breaking:** **Studio crash recording is now off by default and must be turned on** in Workspace → General → Crash reports. It previously recorded unconditionally. Nothing is recorded until you enable it — no session record, and no `console.error` capture — so a crash before you turn it on leaves no trail. Reports already saved stay readable and clearable with the switch off, and turning it off mid-session also drops the partial record of the session in progress.
