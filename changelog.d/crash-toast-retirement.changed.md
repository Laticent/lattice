- **Studio: the crash toast is gone.** Returning to a Studio tab the browser had unloaded while it sat in the background announced "The Studio stopped unexpectedly" — a crash notice where nothing had crashed. From inside a page an ordinary background unload and a dead renderer are the same observation, so that alarm was wrong most of the times it fired. Recording is unchanged and still unconditional; the report is now reached at **Workspace → General → Crash reports** and nothing interrupts you.
- **Studio: crash reports now capture `console.error`.** `window.onerror` only sees exceptions nobody caught, so a session that logged a stack trace seconds before it died used to report no errors at all. Console errors are recorded with their stacks and printf-style format strings substituted, and are labeled as errors the page survived. Console output itself is untouched.
- **Studio: crash reports lead with what failed.** Errors and files that failed to load come first; memory, freezes and tab identity follow. A session that ended while its tab was in the background now says so — "The Studio stopped while the tab was in the background", with "nothing to do" as its next step — instead of "stopped unexpectedly", and a tab the browser froze and never resumed is reported as an unload rather than a stop.
