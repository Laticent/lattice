- **Dark mode no longer repaints a frame that paints its own canvas.** `section.dark` set the deck-wide canvas at the same `(0,1,1)` specificity as a frame's own rule, and component stylesheets are bundled before `base.modifiers.css` — so under `color-mode: dark` the deck ground won and the frame lost the surface it drew for itself. Nine frames are now exempt: the four anchors (`title`, `divider`, `closing`, `topic`) and the five accent covers the splitter emits (`decision-cover`, `compare-code-cover`, `compare-split-cover`, `list-tabular-cover`, `split-panel-cover`).
- **The accent covers were an accessibility defect, not only a cosmetic one.** A cover keeps `color: var(--on-accent)` — dark navy under dark — while losing its accent ground, so it rendered dark navy on dark navy. Measured in Chromium on indaco: `decision-cover` and `split-panel-cover` both went from **1.52:1 to 6.10:1**.
- **`divider` gets its spectrum rail back** — `background-size` was collapsing from `3.75px 100%` to the deck-wide `100% 1px` hairline, and it had been shipping that way in `divider.gallery.dark.pdf`.
- A new gate, `test/unit/palette/dark-canvas-ownership.test.js`, reads every component stylesheet, finds the frames that paint a non-`--bg` section canvas, and fails if that set and the selector disagree in either direction, so the exemption list cannot rot.
