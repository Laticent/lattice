- **Deck presets.** A new `preset:` front-matter key picks a named look — `classic` (the default), `editorial`, `brand` or `minimal` — that sets the brand bar, card rails, trim, heading rule, eyebrow, headline alignment, card lift and corners at once. Any of those keys you write still wins over the preset. The linter flags an unknown name (`unknown-preset`).
- **Basic and Advanced settings in the Studio.** The deck and slide settings panels open on a short Basic list: theme, preset, color mode, size, page numbers and logo for the deck; canvas, type scale, clean slide and the speaker note for a slide. Advanced shows every setting. Search always covers every setting. The deck panel shows how many settings differ from the preset and offers a Reset.
- The "More look settings", "More accent settings" and "Developer" drawers are gone; their rows now sit under plain headings in Advanced.
