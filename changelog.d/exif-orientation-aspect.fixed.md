- A JPEG saved with an EXIF Orientation of 5 to 8 (a phone photo held upright) is now measured at build time as the shape it is painted, not the shape of its stored pixels. Before, the `image` layout picked its treatment for a landscape shape while the browser drew the photo upright, so the PDF and the live preview disagreed. Decks with rotated phone JPEGs may pick a different `image` treatment on the next build. No JPEG committed to this repository carries a rotation, so no gallery slide changes.
