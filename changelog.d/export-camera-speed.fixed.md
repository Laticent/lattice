- A Studio export of a photo-heavy deck was about twice as slow as before the shared slide camera, and could run a phone out of memory. The camera now leaves out the `@font-face` rules for families a slide does not draw with (79 rules and 1.7 MB of base64 went into every slide's SVG), swaps picture addresses in one pass instead of one per address, and lets the browser write the SVG's base64. The shared PNG encoder reads and compresses 64 rows at a time instead of holding three whole-picture copies; its files are byte-identical. A 52-slide 4K deck's PDF export went from 112 s to 65 s in Chromium (`main`: 51 s, with a 33 MB file; this one is 6 MB).
