- A Studio export of a photo-heavy deck was about twice as slow as before the shared slide camera, and could run a phone out of memory. A 52-slide 4K deck's PDF now exports in 52.7 s in Chromium, from 112 s (`main`: 51.9 s, with a 33 MB file; this one is 6 MB). What changed: the camera leaves out the `@font-face` rules for families a slide does not draw with, and the page's copy of a face the host already supplies (79 rules and 1.7 MB of base64 went into every slide's SVG); it swaps picture addresses in one pass; the shared PNG encoder reads and compresses 64 rows at a time instead of holding three whole-picture copies (its files are byte-identical); and the Studio's PDF writes PNGs in workers while the next slide is captured. Captures still take turns.
- The Studio's Share → PDF test for the 4K Resolution choice (`pdf-shared-writer.spec.ts`) now uses the dropdown that replaced the radio buttons.
