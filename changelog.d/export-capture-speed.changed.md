- **Exports and live previews lay slides out faster.** The preview runtime measured each slide's size right after resizing the one before it, so a 40-slide export forced the browser to lay the whole document out 40 times in a row; it now measures every slide first and then resizes them. The PDF export also reuses its font-face filtering across slides instead of redoing it per slide. Exported PDF and PowerPoint files are unchanged; the HTML and player exports carry the updated runtime.
