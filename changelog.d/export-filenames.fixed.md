- Studio exports keep the deck's name on iPhone and iPad. Firefox and other iOS browsers saved every export as an internal ID like `76f752a8-….pdf`, because Firefox for iOS names a page's download after its link and ignores the name the page asks for. On iOS every browser — Safari included, for one native behavior — now finishes an export with a single **Save** message that opens the share sheet with the named file; **Save to Files** keeps the name. The message goes away when you switch formats or close Share.
- Every Studio download on a computer now carries the deck's name on the download link itself, not only in the `download` hint, so desktop Firefox keeps it too when the hint is dropped, and the Print panel's 2-up / 4-up / Notes PDF tab shows and saves the deck's name instead of an ID and `document.pdf`.
- On a phone, the Share sheet and the other bottom sheets stay full height unless you are typing into one of their text fields. The Share sheet could come up half height over the Studio on an iPhone after a Webpage export.
