- **PDF, the offline player, PowerPoint and LibreOffice now shrink big pictures by default.** In the PDF and the player, a photo or screenshot over 150 KB is capped to 2560 px on its long edge and re-encoded as JPEG (quality 88), but only when that saves at least 30% (a format change) or 10% (a resize). A file that does not win, a transparent PNG, a GIF and an SVG are embedded exactly as before. On a 52-slide deck of 21 PNG screenshots (23.9 MB), the PDF went from 33.5 MB to 6.0 MB and the player from 66.6 MB to 12.6 MB; a deck of photos that were already tight JPEGs came out the same size.
- **You decide, per deck or per export.** `images: original` in the front matter, `--images original` on the command line, or **Original quality** in the Studio's Pictures control embeds every picture as added and keeps PowerPoint and LibreOffice slides as lossless PNG (13.7 MB becomes 26.3 MB on that deck). `images-quality: N` or `--images-quality N` sets the JPEG quality, 1 to 100. The Studio and the command line read the same keys and run the same plan (`lib/core/image-policy.js`) with Chromium's JPEG encoder.
