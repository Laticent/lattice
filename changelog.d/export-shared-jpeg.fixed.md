- **Exports write their JPEGs with one encoder of our own, so a picture's size no longer depends on the browser.** The PDF photo layer, picture PowerPoint and LibreOffice files, image sets, cropped pictures in editable Office files and recompressed author photos all used the browser's own JPEG encoder, which differs by engine: on one 52-slide deck's pictures Firefox's wrote a third more than ours, and the deck's picture PowerPoint came out 43.3 MB from an iPhone where Chromium wrote 12.5 MB: at the same quality, Safari's own encoder writes 1.5 to 2.2 times the bytes (measured in an iOS Simulator). The PowerPoint export's main-thread fallback also wrote every slide as a lossless PNG; it now writes the same pictures as the worker. `lib/core/jpeg-encode.mjs` writes the same bytes from the same pixels in Chromium, Firefox, WebKit and the command line, within about 1% of libjpeg's size, in the Studio's worker pool.
