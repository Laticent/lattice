- **Markdown, Marp and re-openable PDF/PowerPoint exports now carry the Library photos a deck names.** A Markdown export of a deck with photos downloads as a `.zip` (the `.md` beside `photos/<name>.<ext>`, references rewritten to those paths); a deck with none is still a bare `.md`. The Marp bundle puts the photos in a `photos/` folder. The `.lattice` inside a re-openable PDF or PowerPoint carries them too, up to 32 MB of photos (smaller than the standalone `.lattice`'s 128 MB so the file stays under the size the importer opens), and the importer reads that larger embedded file. Photos over the cap or missing from the Library are named in the toast.
