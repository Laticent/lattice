- **`fit: pack`: the measured split, on every surface.** A deck with `fit: pack` in its front matter keeps a slide whole when it fits its box and venue, even past its component's item count, and packs an overflowing slide's items onto as few pages as fit by measured height, instead of one a page. The CLI export, the Studio's live preview, the Playground and the Studio's webpage export all split it the same way: each browser surface measures the deck in a hidden frame built as its own preview frame is, then runs the same plan as the CLI (`lib/core/fit-measure.js`). In the live preview a slide keeps its pages while you type: it goes back to one page only when it fits with 30% of its room spare, keeps its items on their pages while they still fit, keeps its page count when the items no longer fit their old pages but some packing still can, and undo restores the split a slide had for that text. Fresh pages are packed to 90% of their room so a small edit has space to grow.
- **`--fit-pack greedy|balanced|knuth-plass` and `--fit-memory FILE`.** `--fit-pack` turns the measured split on for any deck and picks the packer (`fit: pack` uses Knuth–Plass); `--fit-memory` reads and writes the split decisions between renders, as the live preview keeps them. On boxes that split (square, portrait, story, mobile), the CLI renders the deck whole, keeps a slide whole when it fits, and packs an overflowing slide's list items or table rows onto as few pages as fit by measured height. Cover, closing page and carousel chrome stay as they are. A body page that still does not fit is re-planned up to three times and then falls back to today's one-member-a-page split, so no slide gets worse. Over the 94 galleries at six box-venue cells, pages fall from 18,066 to 12,405 and slides that lose content from 246 to 153, with no new clipped, unreadable or lost slide (one state-chart slide at square@hall reads a 1px cut as content or footer from run to run, under every split). Without the flag, the split is unchanged.
- **`@laticent/fluido` exports `pack`, `fills`, `badness` and `FILL_BAND`:** pure page packing by measured height, with three packers (greedy first fit, balanced, Knuth–Plass total fit). Knuth–Plass, the recommended packer, puts a lone member on the last page, never first or in the middle.
- **Fit harness:** the fit verdict (overflow, a clip outside chrome, crammed prose) lives in `lib/core/fit-measure.js` and is shared with the measured split; new `--fit-packs` arms score the measured split through the real CLI; a carousel body page's fill no longer reads as 0% when its footer is hidden.
- **cards-grid `three` and `four` reflow by text room.** Under the shared 34em threshold the N-up cards step down to 2-up, or to one column where the box already stacks (portrait, story), and under 23em to one column. Before, three or four cards side by side at a narrow box left a few words a line, which also made every gallery's anti-pattern slide split one card a page. 16:9 decks are unchanged.
