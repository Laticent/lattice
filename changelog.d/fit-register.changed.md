- **`fit:` is one deck setting for everything the engine does to make a slide fit.** `fit: heal` is the default: the engine splits an overfull slide at portrait sizes, and renders a slide that does not fit the deck's `scale-l/xl/2xl` at the designed size, without losing any words. `fit: trim` also lets it cut text that does not fit. `fit: report` switches every move off: the engine only flags. It replaces `guards:`, which still works for now (`strict` means `trim`, `loose` means `heal`). `lint:deck` names the new spelling. The Studio's "Text overflow" field is now "Fit". A slide overrides the deck with `_class: fit-report`, `fit-heal` or `fit-trim`.
- **`_class: guards-strict` and `guards-loose` no longer lint as unknown classes.** They were documented as per-slide overrides but flagged by `unknown-class`.
