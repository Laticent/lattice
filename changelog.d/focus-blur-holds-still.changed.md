- `_focusStyle: blur` no longer scales its target or casts a shadow past it. It only defocuses the rest, so no card moves between `_focusSteps` and no outer card is clipped at the stage edge. Measured at 1280px on six card lists, the clip overhang drops from up to 14.75px to 0 and the cards move by 0px instead of up to 23px. The `--focus-pop`, `--focus-pop-shadow`, `--focus-berth` and `--focus-lift` tokens are gone with it.
