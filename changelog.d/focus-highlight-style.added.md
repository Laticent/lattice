- `_focusStyle: highlight` shows the `_focus:` target with an accent tint and an edge, the `preferred` look of `verdict-grid` without the spark, and leaves every other target untouched. It works on every axis `_focus:` addresses: `item`, `row`, `col`, `cell` and `line` take the tint and edge, a `mark` takes an edge, a `series` a heavier line, and `_focusSteps` and the lint and editor completions accept it. Demos: `examples/focus-styles.md` and `examples/focus-highlight-axes.md`.
