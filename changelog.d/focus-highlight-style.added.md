- `_focusStyle: highlight` shows the `_focus:` target with an accent tint and an edge, the `preferred` look of `verdict-grid` without the spark, and leaves every other target untouched. It works on every axis `_focus:` addresses (`item`, `row`, `col`, `cell`, `line`, `mark`, `series`), and `_focusSteps` and the lint and editor completions accept it. Demos: `examples/focus-styles.md` and `examples/focus-highlight-axes.md`.
