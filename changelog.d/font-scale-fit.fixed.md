- **The projection font scale no longer clips slides.** `scale-l`, `scale-xl` and `scale-2xl` grow the type but not the box, so a slide filled to its component's capacity spilled past the frame: 25 of 64 slides on a real deck at `scale-xl`, and 133 slides across the component galleries. The engine now renders a slide that does not fit at the designed size (1x) instead, so a projected deck carries two sizes at most and never goes below the designed one. The export prints a `↓ SCALE` line naming each page it stepped down. `fit: report` turns this off. Decks without a scale class render pixel-identically.
- **`lint:deck` flags a slide that will render smaller at the deck's scale** (`capacity-scale`, info). It covers a counted component past its measured budget at that scale, and a `code` block past the pane's scaled line or column budget. Each component's docs gain an "At a projection scale" line with the budget.
- **Code keeps scaling at a projection scale, and its line cap scales with it.** A `code` pane at a wide @size holds 15 / 13 / 11 / 10 lines at 1x / `scale-l` / `scale-xl` / `scale-2xl`, one or two fewer under an eyebrow, and `floor(102 / s)` columns.
