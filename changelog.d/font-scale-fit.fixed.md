- **`lint:deck` names a slide that will clip at the deck's projection scale.** `scale-l`, `scale-xl` and `scale-2xl` grow the type but not the box, so a slide filled to its component's capacity spills past the frame: 25 of 64 slides on a real deck at `scale-xl`, and 133 slides across the component galleries. `capacity-scale` warns before you render (a warning under `venue:`, a note for a bare `scale-*`), and the export's `⚠ OVERFLOW` line names every clipped page. The scale is never reduced for you.
- **Code keeps scaling at a projection scale, and its line cap scales with it.** A `code` pane at a wide @size holds 15 / 13 / 11 / 10 lines at 1x / `scale-l` / `scale-xl` / `scale-2xl`, one or two fewer under an eyebrow, and `floor(102 / s)` columns.
