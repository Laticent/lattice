- `venue:` front matter names where a deck will be seen: `laptop` (the default), `huddle` (4–6 people), `conference` (10–30) or `hall` (50–2,000). Lattice derives the type size from it (1x / 1.15x / 1.3x / 1.5x), lifts labels, captions, the header and page numbers a little more than body text at `conference` and `hall`, and `lint:deck` warns when a slide is over its budget for the room. `engineering/typography.md` §7 "Venue" has the viewing-distance math behind the four bands.
