- `venue:` front matter names where a deck will be seen: `laptop` (the default), `huddle` (4–6 people), `conference` (10–30) or `hall` (50–2,000). Lattice derives the type size from it (1x / 1.15x / 1.3x / 1.5x), lifts labels, captions, the header and page numbers a little more than body text at `conference` and `hall`, and `lint:deck` warns when a slide is over its budget for the room. `engineering/typography.md` §7 "Venue" has the viewing-distance math behind the four bands.
- A projection-scaled deck now renders at one size. When a slide is too full for the requested scale, every slide that asked for that scale renders at the largest scale all of them fit (1.15x in a 1.3x deck, say), instead of that one slide dropping to 1x while its neighbors stay large; this replaces the "two sizes at most" rule. A `class: scale-xl` deck with one too-full slide therefore now renders every slide smaller. The export's `↓ SCALE` line names the slides to trim to get each size back, and `fit: report` turns all of it off. Decks with no scale render exactly as before.
