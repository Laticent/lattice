- **Closing the add-slide gallery puts the caret back on the slide it came from.** With a mouse or trackpad, open the gallery from a slide in Compose and close it without inserting: the caret now returns to where it was. On a busy computer it could land at the start of the first slide instead. Other sheets that hand focus back to an editor do the same. (On a touch screen, focus goes to the button that opened the gallery, as before.)
