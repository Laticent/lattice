- **Pane layouts: `columns` and `rows` (experimental).** Name a two-pane slide in `_class` — `<!-- _class: columns 60/40 -->` side by side or `<!-- _class: rows 35/65 -->` stacked, the ratio optional — and write each pane the way you write a slide, one heading level down: `<!-- _pane: bar -->` names its component (default `content`), then its modifiers (`<!-- _pane: team-profile sides -->`), and a `### title` names the pane, set as a label in the eyebrow's voice (an eyebrow pill above it joins it: `Shortlist · Cleared`; a subtitle pill below it stays). Every top-level `###` on such a slide is a pane's title: right under a marker it titles that pane, and anywhere else it starts a text pane. `no-title` hides a pane's title from the eye and keeps it for screen readers, the Read view and the outline. Side by side, the two titles share one line. Demo: `examples/pane-layouts.md`.
- On a slide in the pane syntax, the Key Insight (`>`) or note (`— `) that closes either pane is the slide's, set below both panes; a `>` in the middle of a pane is a quotation and stays, as does what the pane's component uses itself (a quote's attribution, a chart's caption) and a pane that is nothing but a quote. A slide in the experimental comments keeps the rule it had: only what follows the last pane is the slide's.
- `lint:deck` checks the new syntax: `pane-layout` also names a layout with fewer than two panes, a component in the layout's `_class`, a modifier the pane's component does not know, a `no-title` with no title and a deck-wide `class: columns`; `pane-title` suggests one line for a pill above a pane title, and a title of five words or fewer; `pane-insight` names a slide with two Key Insights; `pane-syntax` suggests the rewrite for the experimental `<!-- panes: -->` / `<!-- pane: -->` comments, which still render as before. A pane with a title holds proportionally fewer items.
- **Breaking:** `columns` and `rows` in a slide's `_class` now name a pane layout. A deck that used either as its own class (a Marp idiom: `section.columns { … }` in the deck's CSS) keeps it on a slide with fewer than two panes, but a slide with two or more top-level `###` headings is now laid out in panes. Rename the class (`my-columns`) to keep the old behavior.
