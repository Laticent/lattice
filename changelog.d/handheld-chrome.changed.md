- **A slide read in a hand drops its running header and footer text.** On a square, portrait, story or mobile deck at the phone default, `venue: phone` or `venue: tablet`, the deck's `header:` and `footer:` lines no longer print, and the slide's top margin closes to its side margin, so the content gets that room back. The page number and the dots of a split run stay at the bottom. 16:9 slides, the laptop and room venues, and chart slides keep both lines; `with-header` or `with-footer` on a slide brings either back.
