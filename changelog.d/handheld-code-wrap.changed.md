- On a phone or tablet (a square, tall or strip slide with no room `venue:`), every code block wraps, and a wrapped continuation hangs two columns in. A line used to be cut off at the panel's right edge. `lint:deck` no longer reports `code-line-clipped` there; a narrow deck set for a room (`venue: laptop`, `huddle`, `conference` or `hall`) keeps whole lines and its column budget.
- The `closing` `index` variant reads its rows at body size on a square, tall or strip slide. Five two-line rows at lede size ran past the footer on a phone square, in 40 gallery "See also" slides.
- A plain `redline` clause no longer paints over the gloss list after it. On a phone square the clause shrank below its own text and printed the amendment over "Why this matters"; it now keeps its height, and an overfull slide overflows at the stage edge, where the export reports it.
