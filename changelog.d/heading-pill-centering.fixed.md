- **Large pills in the exported PDF sit on center.** The export's Chrome takes a fallback that nudged every pill's label down 0.05em. On a pill set at 18.5px or larger, which in today's type scale means a pill in an `h1` or an `h2`, that nudge put the label low: up to 2.25px in an `h1` and 1.38px in an `h2`. Those pills now take no nudge, which leaves them within 0.63px of center. Smaller pills (body text, `h3`, and the `:sm` size in any heading) and every pill on a sketch slide keep the nudge as before. Current browsers, which center with `text-box`, are unaffected.
