- `heatmap` can show a **band key**, naming each of the five ramp steps so a reader can tell why two cells share a tone. Add `scale` to the slide and the chart names the bands itself, by the range of values each covers. The key is opt-in — a heatmap that does not ask for one renders exactly as before.
- **Label sets** — one way to name the members of a set, wherever a chart has one. Write `` `[{1, Cold}, {3, Warm}, {5, Hot}]` `` above the table and those bands take your words; the ones you skip keep their derived range. The construct lives in `lib/core/label-set.js` so the other charts that hard-code their keys can adopt it.
- A heatmap cell's `` `# prose` `` annotation now reaches both surfaces it was designed for: a hover popover on the live surfaces, and the slide's speaker note so the exported PDF keeps the same words.
