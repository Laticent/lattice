- **Hide a slide.** Put `<!-- hide -->` (or `<!-- _hide -->`, `<!-- _hide: true -->`) at the top of a slide and it stays in the deck and the editor, but Present and every export leave it out: PDF, PowerPoint, LibreOffice, PNG and image sets, and the webpage, from the Studio and from the command line. Speaker notes, alt text and review comments stay with their own slides. Page numbers count only the slides that show, so hiding slide 3 of 6 gives a PDF numbered 1 to 5, and a hidden divider opens no section on the progress rail.
- **Studio:** the slide menu and the command palette have **Hide slide** and **Show slide**. A hidden slide keeps its place in the strip and the slide map with an eye-off mark, and the preview bar shows a **Hidden** chip that brings it back. One Undo takes either back.
