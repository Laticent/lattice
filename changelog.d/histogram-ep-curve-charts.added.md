- **New `histogram` chart for distributions.** Write each band as a range and its share: `` `$1M..$10M` `55%` ``. Bars touch because the bands join end to end, and each bar's area is its share, so bands of different widths draw honestly. Add `log` to the axis line (`` `[{Annual loss, $1M..$250M, log}]` ``) when the bands widen toward the tail. `lint:deck` names a band written with a dash, bands that leave a gap or overlap, and `log` over a band that starts at zero. Demo deck: `examples/histogram.md`.
- **New `ep-curve` chart for return-period losses.** Write each point as `` `{return period, loss}` ``, such as `` `{100, $410M}` ``, and nest a curve's points under its name for OEP and AEP or gross and net (up to four curves). The return period runs across on a log scale labeled `1-in-10`, `1-in-100`, `1-in-1,000`, and narration reads each curve point by point. Demo deck: `examples/ep-curve.md`.
- **The chart family guide now lists how every chart is written** (`chart-family.docs.md` § How a chart is written): ranges are `a..b`, a position is a `{x, y}` record, an axis is one bracketed line above the heading, and a log axis is the word `log` in it.
