- **hub-spoke under `mode: sketch` measures the hand face it paints.** The width estimate billed every character at a flat 0.66em; Shantell Sans paints digits at 0.70em, `%` at 0.98em and `M` at 1.04em, so values ran past their room and a hub value like `$120M` reached past the edge of its disc. A measured per-character table replaces the flat number: every sketch label on the demo deck and both galleries now paints at or under its estimate (worst 0.975×, was 1.270×), and names wrap less often because lowercase is no longer over-billed.
- **A hub name keeps its words whole when a smaller type size fits.** `fitHubText` cut a word mid-run at full size before trying a smaller size, so a tiered hub printed `Framewor` over `k` in sketch. A hub whose name only fits by such a cut now prints in smaller type, in either face; a break after a hyphen, slash, dot or colon, or between CJK characters, is unchanged. No shipped deck's clean output changes.
- `npm run fonts:measure` re-measures hub-spoke's hand table beside `GLYPH_UPPER` and fails `--check` when an entry under-counts.
