- **New chart component: `hub-spoke`** — one center, its satellites, and what flows between them. Write one top-level item (the hub) and nest the satellites under it; the chart draws a heavy neutral hub, solid satellite discs and constant-width connectors, spread across the stage. Use `bar` or `stacked-bar` instead when the claim is a ranking or a share.
- **Satellites are neutral unless you group them.** A word pill (`Retail`, `Wave 1`, `FY26`) groups a satellite, and up to six groups get a hue and a key band; a group never takes the hub's lightness, even on a palette that spends a slot on the heading ink. A status pill (`at-risk`, `blocked`, `on-track`) marks only its own spoke with a halo, a heavier edge and the spoken status word; nothing else on the slide changes.
- **Nothing scales until you ask.** Values print by default. `sized` scales disc area by value (up to 8 satellites), and `flow-out`, `flow-in` or `flow-both` draw arrowheads and step connector weight in four classes. A per-spoke `flow:in` pill changes that spoke's arrowhead only.
- **`tiered`** hangs leaves off each satellite: up to 6 branches and 18 leaves, each branch its own hue. Every connector keeps a visible floor (hub to branch and branch to leaf), no two discs overlap, and a six-branch slide lays out in about 50 ms.
- **Twenty-five `hub-spoke-*` lint rules** say when a pill is dropped, when `sized` or a flow class switches itself off and why, when the hub name will not fit (an error), and when a slide sits outside the layout's proven envelope (`hub-spoke-crowded`). The grammar, the channel rule and the lint live in one module, `lib/core/hub-spoke-model.js`, shared by the kernel, `lint:deck` and narration; the linter and the narrator read the slide's list through one reader, `lib/core/first-list-tree.js`, so an ordered-list hub (`1. Hub`) lints as it draws.
- `lib/core/chart-values.js` gains `unitKey()`, the family's answer to "are these two values in the same unit" (`$1.2bn` and `$1.2B` are; `20 mg` is not twenty million).
- `_chart-family/arrowhead.js` lifts state-chart's filled arrowhead into a shared geometry helper; state-chart itself is unchanged.
