- **Icons draw on a raw Marp render.** An Export-to-Marp bundle, or any Marp preview that loads the Lattice runtime, now draws `^{…}` icons and pill icons. Before, they stayed as code. The runtime fetches the drawings (`lattice-plugin-icons.js`) from beside itself the first time a slide writes an icon, and the bundle carries that file. A bundle whose deck turned the icons plugin off leaves its spans as code.
