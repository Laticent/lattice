- **A card opacity level, and hairline dividers, on `image spotlight` and `image statement`.** The backing is now a 0–100% level: the four names stay (`card-solid` 100%, `card-soft` 80%, `card-sheer` 55%, `card-clear` 0%) and every other 5% step is a class too (`card-o5` … `card-o95`). On the deck line, `image-card: 65%` writes a level. In the Studio, Deck settings → Image and a slide's Image tab show the backing as a slider with the four names as chips. A thin backing needs a matching `ink-light` or `ink-dark`.
- **Dividers that take no space.** A stacked card now draws a thin line under its heading, and a `band` draws a vertical line between its heading and text columns. Both paint `--spectrum-structure`, so they follow the deck's `spectrum-trim:` and a slide's own `spectrum-trim` class (a quiet accent tint by default, the deck's spectrum under `on`); `rule: none` drops them. Measured with `check:jank`: the card's geometry is identical with and without them at every heading length. **Existing `spotlight` and `statement` slides with a heading and a body gain the under-heading line.** Demo: `examples/image-band.md`.
