- **`band` — a full-width strip for the text card on `image spotlight` and `image statement`.** Add `band` to `at-top` or `at-bottom` and the card stretches across the slide along that edge, heading on the left and text on the right, so the middle of the photo stays clear of words. It keeps the card's inset and rounded corners on spotlight, and shortens the scrim on statement. It composes with every other card control (`card-soft`, `ink-dark`, `card-sm`, `compact`). On a corner, on `at-center`, or in a portrait or square deck, `band` does nothing. In the `image-card:` register it is the new `shape` axis: `image-card: bottom band`; `card-float` is the slide-side way out of a deck-wide `band`. The Studio's **Image** tabs gain a Card shape control. Demo: `examples/image-band.md`.
