- **`image spotlight` and `image statement` card controls.** Four new class axes now control the text card over a full-bleed photo. **Placement:** `at-tl` · `at-top` · `at-tr` · `at-center` · `at-bl` · `at-bottom` · `at-br`. **Backing:** `card-solid` · `card-soft` · `card-sheer` · `card-clear` sets the opacity of the spotlight panel or the statement scrim. **Ink:** `ink-light` · `ink-dark` sets white or near-black words, and the backing flips to match. **Size:** `card-sm` · `card-md` · `card-lg` steps the width, padding and type together. `compact` tightens the card's padding and its inset from the slide edge to 70% at any size. The statement scrim follows the card's placement. Slides that name none of these classes render as before. Demo: `examples/image-card-controls.md`.
- **`image-card:` front-matter register.** One line sets the card for every image slide: `image-card: top-right soft sm compact`. It takes up to one word per axis: placement (`top-left` … `bottom-right`, `center`), backing, ink, size and density (`regular` · `compact`). It rides image slides only, because `at-*` and `compact` mean something on other layouts too. A slide's own class overrides one axis and keeps the rest; `card-regular` lets one slide step out of a deck-wide `compact`. Lint reports an unknown or doubled word (`unknown-image-card`). In the Studio, the Deck settings and the slide inspector each have an **Image** tab with one control per axis.
