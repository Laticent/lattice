- **A thin spotlight card gets an ink that reads, picked from the photo.** A spotlight card under 60% opaque lets the photo show through, so the theme's own ink was read against the photo and could vanish. In the Studio, dragging a slide's Card backing slider below 60% with no ink set on the slide or the deck now samples the pixels behind the card in the live preview and writes `ink-light` or `ink-dark`, whichever has the higher contrast ratio (WCAG 2, counting the card's own fill at that opacity). It never overwrites an ink you set, Card ink → Auto removes it, and a photo the browser cannot read (a cross-origin image) gets no automatic ink. For everyone else, lint warns when a thin spotlight card has neither: `thin-card-no-ink`. The rule and the choice share one kernel, `lib/core/card-ink.mjs`.
