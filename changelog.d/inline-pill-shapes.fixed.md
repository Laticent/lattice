- Inline pill shapes now match each other. `:circle` and `:diamond` are as tall as the capsule, so a line or a `list-tabular` row that holds one stops growing. Before, they measured 31.9px and 37.9px beside a 25.9px capsule. `:diamond` is now a 1.4:1 rhombus, so it can hold two characters at that height. The chevrons and the diamond draw the same 1px edge as every other shape; before, they rendered as flat tint with no edge. `:tag-bordered` now draws its double stroke inside its own box, where the old outer ring spilled 3px onto the words beside it.
