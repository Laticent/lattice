- **Fixed: inline pill shapes now match each other.** `:circle` and `:diamond` are as tall as the capsule, so a line or a `list-tabular` row that holds one stops growing. Before, they measured 31.9px and 37.9px beside a 25.9px capsule. `:diamond` is now a flat rhombus that widens with its label, so two characters fit inside the outline at that height. A `:circle` holding a wider label becomes a capsule with room around the text. The chevrons and the diamond draw the same 1px edge as every other shape; before, they rendered as flat tint with no edge. `:tag-bordered` now draws its double stroke as a `double` border inside its own box, where the old outer ring spilled 3px onto the words beside it, and lowercase descenders clear both lines.
- **Fixed: pill labels sit on the pill's visual center in every browser**, not just on the center of the line box. Where the browser supports `text-box` (current Chrome, Firefox and Safari), the label is trimmed to its capitals and centered on them. Measured, the worst pill is now 0.58px off in Chrome, 0.21px in Firefox and 0.42px in WebKit, where Chrome used to put labels up to 1.3px high. Exported PDFs render with Chrome 131, which predates `text-box`, and take a measured fallback: the worst case in the PDF drops from 1.47px to 0.68px. The label is also no longer pushed left by the letter-spacing after its last letter.
- **Fixed:** the left chevron's flat right edge no longer disappears in WebKit (Safari and the macOS app) when the pill's width lands on a fractional pixel.
- **Changed: inline pills size themselves from the text they sit in.** In a heading, or in any text more than about twice the pill's size (a lead or emphasis paragraph, a hero line), a pill is half that text's size, so it no longer looks tiny beside a title. In a below-note, a caption or a footer it takes the small size. Pills in body text, lists, cards and table cells are unchanged. `:sm` and `:lg` still work and now scale that automatic size.
- **Changed: `lint:deck` now warns when a `:circle` or `:diamond` pill holds more than one character or a two-digit number**, and the Studio and Playground editors underline the pill itself. The limit comes from measurement: `{12}` keeps a circle round, while `{OK}` makes it an oval and `{WM}` a capsule. The check was a quiet suggestion that allowed any two characters; it is now the `pill-shape-crowded` warning in the lint core. It never blocks a deck.
