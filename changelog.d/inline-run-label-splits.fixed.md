- A pill, an inline `code` or a bold word in the middle of a list item's text no longer splits the text into separate pieces in `list-steps`, `checklist`, `pricing`, `verdict-grid`, `matrix-2x2`, `cycle`, `cards-grid`, `cards-stack`, `kpi`, `list` and `agenda`. Before, a flex or grid item laid each piece out on its own, so a step title could become the title, a pill stretched across the card, and the rest of the title beneath it. A trailing pill or `code` still sits in its own slot. A demo deck, `examples/inline-run-labels.md`, shows it; twelve decks in the repo render differently, each from scattered to a flowing sentence.
