- A brand mark for the **Laticent** org, in `design/logo/laticent/` — an incised `L`, the letter with its load path cut through it. It ships in two forms with different jobs: a **bare letter** (light/dark adaptive) that sits beside the five product marks, and a **bluestone tile** for app icons, favicons and avatars, whose content is the only form in the family that clears the Android maskable safe circle (45.3 against 51.2). Both have `-min` variants that drop the channel below ~28px, plus light and dark lockups in each form. `generate.py` rebuilds all eight and asserts two invariants from the geometry; `audit.py` checks every asset against a round-avatar crop and is transform-aware.
- The lockup's wordmark is an **outlined path**, not live `<text>`. Measured in real Chromium, "Laticent" spans 219.6 to 315.9 units across the family's own fallback chain, so no fixed viewBox allotment is both tight and safe — the five sibling lockups still set live text and still carry that defect. `outline-wordmark.py` re-derives the path from Fraunces on demand.
