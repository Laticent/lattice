- Added `examples/lattice-executive-briefing.md` — a 54-slide executive briefing that argues the engine is a compiler rather than a slide app. The spine is a four-layer stack (text, the `_class` grammar, 61 typed manifests, and everything those project), with the visible capabilities presented as what the stack yields rather than as a feature list, and a full-disclosure section naming what is unverified.
- The briefing is the first deck in the repository to use **reader lenses**: it ships five views from one source (`full`, `brief`, `investor`, `buyer`, `board`, `pager`) with every slide tagged. The lenses are authored but deliberately unapproved — approval is a content hash stamped by a human in Studio, so the deck demonstrates the fail-closed property rather than working around it.
- The `pager` lens projects the deck to a **three-page leave-behind**. Measured first: a single page holds ~130 words of prose at 16:9 and only ~60 at `portrait`, so a conventional 300-word one-pager is not achievable at any canvas the engine ships — the tall social sizes hold *less* prose, not more. Three landscape pages is the smallest honest format, and the middle page is a new marquee-overview slide the full deck also wanted.

- The briefing is grounded in three measurements the repository had not previously published: a reverse-dependency closure showing what dies when a layer is removed, the browser-to-command-line source overlap (115 of 117 files shared, 225 of 234 committed decks byte-identical through both paths), and a table of the tradeoffs the engine refuses rather than balances.
