- Added `examples/lattice-executive-briefing.md` — a 51-slide executive briefing that argues the engine is a compiler rather than a slide app, split into a marquee tier (ten demonstrable capabilities) and a substrate tier (the machine-enforced engineering underneath), with a full-disclosure section naming what is unverified.
- The briefing is the first deck in the repository to use **reader lenses**: it ships five views from one source (`full`, `brief`, `investor`, `buyer`, `board`) with every slide tagged. The lenses are authored but deliberately unapproved — approval is a content hash stamped by a human in Studio, so the deck demonstrates the fail-closed property rather than working around it.
