- **Changed: the library marks no longer share one palette.** Fuoco swapped its two colors (ember with a teal accent) and Trama moved from sienna to brick red with a gold accent, Lente moved from blue to cyan, Suono moved toward emerald, and Cadenza and Vetrina traded the shared amber accent for magenta and violet. The closest two library marks are now 0.071 apart in OKLab instead of 0.027. The seven library demo pages carry the new colors.
- **Fixed: library mark and demo-page colors hold contrast in light and dark.** Veduta's light accent was 2.83:1 on paper and Trama's was 3.0:1; every drawn color is now at least 3:1 on both grounds, and the text colors on the demo pages are at least 4.5:1. A new unit test, `test/unit/docs/library-mark-palette.test.js`, keeps it that way.
- **Changed: each library's colors are written once.** `docs/src/lib/library-palettes.json` now holds the roles behind a library's brand SVGs and its demo page. `node tools/build-library-marks.js` writes the SVG colors from it, and a test fails if they drift. On the demo pages, an explicit light or dark theme now also sets the shadow (five pages used to keep the other scheme's), and Veduta now honors an explicit dark theme.
