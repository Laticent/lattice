- The Studio Library has an **Images** tab. Add photos from your camera roll, camera or files (on Android and iOS the picker opens natively), or drop them on the panel. Each image can belong to **all decks** or to **this deck** only, and moves between the two without renaming. JPEG and HEIC photos are resized to a 2560 px long edge and re-encoded, which also removes their embedded location. Photos are included in workspace backups and come back on restore, to the same deck. In a browser that refuses to store files (Safari in a private window), the Library keeps the photo as raw bytes instead of failing, and says so if it cannot store it at all. Deleting a deck deletes the photos kept to that deck only, and the Workspace storage line now counts photos by their size.
