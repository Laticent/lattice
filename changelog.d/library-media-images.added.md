- The Studio Library has an **Images** tab. Add photos from your camera roll, camera or files (on Android and iOS the picker opens natively), or drop them on the panel. Each image can belong to **all decks** or to **this deck** only, and moves between the two without renaming. JPEG and HEIC photos are resized to a 2560 px long edge and re-encoded, which also removes their embedded location. Decks cannot show these images yet; that follows in the same release.
