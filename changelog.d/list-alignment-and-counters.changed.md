- **Changed: `list` follows the `cards:` register.** `cards-top`, `cards-center`, `cards-stretch` and `cards-spread` (or the deck's `cards:` key) now place a list's rows, in every register (plain, `takeaway`, `takeaway numbered`, `principles`). Before this, every list ignored them. With no setting, a list on a wide slide now **sits centered at its content height** instead of stretching every row to fill the stage. Write `cards: stretch` (or `_class: list cards-stretch`) for the old full-height look. Square, tall and strip frames keep their spread-out rows, and a list above a Key Insight still stretches.
- **Changed: `list` counters share one scale.** Every counter is 1.4× its row's text, sits in the same box, and clears the text by one `--sp-md` gap. The plain numbered list's counter grows to match, the empty space after a `takeaway numbered` counter shrinks from ~72px to ~26px, and `principles` steps down from 1.6× (64px to 56px) with its counter-to-text gap narrowing from 32px to 24px. A lead over a nested gloss now keeps the row's full size instead of shrinking to the size of its gloss.
- **Changed: a full `list` tightens its rows.** Five or more rows take less padding (and six pills a smaller gap), so every register still fits the stage at its capacity.
- **Fixed: a wrapped `list` row stays inside its pill.** A list gave every row an equal share of the height, so on a full slide a row that wrapped to two lines spilled out of its pill, or onto the rule below it in `takeaway`, and no overflow check saw it. A row now never shrinks below its text; a list with more than the slide holds overflows, and the export reports it.
- **Added: `lint:deck` warns on a `list` modifier that does nothing** (`list-modifier-inert`): `principles` over `-` bullets (which renders plain pills), `numbered` without `takeaway`, `lettered`/`roman`/`bullet` without `principles`, and `takeaway` over a `1.` list without `numbered` (which hides the numbers).
