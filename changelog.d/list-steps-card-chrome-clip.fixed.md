- **Fixed: a dense `list-steps` strip lost the bottom border, radius and shadow off EVERY card, with no text clipped at all.** The step row is `ol { display:flex; flex:1 }` inside `.cell-stage`, a bounded clipping cell — but a flex item's default `min-height:auto` floors it at its CONTENT height, so the row refused to shrink to the cell and `overflow:clip` sheared the card chrome. `align-items:stretch` gives every card the tallest card's height, so one overstuffed step took all four cards' bottom edges with it. Measured at `size: hd`: a 460.3px row in a 438.2px stage, 22.0px shorn, and the text ended 3px above the clip — pure chrome damage on cards that were not even full, plus a spurious "Content clipped" warning on a slide that had lost nothing. `min-height: 0` on `section.list-steps ol` releases the floor, the same fix `cards-stack` already carries and documents. Landscape hits it first: a 16:9 stage is the shortest one a four-column strip has to fit into, which is why `size: hd` and `size: 4k` both showed it and `standard` (4:3) did not.
