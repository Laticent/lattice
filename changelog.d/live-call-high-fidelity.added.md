- Live calls get a per-person **High fidelity (music)** switch in the microphone menu (the arrow beside Mute). It re-captures the microphone with echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain off and sends 128 kbit/s mono instead of 64 kbit/s, for music or a demo. Wear headphones with it on. Stereo is not offered: the transport gives us no way to ask the other side for it.
