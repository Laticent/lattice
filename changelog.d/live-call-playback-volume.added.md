- Live calls get a per-person volume control: a speaker button on every other person's row, always there, that opens a popover with a 0 to 200 % slider (kept for the session). Above 100 % the voice plays through Suono's new `stage.amplify(stream, gain)` gain stage, for calls that play quiet (an iPhone with its microphone on). Where it cannot act yet, the popover says why (join the call; they are not on it).
