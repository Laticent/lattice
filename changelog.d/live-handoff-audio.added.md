- **Live sessions show how each person is connected.** Each row in the Live panel carries an icon for the connection: same network, direct across networks, or through a relay. The person's ⋯ menu spells the connection out with the raw candidate pair, so a two-device test can be recorded from a phone (`engineering/decisions/2026-10-06-studio-live-collaboration.md` §8.1).
- **A live session keeps going while the host is away.** If the host's connection stays down for about half a minute, the next person who can edit hosts until the host is back. Everyone else rejoins without knocking, chat keeps flowing, and new people can knock again. When the host returns, they take the session back, with the chat and the people let in meanwhile. The host's panel names who stands in. Tavola's wire protocol moves to version 2, so a tab still open from before shows *Reload to join*.
- **Live sessions have audio calls.** *Join with audio* in the Live panel or the header pill sends your microphone to everyone in the session, with echo cancellation and noise suppression. You can mute, pick a microphone, or leave the call. Each person shows their mic and a speaking ring while they talk, and the tab title starts with *On air* while your microphone is live. Audio goes only to people the host let in.
