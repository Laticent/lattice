- **Breaking:** `logo-style:`, `logo-on:`, `logo-x:`, `logo-y:` and `logo-scale:` no longer render (`logo-style:` is now `logo-finish:`, named like `finish:` and `chart-finish:`). `lint:deck --fix` (and the Studio's Fix all) folds them into one `logo-finish:` line: `logo-on: title` becomes `title`, `logo-scale` becomes `sm` or `lg`, and a free `logo-x`/`logo-y` position becomes the nearest of the six placements.
- `logo-style: brand` becomes `logo-finish: brand` and keeps its meaning (the logo's original colors on a plate); `auto` is the default color word.
