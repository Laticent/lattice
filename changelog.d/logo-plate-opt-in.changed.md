- **Breaking:** the deck logo's `brand` color word no longer paints a soft plate behind the mark: it keeps the file's own colors with nothing behind them, so a logo with a transparent background stays transparent. The plate is now its own `logo-finish:` word, `plate`, off by default: write `logo-finish: plate` (or `brand plate`) for the old look, or `_class: logo-plate` on one slide, and `_class: logo-no-plate` takes a deck-wide plate back on one slide. Decks that overrode the plate in their own `style:` can drop the override.
