- **`math compare` moves onto the Form frame.** The comparison's columns now live in the stage cell rather than on the section, so a compare slide gains the masthead band, `meta:`, the progress rail and the watermark tile like every other Form layout.
- **It gains a responsive reflow it could not have had before.** `compare` used to stay two- or three-up at every size, and the reason was structural: the multicolumn lived on the `<section>`, and a container query cannot style its own container. With the multicol on `.cell-stage`, a tall or strip canvas now collapses the comparison to a single column instead of squeezing three columns into 1080px.
- **Capacity is reduced, and this is the trade.** The stage is the frame's content box — 1152px wide against the section's 1280, and about 54px shorter — so a compare slide holds less than it did. The committed gallery's compare slides fit with no overflow warning before or after; a slide that only fitted by using the footer band now overflows and is flagged. The hero equation is 1.25em (was 1.4em) so the same equations fit the narrower column.
- The `column-span: all` heading and the `break-inside: avoid` eyebrow arm are **gone**, because the boxes they governed are in the masthead now. That removes #1554's root cause structurally — and retires what its WebKit raster guard was guarding, so the structural fact is asserted per-PR instead. Whether the ghost is gone on real WebKit is **unverified** here: no WebKit in this sandbox.
