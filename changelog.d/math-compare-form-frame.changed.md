- **`math compare` moves onto the Form frame.** The comparison's columns now live in the stage cell rather than on the section, so a compare slide gains the masthead band, `meta:`, the progress rail and the watermark tile like every other Form layout.
- **Its responsive reflow becomes per-slide.** A portrait `compare` already collapsed to one column, keyed on the deck-wide `data-orientation` stamp; it now keys on the per-slide `[data-family]` instead, and the three-column case collapses too (it previously lost a specificity race and kept all three columns in a portrait box).
- **Capacity is reduced in HEIGHT only.** The stage is about 54px shorter than the section's body region, because the frame reserves space below the content. Width is unchanged — the sovereign section already carried 64px of horizontal padding, so the column boxes measure identically before and after. The committed gallery's compare slides fit either way, with no overflow warning; a slide that only fitted by using the space below now overflows and is flagged.
- The hero equation is 1.25em (was 1.4em). This buys margin rather than correctness: at 1.4em the widest estimator already overran its column on the sovereign arm, so the shear predates the migration — what changed is that `overflow: clip` now cuts it instead of letting it paint into the section's padding.
- The `column-span: all` heading and the `break-inside: avoid` eyebrow arm are **gone**, because the boxes they governed are in the masthead now. That removes #1554's root cause structurally, so the structural fact is asserted per-PR in place of a WebKit raster guard that could no longer fail. The ghost was then checked on real (desktop) WebKit and is gone, with an oracle proven able to fire against a deliberately broken arm; iPadOS Safari — where #1554 was reported — is still unverified.
