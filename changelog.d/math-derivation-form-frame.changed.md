- **`math derivation` moves onto the Form frame.** The proof chain lives in the stage cell now, so a derivation slide gains the masthead band, `meta:`, the running header and footer, the progress rail and the watermark tile — none of which a sovereign math slide could render.
- **The absolutely-positioned title is gone.** The sovereign arm pinned the `h2` with `top: calc(var(--sp-2xl) + var(--sp-md))` — the masthead band's geometry, copied by arithmetic — so the table could center in the well beneath it. The frame owns the title now and hands the stage the height that is left, so centering is `justify-content` on a box that already knows where it ends.
- **The proof chain tightens its row rhythm in a tall or strip box.** The Form masthead is an in-flow band where the sovereign title was not, and at portrait a wrapped title takes 271px of it, which put the committed sample 33px over the stage. Row padding drops from `--sp-sm` to `--sp-xs` vertically at `tall`/`strip`; nothing collapses and no type shrinks.
- The base `<table>` deny guard entry narrows from `.math.derivation` to `.math.derivation.form`, because that is the granularity the component's CSS now claims. A `math derivation` section without the form class gets the universal table defaults instead of nothing.
