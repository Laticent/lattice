- **Breaking:** a `math` slide can no longer opt out of the Form frame. `<!-- _class: math no-form -->` on the migrated variants now renders unstyled prose, because those variants no longer carry a second, sovereign layout. The combination has never behaved differently from a plain `math` slide — math was already chrome-exempt through `FORM_TOGGLE_SKIP`, so `no-form` was a no-op on it — and no committed deck uses it. `form` is not optional.
- The `math` layout begins moving off its sovereign frame onto the shared Form frame, one variant per commit. **`feature` and bare `math` move first.** They gain the masthead band, the `meta:` strip, the progress rail and the watermark tile — none of which a sovereign math slide could render, so `meta:` on a math slide silently did nothing until now. The hero equation and its `where` legend keep their layout.
- Two visible differences on a migrated math slide: the heading sits in the masthead band rather than in the layout's own grid, and it gains the masthead's hairline, which every other Form slide already has.
- The seven remaining variants render **byte-identically** and keep their sovereign frame until their own commit.
