- **Breaking-ish: `math` no longer has a sovereign frame.** `lib/forms/frame/math/` is deleted, `math` leaves `FORM_TOGGLE_SKIP`, and the component declares `stage: "canvas"` with `conformance: "strict"`. Every math slide now renders with the masthead band, `meta:`, the running header and footer, the progress rail and the watermark tile — none of which a chrome-exempt section could produce. `meta:` on a math slide used to do nothing at all.
- **`<!-- _class: math no-form -->` and `form: off` now render math unstyled.** The sovereign arms are deleted, so there is no second shape to fall back to. That combination never behaved differently from a plain math slide (math was already chrome-exempt, so `no-form` was a no-op on it) and no committed deck uses it.
- **Math is the only component ever to leave the sovereign list**, and it is worth saying why: its claim was "drives its own `> h2` title grid" — a heading-placement preference, not the "this slide has no room for chrome" that `title`, `divider`, `image` and the split frames rest on. In practice that title grid was the masthead band's geometry copied by arithmetic, `top: calc(var(--sp-2xl) + var(--sp-md))`, in three separate variants. `compare-code` carries the same wording in its manifest and not the same substance: it builds a real four-row explicit grid and places its `> h2` at `grid-column: 1 / -1; grid-row: 1`. Math's migration is not a precedent for migrating it.
- **The eight variant summaries are complete sentences now.** All eight were cut at 70 characters with an ellipsis — several mid-word — and math was the only component in the tree with the defect (the other 195, across the 45 components that declare variants, are intact). They are read by the docs site, the agent-facing component catalog and the gallery footers, so an agent picking `decompose` was choosing from half a sentence. One also carried a British spelling (HARD RULE #21).
- The first attempt at that replaced them with FULL sentences, which broke the gallery: those summaries are injected verbatim into each slide's generated `_footer:`, and at 130–138 characters the footer band clipped them — six slides over the overflow ratchet, and a visibly ellipsed footer in both committed gallery PDFs. The 70-character cap was load-bearing and was not established as such before being removed. The summaries are rewritten to fit it as whole sentences instead, which is what the defect actually was.
