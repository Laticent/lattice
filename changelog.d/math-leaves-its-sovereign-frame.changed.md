- **Breaking-ish: `math` no longer has a sovereign frame.** `lib/forms/frame/math/` is deleted, `math` leaves `FORM_TOGGLE_SKIP`, and the component declares `stage: "canvas"` with `conformance: "strict"`. Every math slide now renders with the masthead band, `meta:`, the running header and footer, the progress rail and the watermark tile — none of which a chrome-exempt section could produce. `meta:` on a math slide used to do nothing at all.
- **`<!-- _class: math no-form -->` and `form: off` now render math unstyled.** The sovereign arms are deleted, so there is no second shape to fall back to. That combination never behaved differently from a plain math slide (math was already chrome-exempt, so `no-form` was a no-op on it) and no committed deck uses it.
- **Math is the only component ever to leave the sovereign list**, and it is worth saying why: its claim was "drives its own `> h2` title grid" — a heading-placement preference, not the "this slide has no room for chrome" that `title`, `divider`, `image` and the split frames rest on. In practice that title grid was the masthead band's geometry copied by arithmetic, `top: calc(var(--sp-2xl) + var(--sp-md))`, in three separate variants. `compare-code` is the last frame making the same weak claim.
- **The eight variant summaries are no longer truncated.** All eight were cut at 70 characters with an ellipsis — several mid-word — and math was the only component in the tree with the defect (the other 195 variant summaries are intact). They are read by the docs site, the agent-facing component catalog and the gallery footers, so an agent picking `decompose` was choosing from half a sentence. One of them also carried a British spelling (HARD RULE #21).
