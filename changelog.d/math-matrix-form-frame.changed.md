- **`math matrix` moves onto the Form frame**, and is the first math variant to render clean at all five registered sizes. On the sovereign frame it clipped at portrait, story and mobile; it now clips at none of them.
- **The equation no longer scales UP in a tall box.** The sovereign reflow took it from 1.7em to 2.4em on the theory that a full-width row has room; a 4×4 `pmatrix` does not, and portrait ran 279px wide and 206px tall past the section. It is 1.15em at tall/strip now, and the width it gains from the full row is the point of the reflow.
- **Fixed: a legend value that wraps no longer returns to the left margin.** `min-width: 4em` on the label reserved the width but not the indent, so "rank — full-rank for OLS to have a unique solution" put its second line under the label instead of beside it, breaking the two-column reading on exactly the rows long enough to need it. It is a hanging indent now. Pre-existing at every size, on both frames.
- **Both grid tracks are `minmax(0, …)`.** A track's default minimum is its min-content width, so the legend's label reservation gave it a 358px minimum against a 333px track and pushed the whole stage 25px wide — while the list itself reported no overflow, because the overflow belonged to the track.
- **Fixed in the engine: a clip cell laid out in COLUMNS no longer reports phantom overflow.** `probeSectionOverflow` measured a possibly-squeezed child against "the next flowed sibling's top", which assumes the children stack. In a two-track grid the next sibling sits *beside*, so the subtraction returned the child's own height: 2px of phantom inner overflow on a KaTeX equation (its `vlist` struts make every enclosing box report hidden pixels) became `dy: 265` and a "Content clipped" badge on a slide with nothing clipped. The sibling's top is used only when it actually sits below; otherwise the limit falls back to the cell's content bottom, so a genuinely crushed child in a multi-column cell is still caught. Multi-column clip cells are built by `kpi`, `redline`, `inventory`, `citation-card` and the legal components as well as math.
