- **Math is its own family in the component reference.** It used to share a heading with code under "Code & math", where it was the smaller half of someone else's family on the one surface where a reader browses by shape. It is a distinct substance — `prose` with a typeset equation as the argument, against code's `snippet` — its own engine bucket, its own URL space and its own gallery, and the only component whose body is rendered by a third-party typesetter. "Code" now covers `code` and `compare-code`; "Math" covers `math`.
