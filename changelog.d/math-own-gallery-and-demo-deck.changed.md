- **`math` is out of the `data-viz` showcase.** That deck is generated from `buckets: ['chart', 'math']` and is now charts only. Math is rendered evidence, which is true and is not the same as being a chart: a chart plots a dataset and a math slide typesets an expression — no shared transform, no shared dataset shape, no shared reflow — and a reader walking the charting surface to compare `bar` against `waterfall` does not want an equation in the middle of it.
- It did **not** gain a showcase of its own, and that was tried first: a showcase composes one `manifest.sample` per component, so a single-component bucket makes a two-slide deck that duplicates the bucket gallery already in the tree. The eight-variant survey is the component gallery. Both facts are pinned so neither drifts back.
- **New: `examples/math-form-frame.md`** — nine slides, one per variant plus a cover, with `header:`, `footer:`, `paginate:` and `meta:` set so the chrome math gained is the thing you see. Zero overflow at 16:9; `lint:deck` clean.
- The demo deck's `decompose` equation puts its `=` at the end of the previous line rather than on its own. That is not style: a lone `=` continuation line inside `$$…$$` is read as a setext `h1` underline by `positionIsTrustworthy`, which blanks fenced code before its heading scan but not display math, and the whole deck is silently refused a supplied slide position.
