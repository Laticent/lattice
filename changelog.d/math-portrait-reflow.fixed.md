- A `math` slide no longer loses its adaptive reflow in a portrait, story or mobile box. The rules that collapse the equation and its legend into one column were scoped to direct children of the `<section>`; moving the body into the Form frame's stage cell made every one of them inert, and the legend ran 64px off a 1080px slide. Measured, and now covered by a gate that fails when a migrated variant has no Form-arm reflow and has not declared why it needs none.
- The hero equation in a tall box renders at 2.4em rather than 2.7em. At `size: story` the reference equation was 1029px wide against a 972px box — 57px of it clipped. That was true before this change too and had simply never been visible: math had no clip cell, so the overflow watcher never looked at it. Moving math into the shared frame is what surfaced it.
