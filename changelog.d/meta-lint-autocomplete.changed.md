- Lint now warns on every old directive comment (`<!-- _class: cards -->`, `<!-- _footer: … -->`, …) with `legacy-directive-comment`, and its fix rewrites the whole deck as `<!-- @meta{…} -->` records: the Studio's quick fix and "Fix all", `lint:deck --fix` and `lattice migrate meta` all write the same text, and the slides render the same.
- `@meta` is the one record spelling. A bare `<!-- {…} -->` record gets a `meta-bare-record` warning whose one-line fix adds `@meta`, and `lattice migrate meta` now writes `<!-- @meta{…} -->` and respells bare records.
- `lattice migrate meta` now merges a slide's adjacent records into one, as its help always said; the merge had never run.
- The Studio and Playground editors complete `<!-- @meta{`: `<!-- @m` offers it, then the fields, then each register's values. The blank-line insert list offers `<!-- @meta{layout=} -->` and `<!-- @meta{} -->` in place of `<!-- _class: -->` and the bare `<!-- {} -->`. Lint fix messages teach the record.
- The `![bg]` keyword fix writes the class it adds as a `<!-- @meta{class=…} -->` record. On a slide whose class a `@meta` record already sets, it no longer writes a second class line: it names the word to add to the record.
- `lint:deck --strict`, the pre-commit deck check and the Coach's Contract score do not count the two old-spelling warnings yet. `lint:deck` lists them once per file. This lasts until the committed decks are converted.
