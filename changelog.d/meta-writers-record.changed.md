- The Studio and every Lattice writer now write slide settings as one record, `<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, footer="Q3"} -->`, never `<!-- _class: … -->` or the bare `<!-- {…} -->`. That covers the "This slide" drawer, Compose's Layout sheet (both lists) and pane picker, Hide and Show, picture placement, the thin-card fix, new decks and new slides, chat charts and figures, motion scenes, the component skeletons that `lattice new slide`, Fabricate and the AI prompts hand out, and the slides the gallery generators add. Editing a setting written in an old comment moves it into the record, and the slide renders the same. Decks you already have keep rendering until the cut; `lattice migrate meta` converts a whole deck.
- The structured editor now reads a comment that holds two directive lines (`<!-- _class: stats` then `_footer: Q3 -->`) the way the engine renders it: one class, `stats _footer: Q3`, and no footer. The class shows read-only, and setting the footer writes a record that applies.
- The Studio preview's whole-deck check reads a slide record's class and `carry` only. A footer or note that says "proof", "carry", "agenda" or "divider" no longer sends the preview down the slower whole-deck render.
