- **A narrated deck can open with a greeting and end with a closing.** Set `greeting:` and `closing:` in front matter, as `true` or custom text. `{greeting}` in the text becomes "Good morning", "Good afternoon" or "Good evening" from the listener's own clock (04:00, 12:00 and 17:00 boundaries, never "good night"). The greeting plays before slide 1 when narration starts there. The closing plays after the last slide when narration reaches the end by itself, including autoplay onto a silent "Thank you" slide. Each plays at most once, and pausing or going back never repeats it. Both work in the Studio's Present view and in an exported player, which carries all three greetings and picks one when Play is pressed. A video export and a downloaded `.vtt` say "Hello", because they have no listener clock. The linter warns about a greeting that hard-codes the time of day (`greeting-hardcoded-period`), a `{…}` that would be read aloud (`unknown-bookend-placeholder`), and an English salutation in a non-English deck (`greeting-not-english`).
- **The timing track (LTT) gains an optional `bookends` section** beside `segments`, holding the greeting's four variants and the closing, each laid out like a narrated slide. `timeline()` takes a `greeting` variant and returns `greeting` and `closing` entries, with `segments[i]` still slide i + 1. An export's audio blocks for them are keyed by name (`greeting-morning` … `closing`).
