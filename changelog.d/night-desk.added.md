- New `/desk-night` acts for the owner while they are away. After the owner types `/desk-night` and then "good night" or "afk", it merges the owner's non-visual VERY HIGH PRs that the merge gate finds eligible on GitHub, until the owner's next message there or 12 hours. Visual PRs and anything that needs the owner wait for a morning digest.
- `tools/night-eligible.mjs` answers whether one PR may merge tonight, with every reason it may not.
- A new push to a PR now turns its auto-merge off (`.github/workflows/disarm-on-push.yml`), so an approval or a night check never carries over to code it did not see.
