- In an editable PowerPoint or ODP, a line is split into one text box per piece where an inline icon, spark or pill takes room in it, instead of holding that room with stretched character spacing, which iPhone Preview honored only in part (a word slid under the icon). Only the line the icon sits in is cut; the rest of the paragraph stays one box. Headings (so the slide's title stays whole), right-to-left, justified and indented text, math, and gaps made only of spaces are never split.
