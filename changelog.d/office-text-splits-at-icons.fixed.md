- In an editable PowerPoint or ODP, a line is split into one text box per piece where an inline icon, spark or pill takes room in it, instead of holding that room with stretched character spacing, which iPhone Preview honored only in part (a word slid under the icon). Right-to-left, justified and indented text, and math, stay one box. A sentence with an inline icon now edits as pieces; a heading split this way is still the slide's title.
