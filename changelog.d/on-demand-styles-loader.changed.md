- **Playground:** the preview loads only the engine styles a deck uses. A style loader fetches an index and the always-needed modules, scans the slides the engine rendered, and fetches the modules those slides need before the slide paints. A component added in an edit brings its module with it. `?styles=full` loads the whole sheet, as before. The Studio, the landing page and exports are unchanged.
- **Tooling:** `tools/style-loader-gate.mjs` checks the loader on the real Playground, comparing every element's computed style (screen, print, dark) against the whole sheet. `tools/style-needs-audit.mjs` now renders its baseline through the default path and feeds subsets as the minified modules, so a subset takes the same content mask as a full render. The token index drops alternatives another alternative already covers (33 KB to 14 KB gzipped).
- **Playground:** two more opt-in loader modes for comparison, `?styles=floor` (the start-up styles as one prebuilt file) and `?styles=bundle` (also later modules as one request to a composing endpoint). `docs/scripts/style-loader-bench.mjs` compares all four strategies on HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2; results are in the decision note.
