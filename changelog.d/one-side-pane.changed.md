- **Studio shows one side pane at a time.** Coach, Chat, Live, Reader views, Library, Slide settings and Deck settings now share one slot: opening any of them replaces the one showing, so the editor and preview are never squeezed by two docked columns (at 1100px the editor stays on screen instead of folding away). Closing a pane returns to the slide and never reopens an earlier one. When one pane opens another (Chat's "Open Library"), the new pane shows a "‹ Chat" chip back; on a phone the back chevron says "‹ Chat" and returns there. The rail marks the pane you used last, and Esc closes a docked pane when focus is inside it.
- **The open pane is easy to spot on the Studio rail.** Its icon now sits on a solid chip in the theme's heading color with a light label, the same look as the Read/Write/Craft switch. The old pale tint was almost the same tone as the rail. Measured in all 18 palettes in light and dark: the chip stands out from the rail by at least 9.88:1, and its label reads at 9.65:1 or better.
- **Compose's caret stays on its text when a pane opens or closes.** While the editor isn't focused, Compose draws its caret; it used to stay where the text had been when the column changed width, and could show outside the editor. It now moves with the text in the same frame and is clipped to the editor.
- **Every side pane has the same header.** Coach, Chat, Live, Reader views, Library and Slide and Deck settings now share one header on desktop and tablet: the pane's icon, its name, its own buttons, and a collapse button on the edge it folds toward. Four panes used to show only a small uppercase label with no way to close them from inside, and Settings showed its scope ("Slide 1 override") where its name belonged; that scope line now sits at the top of the pane. Search works the same everywhere: a search button that opens the field under the header, focused, and Esc closes it. On a phone, panes keep their back chevron and the Library keeps its search at the bottom.
- **The Studio rail's labels are readable on a laptop.** The words under the rail's icons are larger (10px, from 8.5px), in the body text color instead of gray, and the icons are a little smaller and lighter, so the word is no longer lost under the icon. Read/Write/Craft gets slightly larger words and lighter icons for the same reason.
- **Slide and Deck settings each have a Reset button in their header.** It puts back what you changed since you opened the pane (the slide, or the deck's settings), as one step you can undo. It stays off until you change something.
