- The exported HTML player no longer cuts the slide's edge off at every corner (seen in a phone's file preview, which runs the player without its script), and no longer rounds the slides of a square deck in its Read·Slides and Present views. Every surface that shows a slide — the player, the Playground, the Studio (editor preview, Present, thumbnails, pickers, Fabricate) and the docs site — now frames it with one shared slide frame (`lib/core/slide-frame.mjs`) that follows the deck's own `corners:` setting, square or rounded, instead of drawing a corner of its own.
