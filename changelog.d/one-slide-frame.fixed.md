- The exported HTML player no longer cuts the slide's edge off at every corner (seen in a phone's file preview, which runs the player without its script), and no longer rounds the slides of a square deck in its Read·Slides and Present views. Every surface that shows a slide — the player, the Playground, the Studio (editor preview, Present, thumbnails, pickers, Fabricate) and the docs site — now follows the deck's own `corners:` setting instead of drawing a corner of its own.
- The slide's 1px edge is now drawn by the engine, in the deck's own border color, above the slide's content and inside its corner, so no host can clip it and no full-bleed layout can cover it. It yields to the spectrum bar on whichever side the bar sits. It appears only where a slide is shown on a page; PDF, PNG and PPTX exports are unchanged.
- A title's text no longer shifts a beat after the exported player opens: the player's embedded fonts are laid out from the first paint. In the Studio, fonts that arrive after the preview's 1.5s wait now fade in rather than making the text jump.
