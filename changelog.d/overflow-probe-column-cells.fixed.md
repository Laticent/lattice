- **Fixed: a clip cell that lays its children out in COLUMNS no longer reports phantom overflow.** `probeSectionOverflow` measures a possibly-squeezed child against "the next flowed sibling's top", which assumes the children stack. In a two-track grid or a flex row the next sibling sits *beside*, so the subtraction returns the child's own height. Measured on `team-profile sides`, where a genuinely squeezed 9-person roster hiding 261px was reported as 699px — 2.7× the truth, and that number feeds autosplit — and on a migrated `math matrix` slide, where 2px of phantom inner overflow from KaTeX's `vlist` struts became a 265px reading and a "Content clipped" badge with nothing clipped.
- The limit is now taken only from a sibling that **overlaps this child horizontally and starts below it**; the scan continues past one that does not, rather than stopping at it. Both halves were wrong alone, and both were measured: stopping at the first sibling silently missed 170px of overprint onto a later same-column box, and scanning without the horizontal test reached a `citation-card margin` list 640px to the right and took its top as a limit.
- Tolerances are in layout px (`K`-scaled) rather than visual px. Unscaled, the same slide answered `over: true` at scale 1 and `over: false` at scale 1.5, contradicting the scale-independence the probe asserts elsewhere.
- Blast radius measured across 232 rendered slides from nine galleries: exactly one verdict changes, the `math matrix` false positive. `citation-card`, `inventory`, `redline`, `split-panel` and `team-profile` all build multi-column clip cells and none moves.
