- A theme rule that leads with `:where(section…)` now applies in the Studio and Playground preview. `:where(section.finish) .panel` used to match nothing there while the exported deck applied it, so the preview and the export disagreed. The preview now writes the slide arm and keeps the descendant arm it always had, so a raw `<section>` nested in a slide still matches too. Measured on a fixture theme: a single-arm rule went from `outline-width` 0px to 3px (the export gives 3px) and a multi-arm rule from 0px to 4px (export 4px); a rule that hits a nested raw section stays at 9px. It applies only when every arm is one compound that starts with `section`; an arm with a combinator inside it (`:where(section .wrap) .d`), a mixed list such as `:where(section.a, .b)`, and `:where(:root)` stay as before. The packed and flat sheets for all 33 shipped themes are byte-identical before and after, because no shipped selector uses the shape.
