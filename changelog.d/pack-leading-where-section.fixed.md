- A theme rule that leads with `:where(section…)` now applies in the Studio and Playground preview. `:where(section.finish) .panel` used to match nothing there while the exported deck applied it, so the preview and the export disagreed. The preview now writes it as `article.lattice > :where(section.finish) .panel`, keeping the zero-weight filter the author wrote. Measured on a fixture theme: a single-arm rule went from `outline-width` 0px to 3px (the CLI's sheet gives 3px) and a multi-arm rule from 0px to 4px (CLI 4px). It applies only when every arm is one compound that starts with `section`; an arm with a combinator inside it (`:where(section .wrap) .d`), a mixed list such as `:where(section.a, .b)`, and `:where(:root)` stay as before, and the `css-scope` test still flags the mixed case. The packed and flat sheets for all 33 shipped themes are byte-identical before and after, because no shipped selector uses the shape.
