- The Studio's Compose editor edits a `columns` / `rows` slide's panes as fields. Each pane opens
  with a bar that names its place ("Left pane 60%") and holds a picker of the components that fit
  there. Its `###` title reads as a labeled field, and a pane with no title gets an "Add title"
  button. Picking a component writes the pane's `<!-- _pane: … -->` marker in place.
