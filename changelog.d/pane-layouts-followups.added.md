- The Studio's Compose editor edits a `columns` / `rows` slide's panes. Each pane opens with a
  bar that names its place ("Left pane 60%") and what it holds. "Change" opens the slide gallery,
  showing only the components that fit that pane, and each tile says what picking it does: "Keeps
  your text", or "Starts with an example", which swaps the pane's body for the component's starter
  and offers Undo. The pane's `###` title reads as a labeled field, and a pane with no title gets
  an "Add title" button.
