- **Panes (experimental — the syntax may change)** — put two components' body content on one slide, side by side or stacked, under the slide's one title and chrome. Mark each body with `<!-- pane: <component> -->`; set the split with `<!-- panes: 40/60 -->` (25–75 in 5% steps) or `<!-- panes: stack 35/65 -->`. The Key Insight, below-note, header, footer and page number stay the slide's. Decks without pane markers render byte-identical markup. Demo: `examples/panes.md`.
- Panes carry a **spine** between them — the accent rule that separates a chart from its key — vertical side by side, horizontal when stacked. Add `no-rule` to the `panes:` marker to drop it.
- Every component declares how it behaves in a pane — a `pane` field in its manifest, also in `dist/docs/components.json`: `fit` (`half`, `wide` for 65%+ or a stack, `none` to opt out), a pane `form` (`image` renders as `content`), `stack: false` for `kpi` and `pricing`, and a pane **budget** — measured with `tools/calibrate-capacity.js --pane` where it can be, judged and explained where it cannot.
- `lint:deck` checks each pane before you render: `pane-fit` (an opted-out component, a `wide` one under 65%, a side-by-side-only one stacked), `pane-overflow` and `pane-crowd` (past the pane's budget, counted per pane). A panes slide's bullet ceiling in `wall-of-text` is now per pane.
