- The Studio preview follows a panes slide the engine splits into one slide per pane (every panes slide on a square, portrait, story or mobile deck). The slide after the split now shows with the page number the PDF gives it, instead of falling back to the slide alone numbered 1 of 1. The split slide shows the page whose pane holds the caret, and ‹ › step through both pages. Present and the narration bake keep their projected narration through a split.
- Author and package CSS reaches a pane: a rule written for a component (`section.my-card li { … }`) now styles a pane of that component as it styles its slide, for installed packages, Studio saved components and finishes, and `<style>` blocks in the deck. A component used only in a pane now ships its CSS with the deck.
- A table pane in a `mode: sketch` deck is hand-inked like a table slide. It used to lose its frame and row rules entirely once the slide's heading was inked. A pane that overflows is tagged at the item that overflows, not as a whole.
