- **Studio PDF:** an inline pill no longer draws over the end of the caption before it (kpi `OPS` / `GAP` pills, in light and dark). The Studio's camera re-lays a slide out with every font size rounded down, so a pill placed by the text flow was photographed early while its caption was drawn in place. The shared PDF writer now draws such a chip's fill and border as vectors at the live position and leaves it out of the photo, in the Studio and the CLI alike. In CLI PDFs a pill looks the same, with a sharper edge; a chip under a link underline, a shadow or anything painted over it stays in the photo as before.
