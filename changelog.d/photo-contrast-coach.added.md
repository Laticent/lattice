- **The Studio Coach warns when text on a photo will not read.** With the Coach open, the slide on screen is measured in the live preview: the photo behind the card, the card's fill over it, and the ink on top. A spotlight or statement slide whose text falls below 4.5:1 gets a `photo-contrast` finding, for example dark ink on a dark photo under a thin card. When pinning the slide dark or light would clear the floor, the finding carries a **Pin this slide dark** (or **light**) button that writes the class. Nothing flips on its own, a pin that would not clear 4.5:1 is not offered, and a photo the browser cannot read (a cross-origin image) gets no warning rather than a guess. The finding is outside the deck's Craft and Style grade. The arithmetic is `lib/core/photo-contrast.mjs`, built on the same contrast function as `thin-card-no-ink`.
- **The `thin-card-no-ink` tooltip has a fix button that picks the ink from the photo.** Hovering the warning on a thin spotlight card with no ink now offers the one ink that reads (**Add ink-light** over a dark photo, **Add ink-dark** over a light one), It never offers both. The warning appears at once; the photo is checked when you open it (a **Checking the photo…** note shows meanwhile), from the live preview when it shows that slide and from the photo file otherwise. The answer is kept in the browser, so it survives a reload and is not worked out again. When neither ink reads (a mid-gray photo under a clear card) or the photo cannot be read (a relative path, a cross-origin image), the tooltip says so and offers **Use a solid card** instead, which always reads.
- **On a phone, the Source tab's live preview now follows the caret to another slide.** It draws once per slide crossing (never per keystroke), so the editor's tooltips can read the photo on the slide you are editing. `DeckPreview` has a new `followWhenParked` option, off by default.
