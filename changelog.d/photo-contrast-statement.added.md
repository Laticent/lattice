- **The Coach's photo-contrast check now covers statement slides.** A statement's white words sit on a scrim; with `card-clear` the scrim is gone and the words can vanish on a bright photo. The Coach now flags that slide and offers **Set card ink dark** when the dark ink clears 4.5:1. It judges a statement against its scrim at its strongest, so a statement the scrim already rescues is never flagged, and when a partial scrim is on it says the text reads "at most" the ratio shown.
- **Fixed: the Studio editor's "spark too big" warning now shows when the slide comes into view, not on the next keystroke.** The editor re-ran its lint pass on a new preview measurement with `forceLinting`, which does nothing to an idle linter. It now dispatches its own re-lint effect.
- **Fixed: a one-click edit to the slide on screen no longer sends the preview to the last slide.** A Slide-panel row or a Coach fix rewrote the whole deck and dropped its final newline, which moved the editor's caret to the end. The edit now changes only that slide.
- **The editor and the Coach now give one answer about text on a photo.** Both show the same message and the same fix button, read once from the slide on screen. A statement the Coach flags is also underlined in the editor, with the same fix. On a thin card, the fix both views offer is the card ink that reads (or a solid card), because a pin would leave the editor's thin-card warning standing. The tooltip's button now reads **Set card ink light** (it said **Add ink-light**). A Coach warning no longer lingers for a moment after you move to another slide.
