- The Playground preview now splits a portrait, square or strip deck the way the PDF export does. The structural auto-split (a slide with several rows becomes a cover, one page per row, and a closing page) used to run only in the CLI export, so a `size: portrait` deck showed one tall page per slide in the Playground and one page per row in its PDF. Both now run the same preprocessor, `lib/core/structural-split.js`: your gallery deck at portrait renders 43 pages in both. Landscape decks are unchanged, and Studio exports are unchanged. The component tour keeps one step per authored slide when a slide splits.
- The Studio preview now follows the caret through a split slide. At portrait or square, a slide that splits shows the page holding the line you are editing: the cover while you are in the heading, a row's own page while you are in its bullet, the last page for the insight. Blank lines keep the page you were on. A page pill under the preview reads `2.3 · 3 of 5`. The frame still holds one page, and the rail, comments and slide actions stay keyed to authored slides. Swipe, the arrow keys, the wheel and the ‹ › buttons step through a split slide's pages before they move to the next slide, and stepping back enters a split slide on its last page. The slide navigator under the preview keeps the current slide in view: it scrolls to center the slide when the deck moves, and holds still while you page through a split slide.
- A split slide's cover no longer jumps just after it appears in the Playground and the Studio. The live preview now paints the cover in its final layout instead of letting the runtime rearrange it after the first frame.
- A split slide's cover no longer shows a stray page number in the middle of the slide in the Playground and the Studio. The live runtime re-ran the frame lift on the cover the split had already composed, which swept its footer into the body and added a second one. The lift now leaves a section that already carries its footer cell alone, in both the runtime and the render-time kernel. The CLI export is unchanged: identical HTML and all 94 compared PNG pages pixel-identical.
