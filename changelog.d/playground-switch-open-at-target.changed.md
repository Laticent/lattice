- Switching between Edit and Explore in the Playground no longer flickers. After you edit a slide, the preview used to show the old preview, then a blank, then the deck at slide 1, then jump to your slide; it now shows nothing until the deck is on your slide, and it patches the preview you already have instead of reloading it when the deck has not changed. On a phone with a 60-slide deck and a throttled CPU, the switch takes about 380 ms instead of about 900 ms.
