- **Fixed: `premise` no longer cuts off ordinary terms.** The term column was a fixed 140px, so any term past about eleven characters ended in an ellipsis — `Communication` rendered as `Communic…`, and `Prioritization` and `Accountability` did the same. The ledger is now one grid whose term column sizes itself to the longest term on the slide, so every description still starts on the same line. The claim rail narrows from 34% toward a 26% floor when the rows need the width. When a row's description and framing question no longer fit on one line, the question drops under the description instead of either being cut. Only a term wider than 40% of the ledger still ends in an ellipsis.
- `premise` spaces its rows by count: three rows get a loose gap and taller rows, four or five get a medium gap, six or seven a snug one, and eight keep the tight rhythm. A short ledger no longer floats as a thin strip in a tall slide. Square, portrait and mobile slides are unchanged — they keep their stacked layout, pixel for pixel.
