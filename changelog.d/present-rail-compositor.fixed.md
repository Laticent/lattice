- **Studio Present:** the progress rail and the caption highlight no longer cost a style, layout and paint cycle on every frame while a deck plays. The played range animated `width`; it now moves by `transform`, which the compositor runs without the main thread, and a word lights the moment it is spoken instead of fading in over 150 ms. On the built Studio at 3840x2160 with the CPU slowed 4x, playback went from 146 layouts and about 2.5 s of main-thread work per 3 s to 20 and about 1.5 s. The rail looks the same, apart from a slightly different sliver for the first few pixels of a slide's fill.
