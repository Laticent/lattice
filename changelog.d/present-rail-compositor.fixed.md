- **Studio Present:** the progress rail and the caption highlight no longer cost a style, layout and paint cycle on every frame while a deck plays. The played range and the playhead moved by animating `width` and `left`; they now move by `transform`, which the compositor runs without the main thread, and a word lights the moment it is spoken instead of fading in over 150 ms. On the built Studio at 3840x2160 with the CPU slowed 4x, playback went from 148 layouts and about 2.5 s of main-thread work per 3 s to none and about 1.7 s. The rail looks the same.
