- **Present changes the moment you press Stage.** The presenter view, the pressed Stage pill and the talk clock arrive in the frame of the press, and the caption band and the progress rail leave the console in that same frame, appearing on the Stage once its window is live. While the Stage loads a change (a lens, a palette), they stay off the console rather than coming back for a second. They used to wait about a second for the Stage window to load, and the Next arrow then jumped across the screen under the pointer, so a click aimed at it could be lost.
- **A Stage that cannot show the deck retries, then closes and says so.** Pressing Stage after the deck failed to render builds it again; if it fails again, the window cannot take it, or the deck never finishes starting (20 seconds), the Stage window closes with a notice and Present keeps its captions and rail. It used to stay on "Preparing the stage…".
