- **Present changes the moment you press Stage.** The presenter view, the pressed Stage pill and the talk clock arrive in the frame of the press, and the caption band and the progress rail leave the console in that same frame, appearing on the Stage once its window is live. They used to wait about a second for the Stage window to load, and the Next arrow then jumped across the screen under the pointer, so a click aimed at it could be lost.
- **A Stage that cannot show the deck closes and says so.** If the deck fails to render, or the window cannot take it, the Stage window closes with a notice and Present keeps its captions and rail; it used to stay on "Preparing the stage…".
