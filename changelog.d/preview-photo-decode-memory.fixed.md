- The Studio, the Playground filmstrip and the Stage window no longer force every loading photo to decode at full size. Each photo's placeholder now waits for the photo to load rather than decode, so a picture that is not on screen, such as one in a hidden preview, is no longer held as a full-size bitmap. On WebKit, a 12-megapixel photo is about 48 MB decoded; ten such photos that were loaded but not shown measured 531 MB before and 66 MB after. This mostly helps decks with many photos on an iPhone or iPad.
