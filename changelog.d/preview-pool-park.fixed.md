- Closed Studio panels with slide previews no longer hold on to their last slides' photos. This covers the deck settings, Present's slide overview, the Print preview and Add slide. These panels stay loaded once opened, and each kept its last screenful of slides, photos included, for the rest of the session. A preview with no tile to show for 5 seconds now switches to a blank slide, which frees its photos. Previews whose tiles scrolled away but stayed in an open panel also now give up their place once their short grace period ends; before, nothing released them until something else changed.
