- `progress-rail:` front-matter key: place the section rail beside the page number (`right`, the default), in the middle of the footer (`center`) or upright on the right edge (`edge`), and draw it as `dots` or a segmented `bar`. One line, one word per axis, e.g. `progress-rail: edge bar`. `off` hides it, the same as `class: no-progress`. The Studio completes each word, and the Deck Inspector sets it under Chrome → Section rail. A slide moves its own rail with `_class: progress-rail-edge`.
- The section rail no longer draws on a deck with only one `divider`. It was a single lit pill beside every page number and told the reader nothing.
- The section rail now draws on each `divider` slide, lit on the section that slide opens, so a reader meets the marks next to the section's name. A divider's running footer now ends in an ellipsis before the rail instead of wrapping under it.
- The rail's current mark is in the heading's ink instead of the accent, the same cue the topic track uses for its current topic. A hairline now separates the rail from the page number, so the number no longer reads as one more mark.
