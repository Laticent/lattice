- **A prose slide that overflows a phone or tablet slide splits between its paragraphs.** A `content` slide with no list or table had no place to split, so on a square, portrait, story or mobile slide its paragraphs ran off the bottom. It now splits between its top-level blocks (paragraphs, a code block, a quote or a figure among them) by measured height, with the heading on a cover page; a subheading always starts a page with the paragraph it introduces, and a run of pages points to the next one. A slide of one paragraph and a key insight puts its heading on a cover and keeps the insight with the paragraph when they fit together, or gives the insight its own page when they do not. This happens only when the split measures (the default); `fit: heal` keeps a prose slide whole, because paragraphs have no count to cut by. A single paragraph taller than the page still clips.
