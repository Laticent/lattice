- The Studio preview no longer shows an image slide in a provisional layout and then jumps when its picture arrives: the first reveal waits for the picture's size, up to 4 seconds, and a later arrival fades through instead of jumping.
- Tapping a video's poster in the Studio preview plays the clip again, on desktop and touch. The preview's gesture layer had been swallowing the tap.
- In an exported player, a slide's web link (a video poster, a contact or closing URL) now opens in a new tab. Before, it replaced the player in the same tab and the reader lost the deck.
- A video slide with a light poster no longer shows a near-black tile while the poster loads on a light slide: the tile under a loading poster is the slide's own surface. The no-poster placeholder keeps its dark video tile.
- An image from a site you allowed in the Studio no longer stays blank when that site redirects to one of its own subdomains (picsum.photos to fastly.picsum.photos, for example). A redirect to an unrelated site is still blocked.
- Turning to an image slide in the Studio preview no longer flashes the photo across the whole slide before it settles: the preview measures the deck's photos ahead of time and draws each slide in its final layout from the first frame. A photo that cannot load now shows the hatched placeholder instead of an empty panel.
- Changing slides in the Studio preview no longer paints the text in one spot and then moves it into place a moment later: each slide arrives already laid out. The Playground's whole-deck preview gets the same fix when it swaps a slide in.
- An image slide's text in the Studio and Playground previews now sits in the same column as in the export. The preview had been drawing the text card twice, one inside the other, which indented the text and added a second accent bar.
- In the Studio and Playground previews, an image slide whose photo is still loading now shows a loading placeholder and holds its text back until the photo's size is known. The text then appears once, in its final place, instead of moving when the photo arrives.
