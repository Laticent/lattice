- **`--read`** exports a deck as its reading article — the prose, with real headings, paragraphs and lists, and charts and tables re-hosted as figures — instead of the slide stack. This is the form a reader mode or a "summarize this page" feature can actually read: a slide stack is headings and short list items inside `<section>` elements, which those tools do not score as article text, so most decks were invisible to them. Instead of, not beside — a page carrying both copies would feed a summarizer the same deck twice. The PDF, PPTX and PNG outputs are byte-identical either way, it composes with `--captions` and `--notes`, and it is also available as a `read: true` front-matter key.
