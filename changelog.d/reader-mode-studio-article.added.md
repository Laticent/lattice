- The Studio can show the active deck as an article. Run **Read as an article** from the command palette (⌘K) and the deck's prose renders as real headings and paragraphs in the page itself, with a table of contents. That is also what makes "summarize this page" work on a deck — Firefox's shake-to-summarize, Safari Reader and the Edge/Chrome reading modes only read the top-level document, and every other slide surface here lives inside an iframe they never look into.
