- **A redline clause longer than a phone page splits between its clauses.** A statute's amendment is often one long sentence, and a split could only break between sentences, so on a square phone slide the clause ran off the page. A redline may now also break after a semicolon, or after a comma before "and", "or" or "but", never inside a deletion or an insertion and never between a deletion and its replacement. Quotes and citation cards still break only between sentences.
