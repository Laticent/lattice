- **The chat's `render_slide` look runs past its 15 s budget far less often on a busy phone.** Two things slowed it. On a Full device, the Studio's background warm-up (the Compose, Present and Read panels, then every vendored font) started during the chat's answer and its render and competed with both; it now waits until the chat turn has finished. And the slide camera fetched and embedded 16 font files for a title slide that draws four faces, every weight of each family in a font stack, twice over; it now leaves out the weights we ship that the slide does not draw, and embeds 4. Measured on a loaded WebKit phone profile with Full forced, 200 looks each on the same machine: renders over budget fell from 11 to 2, the median from 10.9 s to 9.3 s and the slowest 1% from 18.3 s to 13.6 s. The two that still ran over had no warm-up beside them; the machine itself was starved. The look's picture came out byte-identical for slides with emoji, every text weight and style, math, icons and photos. An author's own font files, and any deck with a `::first-line` or `::first-letter` rule, keep every face as before. Exports are unchanged.
