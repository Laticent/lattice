- **The chat's `render_slide` look no longer runs past its 15 s budget on a busy phone.** Two things slowed it. On a Full device, the Studio's background warm-up (the Compose, Present and Read panels, then every vendored font) started about 2 s into the render and competed with it; it now waits until the render has finished. And the slide camera fetched and embedded 16 font files for a title slide that draws four faces, every weight of each family in a font stack, twice over; it now leaves out the weights we ship that the slide does not draw, and embeds 4. Measured on a loaded WebKit phone profile with Full forced, the look's median fell from 9.3 s to 7.1 s and its worst case from 18.5 s to 14.2 s, with no run over budget in 50. The look's picture came out byte-identical for slides with emoji, every text weight and style, math, icons and photos. An author's own font files, and any deck with a `::first-line` or `::first-letter` rule, keep every face as before. Exports are unchanged.
