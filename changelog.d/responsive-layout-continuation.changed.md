- **matrix-2x2 stacks in a narrow box and keeps each quadrant's place.** Below 24em of the content area the 2×2 becomes one column instead of squeezing each quadrant to a word per line, and every stacked card carries a small drawn 2×2 mark with its own cell filled. A portrait or mobile deck's single column carries the same mark. 16:9 slides are unchanged.
- **redline stacks in a narrow pane.** A `.three-col` redline below 40em of its box, or a `.split` one below 32em, stacks old, new and the rationale in reading order instead of squeezing each column to a word per line. On a slide, a stack that would overflow is held as the grid. Full-width slides are unchanged.
- **`lint:deck` no longer warns `pane-insight` on a paned redline.** It counted a redline's old and new blockquotes as Key Insights, though the pane carve keeps them in the pane; it now reads the same coda catalog the carve does.
- **Charts no longer vanish in a continuous host.** radar, map, quadrant, word-cloud, piechart and hub-spoke fill a size container, which lends no height when the slide grows to its content; their chart body now takes a 16:10 box there. Slides, PDFs and every other paged output are unchanged.
