- Print, PDF, PowerPoint and image exports of decks with charts are faster and no longer freeze the page for up to 3 s. Exports used to re-style every chart before photographing each slide, a workaround the current slide camera no longer needs. In WebKit, the engine behind every iPhone browser, a nine-heatmap deck prints in 57.6 s instead of 68.6 s and the 28-chart test deck's PDF takes 19 s instead of 38 s (measured in Playwright's WebKit, not on a device), and in Chromium the longest freeze during a PDF or PowerPoint export drops from about 3 s to under 1 s. Chart edges in the exported pictures are anti-aliased slightly differently; colors, sizes and layout are unchanged. The standalone chart `.svg` files in the Images (.zip) export now declare their own size (for example 539 × 225) instead of the slide's capture size, so a chart fills its file's frame instead of floating small in a wide empty box, as the CLI's and the single-chart SVG export already did.
