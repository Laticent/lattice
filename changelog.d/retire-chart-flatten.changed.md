- Print, PDF, PowerPoint and image exports of decks with charts are faster and no longer freeze the page for up to 3 s. Exports used to re-style every chart before photographing each slide, a workaround the current slide camera no longer needs. On an iPhone a nine-heatmap deck prints in 57.6 s instead of 68.6 s. In WebKit the 28-chart test deck's PDF takes 19 s instead of 38 s, and in Chromium the longest freeze during a PDF or PowerPoint export drops from about 3 s to under 1 s. Chart edges in the exported files are anti-aliased slightly differently; colors, sizes and layout are unchanged.
