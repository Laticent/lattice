- **`lint:deck` no longer names the replacement for four classes retired in September and October 2026: `chart-finish-off`, `no-paginate`, `compare-table` and `list-criteria`.** They were already removed from the engine; a deck that still uses one now gets the general `unknown-class` warning instead of a targeted "renamed to" hint. Their replacements are `marks-pigment`, `no-pagination`, `table` and `list takeaway`. Retiring the hints gives back 0.35 KB of the Studio's lint code, which the image-card rename (#3415) needs.
