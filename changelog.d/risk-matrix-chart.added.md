- **New `risk-matrix` chart for risk registers.** Write each risk as its name and `{likelihood, impact}` (1 to 5), with an optional second record for where mitigation takes it. The chart draws a 5 × 5 grid shaded low, medium, high or critical by likelihood × impact, places each risk as a numbered marker (an arrow and a dashed ring show the planned move), and lists the risks worst first beside the grid. Narration reads them worst first in words ("likelihood likely, impact critical, rated critical"). Demo deck: `examples/risk-matrix.md`.
