- **The player runtime and the engine bundle no longer carry the web-reference scanner.** `lattice-runtime-min.js` (the script every exported deck loads) bundled all of `remote-ref.js` for one constant, the blocked-image placeholder, and `sampleName`/`assetName`. Those now come from `media-sources.js`, the leaf the scanner already depended on. Measured with the build's own esbuild settings (`tools/build-runtime.js` minified options) against the merge base: the minified runtime is about 7 to 9 KB gzipped smaller, and about 8 KB gzipped smaller than the same runtime with the scanner's new checks.
