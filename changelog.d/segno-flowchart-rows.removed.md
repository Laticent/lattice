- **Breaking:** `lib/core/flowchart-grammar.js` no longer exports `readArrow` or `LABEL_MAX`. Flowchart and state-chart rows are now read by a parser that Segno generates from `lib/core/flowchart-row-grammar.js`, and `splitRow` returns the same parts and arrows as before, without the arrow's `end` offset. To read arrows, call `splitRow`. The label cap is that file's `LABEL_MAX`, which counts the label itself (61 characters); the old export was 60 and was used as the cap minus one.
