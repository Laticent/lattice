- **Breaking:** `lib/core/state-marks.js` no longer exports `LEADING_MARKER_RE` or `LEADING_MARKER_PREFIX_RE`, `lib/core/matrix-grid-cells.js` no longer exports `CELL_MARKER`, and `lib/core/table-row-label.js` no longer exports `MARKER_CELL`. A parser that Segno generates from `lib/core/list-text-grammar.js` now reads leading state markers, matrix-grid cells and `_track`, and decks render as before. To read a marker, call `readLeadingMarker(text)` from the new `lib/core/leading-marker.js` (`{ marker, rest }`) or `leadingMarkerPrefix(text)` (`{ marker, length }`); `isMarkerCell(text)` replaces `MARKER_CELL.test`, and `parseCell` and `parseTrackSpec` keep their signatures.
