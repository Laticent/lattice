- **Added: the Studio's deck and slide settings panels can be searched, and can
  drop their tabs for one continuous list.** Two controls sit at the top right of
  both panels. **Search** filters every control across all sections at once, so
  "pagination" finds the Page numbers row without opening the Chrome tab to look
  for it; opening the field hides the view toggle and takes the whole row, which
  is the width a phone needs. **View** switches between `Grouped` (the pill-tabbed
  panel, still the default) and `List` (every section down one scroll under its
  own sticky heading). The view choice persists and is one choice for both scopes.
