- **The Studio's Share → PDF and the CLI's PDF export now share one writer** (`lib/core/pdf-compose`). Each slide's background is one photo; words, charts, images and links go on top as real text in the deck's fonts, vector shapes, original images and clickable links, tagged for screen readers with the title and language. It draws several times faster in iOS Preview and Acrobat (a 9-slide finish deck: 13.1 s → 1.45 s in poppler, 335 KB → 237 KB). `--chrome-pdf` prints with Chrome as before; in the Studio, Workspace › General › PDF export › Photo per page keeps one picture per slide. Links to another slide (`#3`) become page jumps, and tabular figures stay tabular.
- **`color-mode: print` no longer draws a finish**, on screen or in any export: the black-and-white handout carries no glow, texture or margin bar.
- **Studio exports keep a slide's tone rail and finish frame** (`tone-warn`, `tone-fail` …): the PDF, PNG and PPTX exports dropped them, while the CLI kept them.
