- On a phone, a tap on the deck above a panel now closes it even in the panel's first moment on screen. A Library opened for the first time could ignore that tap: its real sheet replaces its loading sheet in place, the dialog library starts listening for outside taps only a moment after mount, and in iOS Safari the finger can lift on a different backdrop than it went down on, so Safari sends no click at all. The tap is now read from the finger going down and up on the backdrop, and goes through the same keep-open rules as any outside tap.
