- **On a `mode: sketch` slide, a framed icon's tile is drawn by hand**, like the spark beside it. The edge is inked in the icon's own color, a heavier line on the default pigment look, whose filled tile would hide a thin one. The drawing inside stays crisp, a bare icon has no tile to ink, and header and footer icons stay clean. The ink frame is square even on an `icon-rounded` tile, as a spark's is. Slides that are not sketch render as before.
