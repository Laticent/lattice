- Added `lib/core/slide-absorption.mjs` — an instrument that measures how much is on a rendered slide (prose words vs. scanned labels, chart marks, table cells, CSS-background photos, Mermaid nodes and edges estimated from source) and prices how long it needs to be looked at, with a spend rule that charges an exit hold as a residual against the narration that actually happened.
- Added `tools/absorption-bakeoff.js` — scores competing cost models over real decks against a two-line flat control, reporting what each buys against the silence it adds.
- **Fed the real narration, the instrument reports that no extra time is needed.** A `diagram` slide narrates for 40.6 s and then advances; it was never starved. The premise that motivated this work came from timing one stage of a multi-stage narration pipeline — see §0 of `engineering/decisions/2026-09-06-slide-absorption-bakeoff.md`.
- Nothing is wired to the player: no deck renders, exports, or narrates differently.
