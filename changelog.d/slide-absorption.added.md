- Added `lib/core/slide-absorption.mjs` — a content-weight model for narration timing: it measures how much is on a rendered slide (prose words, labels, chart marks, diagram nodes and edges) and prices how long the slide needs to be looked at. Five competing cost models share one spend rule, which splits the cost into an arrival beat and an exit hold charged as a *residual* against what the narration already covered — so a wordy slide costs nothing extra and a wordless diagram gets its time back.
- Added `tools/absorption-bakeoff.js` — scores the five models over real decks and prints what each would do to the beats. On-demand diagnostic, not a gate.
- `MEDIA_COMPONENTS` (`lib/transformers/prose-projection.mjs`) and `ROLE_MULT` / `TABLE_COMPS` (`docs/src/components/studio/present/rehearsal.js`) are now exported, so the absorption kernel's copies are pinned against them by identity rather than by scraping their source. No behavior change.
- Nothing is wired to the player yet: no deck renders, exports, or narrates differently. Design record, measured comparison, and the nine defects an independent checker found in the first cut: `engineering/decisions/2026-09-06-slide-absorption-bakeoff.md`.
