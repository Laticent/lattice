- Added `lib/core/slide-absorption.mjs` — a content-weight instrument for narration timing. It measures how much is on a rendered slide (prose words vs. scanned labels, chart marks, table cells, Mermaid nodes and edges estimated from source where no browser has drawn one) and prices how long the slide needs to be *looked* at. Six competing cost models share one spend rule that splits the cost into an arrival beat, paid from the look channel only, and an exit hold charged as a residual against narration credited per channel — so a wordy slide costs nothing extra and a wordless diagram gets its time back.
- Added `tools/absorption-bakeoff.js` — scores the models over real decks and reports what each buys (`visual exit`, `visual w/ beat`) against what it costs (`ADDED`, split arrive/exit), alongside a two-line flat control any model has to beat.
- `MEDIA_COMPONENTS` (`lib/transformers/prose-projection.mjs`) and `ROLE_MULT` / `TABLE_COMPS` (`docs/src/components/studio/present/rehearsal.js`) are now exported so the kernel's copies are pinned by identity rather than by scraping source. No behavior change.
- Nothing is wired to the player: no deck renders, exports, or narrates differently. The record does **not** pick a cost model — on the corrected measurement a two-line control beats every measured one.
