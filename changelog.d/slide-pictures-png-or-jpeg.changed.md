- A `.pptx` or `.odp` slide picture is now a JPEG (quality 92) when the slide is a photograph or a soft gradient wash and that is under 70% of the PNG's size, and stays a lossless PNG otherwise. A 35-slide photo deck went from 76.6 MB to 16 MB, and from 44 s to 15.5 s in the CLI, because the slow PNG screenshot of a photograph is no longer taken. The Studio's PowerPoint export follows the same rule (8 photo slides: 38.2 MB and 7.4 s to 8.9 MB and 5.3 s). Flat slides are unchanged, byte for byte; a gradient title slide may become a JPEG.
