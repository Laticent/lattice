- A `.pptx` or `.odp` slide picture is now a JPEG (quality 92) when the slide is a photograph and that is under 70% of the PNG's size, and stays a lossless PNG otherwise. A 35-slide photo deck went from 76.6 MB to 15 MB (CLI `.pptx`; the Studio and `.odp` follow the same rule). Flat slides are unchanged, byte for byte. Export time rises by about a quarter on photo decks, because a photo slide is photographed twice.
