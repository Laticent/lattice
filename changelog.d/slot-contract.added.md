- `lint:deck` now checks every slide against its layout's slot contract (`slot-contract`, an error). A required heading, list, blockquote or table that is missing, a heading written at the wrong level (`#` where the layout reads `##`), and a list written with a marker the layout does not read (`agenda` with `-`, `journey` with `1.`) are reported. Before, `lint:deck --strict` passed a slide with its required heading deleted on 67 of 69 layouts; now it passes none.
- Each manifest slot carries a structured `match` (what the author writes, in Markdown terms) that `lint:deck` executes, and a variant can change a slot's shape with `variantSlots` (`statute-stack lane` takes a table, `split-panel pullquote` anchors on a blockquote). The component guides list each slot as what to write, not as a CSS selector.
- `lint:deck` warns (`variant-inert`) on a variant that does nothing as written: `numbered` on `cards-grid` and `cards-stack`, whose numbers come from a `1.` list.
- `quote` styles only the paragraph right after the quotation as its attribution. A later paragraph renders as body text instead of a second credit in tracked capitals.
- `closing` and `divider` declare `#` as well as `##` for their heading, `agenda` declares its list optional (it fills itself from the dividers), and `glossary`, `image`, `math` and `table` declare the other forms they already render (a table, `@image{…}`, a ```` ```math ```` fence, a raw `<table>`).
