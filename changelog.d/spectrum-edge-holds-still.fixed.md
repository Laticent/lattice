- Choosing where the spectrum bar sits (`spectrum-edge: top|left|right|bottom|off`, or the matching per-slide class) no longer moves the slide's content or chrome. The bar used to take its width out of the content, so a `left` bar pushed the heading and cards 4px right and narrowed them, and a `bottom` bar shortened the content area. The header, footer, page number, draft/confidential stamps, `mark-*` decorations and the overflow marker moved with it. Each side's padding now gives up exactly the bar it carries, so every placement leaves the same content box and the same chrome position. It holds for every component, its manifest variants, the modifiers `footer-inset`, `fill-optical`, `claim-bleed`, `claim-hero` and `safe`, and every class the engine reserves. Centered logos (`logo-finish: top-center`, `bottom-center`) and `![bg]` background photos hold still too: a background now measures from the border box. A `finish:` backdrop still ends where the bar begins, since a backdrop reaching under the bar would hide it.
- A default deck looks as it did, except `premise`, whose content sits 2px higher (1.5px on a narrow canvas): it has no bottom padding of its own, so it now reserves one bar width there.
- The bar no longer shoves content when something adds or removes it. These used to move the slide by the bar's width and now leave it where the unmarked slide sits: `accent` and `tone-edge` slides (chart-family and `topic` slides 4px higher; `title`, `closing` and `divider` 2px higher); a deck-wide `spectrum: solid`, `duo` or `mono` (every dark, chart-family and `topic` slide 4px higher, `title` and `closing` 2px higher); and `spectrum: off`, which now keeps the 4px a default slide has at the top, so ordinary slides sit 4px lower than they did.
- The full-bleed layouts `image`, `scene`, `split-panel`, `split-compare`, a `claim-bleed` chart, diagram, video or big-number, and `collage bleed` now draw the spectrum bar as two thin overlay strips (placement and top) laid over the slide instead of a border. A border there shrank the photo or panel by the bar's width; the overlay takes no room, so those frames keep their true edge under every placement and the bar paints above the art. They show no top bar by default, as before, and a placement, a deck-wide `spectrum: solid|duo|mono`, `accent` or `tone-edge` asks for one. In an editable PPTX or ODP the bar is one native shape.`collage bleed` no longer draws a bar border at the top, so its photos reach the top edge.
- On a portrait or square canvas, `safe` no longer lifts a `progress` chart's tracks and rows by the footer offset. Its selector `[class*='progress']` matched the chart's own `.progress-track` and `.progress-row` as well as the section-dot rail. It now matches only the rail.
- Decks that already set `spectrum-edge: left|right|bottom|off` move once: their content, header, footer and page number now sit where the default (`top`) placement puts them, a 4px move.
- The bar's width is now rounded down to a whole pixel, never below 1px, so the border and the padding that gives it up are equal. On a 1080px-wide canvas it was 3.375px, and the browser drew it as 3px.
- `npm run check:edge-shift` renders every component in light and dark with header, footer and page number on, once per bar placement, and fails if any element's position depends on the placement. `--variants`, `--classes`, `--each-class` and `--front-matter` extend it to manifest variants, modifiers, every reserved class and deck-level registers; `--positions` writes element positions so two builds can be compared.
- Export-to-Marp ships the runtime that injects the full-bleed bar (checked in jsdom, not in a real Marp render). The vector PDF export paints the bar over a `collage bleed` photo, print mode fills it in gray on every placement, and auto-split no longer adds a closing page holding only the strips.
