- Choosing where the spectrum bar sits (`spectrum-edge: top|left|right|bottom|off`, or the matching per-slide class) no longer moves anything else on the slide. The bar used to take its width out of the content, so a `left` bar pushed the heading and cards 4px right and narrowed them, and a `bottom` bar shortened the content area. The header, footer, page number, draft/confidential stamps and the overflow marker moved with it. A dark slide, whose bar takes no room, also sat 4px higher than a light one in the same deck. Every slide now keeps the same content box and the same chrome position under every placement, and a default deck looks as it did.
- The spectrum bar is no longer drawn on the left, right or bottom of the full-bleed layouts `image`, `scene`, `split-panel` and `split-compare`. They already showed no top bar (each draws its own edge), and a side bar there shrank the photo or panel instead of sitting beside it.
- `premise` reserves one bar width at the bottom of the slide so its content box is the same under every bar placement; a default `premise` slide's content sits 2px higher.
- `npm run check:edge-shift` renders every component in light and dark with header, footer and page number on, once per bar placement, and fails if any element's position depends on the placement.
