- **A split cover with a long title moves its subtitle to the first page.** When the cover of a split slide cannot hold its title, subtitle and lead-in above the footer, the subtitle (then the lede, if still needed) moves to the top of the first body page, at body size, above the items. The cover's type never shrinks. Measured on a square check deck: a cover that ran 57px into its footer now fits. A plain `.html` export, which carries no script, keeps the cover as it was.
- The cover lift also reaches a first body page built without a stage cell (split-panel's points pages), and measures that page's free room on its content's own height, so a run that centers its pages no longer reads a half-empty page as full.
