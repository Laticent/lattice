- A tinted logo on a split slide no longer repeats one `<filter id>` across the run's pages. Each continuation page now owns `deck-logo-tint-N-k` (the filter and the `url(#…)` that points at it), so `url(#…)` cannot resolve to another page's filter. Measured on a 5-page portrait deck: before, `deck-logo-tint-1` appeared 8 times; after, `-1`, `-1-2`, `-1-3`, `-1-4` appear twice each.
