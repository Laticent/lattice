- **`split-panel` and `split-compare` keep the running header and footer on their own field.** The chrome used to span the whole slide, so a long header crossed the panel seam in the panel's ink and vanished on the other side, and on `split-compare` it printed over the option cards. Each now ends at the edge of the field it starts on, holds one line with an ellipsis, and the field keeps that band clear so a heading can't grow into it. A cut is reported to the author (the export's `CONTENT CLIPPED` line), with no reader pill, the same policy as the standard footer band.
- **A `finish:` now shows on split slides.** Both panels used to paint opaque fills over the deck's backdrop, so a finish deck's split slides showed no texture, wash or mark. On a finish deck the feature panel is now a frosted wash (`watermark` and `cat-N` keep a tint of their own color) with a seam hairline, its text takes the canvas inks, and both fields take the standard top frame inset so a corner mark like strata's tick never lands on content. Decks without a `finish:` render the solid field as before.
- **`split-compare` no longer drops the footer**, and `no-footer` / `silent` now hide a `split-panel` footer. The transform used to discard `split-compare`'s footer and nest `split-panel`'s inside the right column, out of reach of the chrome modifiers.
