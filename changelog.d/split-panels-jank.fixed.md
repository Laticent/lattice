- **`split-panel` and `split-compare` keep the running header and footer on their own field.** The chrome used to span the whole slide, so a long header crossed the panel seam in the panel's ink and vanished, and on `split-compare` it printed over the option cards. Each now ends at the edge of the field it starts on — the header on one line, the footer on two, each ending in an ellipsis past that — and the field keeps that band clear so a heading can't grow into it. A cut is reported to the author (the export's `CONTENT CLIPPED` line), with no reader pill, the same policy as the standard footer band.
- **A `finish:` now shows on split slides.** Both panels used to paint opaque fills over the deck's backdrop, so a finish deck's split slides showed no texture, wash or mark. On a finish deck the feature panel is now a frosted wash (`watermark` and `cat-N` keep a tint of their own color) with a seam hairline, its text takes the canvas inks, and the right-hand field takes the standard top frame inset so a corner mark like strata's tick never lands on content. Decks without a `finish:`, and `finish-none` slides, render the solid field as before.
- **`split-compare` no longer drops the footer, and `no-footer` / `silent` now hide a `split-panel` footer.** The transform used to discard `split-compare`'s footer and nest `split-panel`'s inside the right column, out of reach of the chrome modifiers. An author's own `<footer>` (a quote attribution, a source line) stays where it was written.
- **A `split-panel` quote no longer loses its text to the left panel.** With no lede above it, the export moved the quotation's paragraph into the panel as the lede and left an empty quote behind.
- **A `steps` slide's phase numeral no longer sits under the running header**, and `split-panel metric mirror` inks its header for the dark field it sits on (it read 2.2:1).
- **Inline code is readable on every `split-panel` field.** The chip took the dark panel's white ink on every variant but `metric`, so on a `steps` panel and on each `cat-N` tint — including a category run's split cover — it rendered white on a light fill (the formulas on `system-design-foundations` pp. 125 and 183). `steps` now keeps the ordinary chip ink, a `cat-N` tint takes `--cat-on-fill`, and `watermark` takes `--on-accent`.
