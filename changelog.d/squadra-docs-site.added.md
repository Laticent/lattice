- **Added: Squadra's demo and Learn pages, and its mark.** `/libraries/squadra/` runs the real library in the browser: six rules for a small notes language, three honest and three broken, and the reader turns them on to watch the compiler refuse each broken one by name before it can touch the text, then edits the notes and presses Fix all. The Learn page at `/libraries/squadra/learn/` carries the code, the refusals and the whole-deck comparison behind Lattice's Fix all. The mark is a copper set-square with a graphite scale on its hypotenuse.
