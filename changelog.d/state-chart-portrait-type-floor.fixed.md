- Chart labels on a portrait or square deck now scale with the deck's type. `--chart-text-min`, the floor under state-chart edge labels and ordinals and roadmap phase tags, was a fixed 11px while the rest of the type grew by `--canvas-scale`. The story-sized portrait gantt/state-chart demo deck no longer prints TYPE FLOOR: its edge labels were 10.8px against a 19.2px floor, and are now at least 24px. Landscape decks keep 11px.
- A state-chart self-loop label no longer sits on its own loop. The label now moves just clear of the arc. On landscape decks, labels that touched their loop now sit slightly above it (or below it, for a loop under the node).
