- Studio: Workspace › AI is two parts. **Models in use** shows the three models the Studio runs on, each naming where it runs and what uses it: the chat model, the image reader (which reads your Library photos) and the voice model. **Connections** shows where the Studio can reach models: OpenRouter (signed in, or a pasted key), your own Gemini, Anthropic and OpenAI keys with what each can serve, and this device.
- Studio: connecting a provider never changes a model you chose. It fills only a model that is not set yet, and says what it filled. A new Studio starts with nothing set (or the free on-device voice on a desktop) instead of guessing. Downloading an on-device model makes it available; choosing it is a separate step.
- Studio: the image reader is its own choice: the chat model by default, or a model of its own on any connected provider, listing only models that read images. Its spend is shown separately from chat's.
- Studio: when a provider retires your model, the Studio moves to the newest version of the same family on the same provider (Claude Sonnet 5 → 5.5) and tells you. It never moves to another family or provider: with nothing newer, the model reads "Model retired" and waits for you. A provider that answers with a different model instead is caught and named.
- Studio: a narrated webpage export speaks in your voice model, including a Gemini voice or the on-device voice; the export panel no longer has a narrator picker of its own.
- Studio: a provider that answers "out of credit" is shown as that under Connections, with the time and a link to add billing.
