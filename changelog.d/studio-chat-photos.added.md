- **Studio chat:** the paperclip now takes a photo. "Add a photo…" opens the device's own picker (Photo Library, Take Photo or Choose File on a phone), and you can paste an image into the message box. The photo goes through the Library's intake (EXIF and GPS removed, capped at 2560 px) to this deck's shelf, shows as a thumbnail in the thread, and goes to a vision model as an image, with its `asset:<name>` so you can ask for it on a slide. A model the catalog lists as text-only refuses the photo before you send.
- **Studio chat:** a short video can be attached the same way, up to 15 seconds and 15 MB, to a model the catalog lists as reading video. It shows as a poster with its length and goes to the model for that turn; it is not stored (the Library has no video shelf yet).
