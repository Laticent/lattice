- The Studio's Export to Marp now carries a deck's sample pictures. It fetches each `sample:` file the deck names, front-matter `logo:` included, into the bundle's `assets/` and points the deck at it, so a team-profile slide's portraits and the sample deck logo render in Marp. A sample the site cannot serve keeps its reference, and the export still drops any other relative `logo:`. The CLI's Export to Marp and the Studio's now share one definition of which references a bundle localizes.
