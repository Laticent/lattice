- The Studio's Webpage (HTML player) export now draws every chart in the **Read · Article** view in color. Before, a radar, pie, bar or heatmap painted solid black, and kanban, progress, roadmap and timeline charts lost all their styling. The view lifts each chart out of its slide, and the Studio handed the player a stylesheet that scoped every chart rule and palette token to the inside of a slide. The engine now returns that stylesheet in the CLI player's shape as well (`render(…, { styles: 'flat' })`), so the two export paths style Read · Article the same way. Slides render as before (278 before/after slide captures, light and dark, showed no change).
- The same Studio Webpage export now themes the player's own chrome and the Read · Article page from the deck's palette, as the CLI player always has. In dark mode both used to stay white.
