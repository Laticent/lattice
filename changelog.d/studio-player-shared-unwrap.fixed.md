- The Studio's **Download as webpage** player no longer styles a `<section>` you nest inside a slide as a slide of its own. It took the slide's 1280×720 box, clipped with `overflow: hidden`, and inside a `dark` slide painted as a white panel. The Studio player, the Reading view, `check:render` and the CLI export now unwrap the engine's flat sheet through one function, `unwrapFlatSheet` (`lib/export/unwrap-flat-sheet.mjs`), so the Studio and CLI players style the same deck the same way.
