- **Studio: the slide gallery finds slides by what you mean, not only by spelling.** Everyday words now find the right component: "grade" finds the scorecard, "movie" the video, "data" the charts. A misspelled component name still finds its component. Measured on an everyday-words benchmark: the right slide comes first for 89% of one-word queries, up from 44%, and for 66% of words held out of tuning, up from 22%.
- **Component manifests gain `searchTerms`:** the plain words an author would type to find the component. Every shipped component carries them, and a unit test keeps it that way. The schema leaves them optional, so a component you make in the Studio is not rejected for lacking them.
- **Studio: a new search no longer flashes the previous result's preview on a tile.** The add-slide gallery reuses a small pool of preview frames, and a frame moving to a new tile kept its last picture until it repainted, so typing `movie` then `science` showed the video slide on the `math` tile for a moment. A frame now hides until it paints its new slide, and a tile that leaves the results gives its frame up at once.
