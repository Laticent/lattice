- Studio split panels now share one size contract: every docked panel (Settings, Chat/Coach/Live/Lenses, Library, tablet Inspector) is at least 280px, opens at 320px and drags to 480px, and the editor takes 46% of the editor|preview pair whenever a panel opens (dragging, or resizing the window, scales them from there; a resize fold and unfold restores them). Before, docks opened at their minimum (260 and 200), the editor share drifted to 41%, and at 1100px desktop and 820px tablet every panel squeezed under its own minimum, clipping the chat composer.
- When Settings and an assistant panel are open on a window too narrow for all four columns, the editor folds to its rail instead of squeezing the other panels, and unfolds when a panel closes or the window grows; "Expand editor" on a folded editor closes a dock to make room.
- The Library header's search field now shrinks, so the Import button no longer clips at the dock's default width.
