- The Studio and the Playground no longer hang on their loading screen when you open them in the minutes after a site deploy. The site's host lets a browser reuse a page for up to ten minutes, and each deploy deletes the previous build's scripts, so a reused page asked for scripts that were gone and the Studio sat on its placeholder until you reloaded. The page now notices the missing script before the app starts, checks that a newer page exists, and switches to it once.
