- **Typing in the Studio no longer stutters on a slower device.** Each keystroke used to re-render the whole Studio before the next one could land. At phone-class CPU speed, every key was a long task of about 160-200 ms. The editor now hands its text to the rest of the Studio as a low-priority update that yields to the next keystroke, and a burst of typing renders once for its newest text. Measured at 4x CPU over 40 keystrokes: long tasks 42-46 to 14-15, main-thread time 23-30% lower. Undo still records every edit exactly as typed.
