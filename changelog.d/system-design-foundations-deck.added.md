- **Added: `examples/system-design-foundations.md`, a 161-slide systems-thinking and
  system-design teaching deck.** Part one builds a seven-word vocabulary (system,
  boundary, environment, process, model, constraint, invariant) and works it through a
  model of an ordinary day; part two frames every design question as one of five
  solution types (MVP, scaled, optimized, optimal, specialized); part three is six
  reference kits — data, compute, network, scale, reliability, security — each entry
  answering the same three questions and each kit closing with its invariants; part
  four designs a photo feed and a conversational model service end to end. Twenty-one
  Mermaid diagrams, a four-page glossary, and `profile: teaching`.
