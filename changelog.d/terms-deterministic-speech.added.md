- **More acronyms and abbreviations are spoken.** Acronyms with one meaning across academic, corporate, team, government and nonprofit decks (GPA, AUC, CRM, QBR, VP, RFP, OIG, GIS, WCAG); the dotted abbreviations with one meaning (e.g., i.e., etc., vs., approx., a.m., p.m., Ph.D., Mr., Ms.); and capitals joined by periods (U.S., D.C.), spelled. An acronym with two meanings (AP, ERP, CFR, …) is never guessed: `lint:deck` names its meanings so the deck can say which (`narration-acronym-ambiguous`).
- **The Studio editor completes a term's fields** (`pronounce`, `expansion`, `definition`) inside `terms:`. On a phone, a completion's help text now sits below the whole list instead of covering the options under the selected one.
