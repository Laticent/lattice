- **A `terms:` entry is read the way YAML reads it, and lint says when one is ignored.** An unquoted key with a space (`Oct. Tenth: October Tenth`), a one-line `terms: { … }` and a `# comment` after a value now work; before, the first two were silently ignored and the comment was spoken. A field the reader does not know (`pronunciation:`, `say:`), a list item or an empty entry is reported by the new `terms-entry-ignored` warning, which names the field that was meant.
- **A deck's dotted phrase term is said as declared.** `"Oct. Tenth": October Tenth` was cut into two caption cues before terms were matched, so it never applied.
- **An abbreviation's period is not spoken as a full stop.** `Oct. 10` read "October. ten", with a pause in the date.
- **Built-in terms take plurals, possessives and compounds** (`APIs`, `CEO’s`, `KPI-driven`), as a deck's own terms do, and a plural never doubles one the expansion has (`OKRs` read "objectives and key resultss").
- **Heatmap narration names each cell by the label the chart draws.** A label with a lone `*`, `~`, an entity or a backslash escape was read differently, so the Guide could not find the cell, and `2*3 grid` was spoken "twenty-three grid".
