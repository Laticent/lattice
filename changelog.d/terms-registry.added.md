- **One `terms:` map for how a deck's words are spoken.** A plain value is what the voice says (`Derby: Darby`); the long form keeps what a term stands for, how to say it and its glossary definition apart (`EBITDA: { expansion: …, pronounce: ee bit dah, definition: … }`). Every narration path reads it: Present, the narration bake, Share and Export, `--captions` and the tours. `acronyms:` and `lexicon:` still work as older names, and `lint:deck` offers to move them (`terms-key-renamed`).
- **A term now matches the forms a deck writes.** One entry covers plurals and possessives (`CROs`, `CRO's`), hyphen and slash compounds (`CRO-led`, `CRO/CFO`), the title-case and all-caps forms of a lower-case or Capitalized key (`Derby`, `DERBY`), and multi-word names (`"Twinkima G"`). Before, only the exact token matched, so each variant read as written.
- **Studio: one Terms list under Deck setup → Speech**, replacing the separate Lexicon and Acronyms lists. Each row has "Say it as", "Stands for" and "Definition", a silence toggle, and ▶ to hear the term in your current voice before a slide uses it.
- **The Studio assistant can fix pronunciation.** It adds or changes deck terms (`set_terms`) and shows how a slide or a line will be spoken (`preview_speech`), so you no longer paste YAML by hand.
- **`@laticent/cadenza` exports `houseTerms()`**: the built-in terms with what each stands for and how it is said, for a glossary or a term picker.
