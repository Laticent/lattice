- **A title slide whose lede runs past the page gives the lede a page of its own.** On a phone (a square, portrait, story or mobile deck at the phone default), a title with a paragraph-long lede that would clip now keeps its eyebrow and title on the cover, and its lede paragraphs move, in order, to the next page on the same background, and the cover keeps the deck's footer. A lede longer than one page still clips, and the export warns. A title that fits stays one slide, and 16:9 slides are unchanged. Two lede paragraphs on a title also keep a line of space between them, where they used to touch.
