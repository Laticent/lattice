- **New `tower` chart for reinsurance and insurance programs.** Write each layer as its limit in excess of its attachment (`$40M xs $50M`, also `x/s` or `excess of`) and list each reinsurer's share under it. The chart stacks the layers from the retention up, keeps one color per reinsurer across every layer, shows any unplaced share as `Open`, and prints each boundary figure on the right. Layers are drawn at equal height so small bottom layers stay readable; their sizes are printed beside them. Narration says the layers aloud ("forty million dollars excess of fifty million dollars") from the bottom up. Demo deck: `examples/tower.md`.
