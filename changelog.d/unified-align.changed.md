- **Breaking:** **One `align:` key places content on both axes, and it replaces `cards:`.** Write where content sits up and down (`top`, `middle`, `bottom`, `spread`, `fill`) and side to side (`left`, `center`, `right`, `full`), one word or two in any order: `align: bottom right`. `align: center` on its own centers both ways. A deck with no `align:` line looks as it did, because each layout keeps its own default. Per slide, the same words are classes: `_class: align-top`, or `align-bottom-right` for both. To move a deck over, write `align: middle` for `cards: center`, `align: fill` for `cards: stretch`, and keep `top` and `spread`; `_class: cards-*` becomes `_class: align-*` the same way.
- **Breaking:** **`align-center` on a slide now centers both ways when it is the only align word.** Before, it centered side to side only. Write `_class: align-top align-center` to keep the old placement. The `fill-center` and `fill-anchor` classes are gone: use `align-middle` and `align-bottom`.
- **`bottom` is new.** Content can sit on the floor of the slide, on every layout, and a split page honors it too.
- **The Studio has two Align pickers** in Look → Frame and fit, up and down and side to side, in place of Card rows, and the editor completes the words.
- **A split slide's pages no longer jump as you turn them.** Every body page of a split run starts on the same line: the line that centers the run's tallest page (or sets it on the floor, under `align: bottom`). Before, each page centered its own content, so a page with three items began lower than one with five. A plain `.html` export, which carries no script, still centers each page on its own.
