- **Fixed: the annotation register now reaches every layout that renders a below-note, up from 15 of 36.** An italic-only trailing paragraph (`_Source: …_`) renders as an annotation — the drawn `✦`, a dotted rule, label-size muted text. That register was a hand-enumerated opt-in union of seventeen layouts in `base.modifiers.css`, written three times over, while the below-note it decorates is opt-out. Measured through the emulator, one probe slide per component: 19 layouts that take a below-note silently rendered an annotation as an ordinary one instead — including `content`, which is what every un-classed slide resolves to. Two of the seventeen names (`timeline`, `principles`) are not components but VARIANT classes, so the union was also reaching across an axis it never declared. The register is now keyed on the `.below-note` wrapper the kernel emits and names no component, so it shares `rendersBeat` with the published `authoring.blocks` contract and the two sets cannot drift apart again.
- **Fixed: `diagram` and `big-number` render a below-note and an annotation.** Both withheld the trailing paragraph via `coda.claims` to protect anatomy they do not render. `diagram` claimed it for a `.diagram-caption` that no code path emits — what actually caught the paragraph was diagram's *dek* rule, so the author's closing sentence set as a second dek at the foot of the slide; the dead rule is deleted with the claim. `big-number` had no rule matching a trailing paragraph at all. Three further candidates were examined and keep their claims: `matrix-grid` and `state-chart` use that paragraph as their swatch `legend` (dropping the claim harvested the legend into the band and the chart lost it), and dropping `math`'s docks the band inside its equation grid at half width.
- **Fixed: a trailing italic note on a split page no longer takes the wrong size.** The split envelope carried a rule whose 16-arm `:not()` chain mirrored the annotation enumeration by hand, to size the em-only notes that enumeration missed. With the register universal there is nothing left to miss, and a stale mirror would have out-specified the register itself. Both copies (`lat-split-native`, `lat-split-cards`) are gone.
- **Fixed: `<!-- annotation: … -->` is documented as what it is.** `base.docs.md` described it as a corner overlay and `design/forms.md` mapped it to the `overlay` Cell. It renders nothing on any layout — the text is swallowed by the speaker-notes channel — so the claim is retired from both, and the `annotation` Tile manifest no longer reports `status: shipped`.
