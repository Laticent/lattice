- **The Playground knows which authored slide each preview page belongs to.** `rendered-index.ts` joins the page stamps of the preview split to the deck outline and names slide clones (focus steps, pane slides) and split runs, with a test over all 292 example decks at three sizes. No rendered output changes.
- **A slide map in the Playground.** **Map** in the toolbar opens a rail of every slide beside the preview (under it on a phone or tablet), with first, previous, next and last. Tiles near the rail are live pictures, the rest are sketches read from the slide's source, and it follows the preview whether you scroll, step or type. Off by default and remembered per browser.
- **A slide map in the Studio.** The map button in the slide navigator bar opens the same rail beside the focused preview (under it on a narrow pane), numbered within a reader lens, with first, previous, next and last. Off by default and remembered.
