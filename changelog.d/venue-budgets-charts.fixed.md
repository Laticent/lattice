- `journey` now holds at conference and hall: at those venues not even one stage fit, because the mood band and curve stayed full size while the legend grew. A venue now tightens the band (the faces and plumb lines follow), and the legends no longer inherit a 1.6 line height.
- `progress`, `gantt`, `journey`, `matrix-grid` and `math` carried "scales to the box" exemptions that were false: each clips when it has too many rows at a larger venue. They now declare a per-venue budget (`venueCapacity`), so `lint:deck` warns, and a `venueProbe` that `check:venue-conformance` verifies at all four venues.
