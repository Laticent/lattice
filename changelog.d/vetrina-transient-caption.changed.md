- **Vetrina — the `caption: 'cursor'` balloon is transient.** It appears when there is something to say and at no other time: the cursor moves (the movement is what brings the eye), it arrives, the balloon appears beside it, it holds for as long as an average reader needs, and then it takes itself down — and only then does the action run. A beat that points somewhere says its line on arrival; a beat whose only movement is a deictic stroke says it once the stroke is drawn; a beat that does not move says it immediately. `Stage.dismissCaption()` is the new seam, distinct from `say('')` in that it leaves the words in the live region rather than reverting to the take-over hint.
