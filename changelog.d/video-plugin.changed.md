- **Breaking:** a `video` slide names its clip with an `@video{"https://…", title="…", poster=…}` record on a line of its own, from the new `video` plugin. The bullet form (`- https://…`, with `caption` and `poster` bullets) is gone: nothing reads a slide's text into an address any more. `lint:deck --fix` rewrites old slides (`video-bullet`); the shipped decks are migrated. An address holding `=` or `,` must be quoted.
- A zip plugin's fence now draws on a `video` slide, where the shipped `video` pass used to keep it out. The address comes only from the author's own span at parse time, so a plugin's text cannot become a poster, link or code. The runtime's inline-code mirror skips everything inside `[data-lattice-plugin]`, and the Studio's video overlay and the Reading view take a poster only from the video plugin's own figure (`data-lattice-media="video"`).
- Inline kinds can be `named` (reached only as `@kind{…}`, no sigil) and `address` (resolved on the markdown-it path only, never on the DOM mirror). New lint rules: `video-bullet`, `video-not-alone`, `video-literal`.
