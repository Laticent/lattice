- `marks: tone ink` and `marks: tone accent` pick the one hue `tone` draws its charts in: the deck's heading ink, or the theme's accent, instead of chart color one. Per slide, `_class: marks-ink` / `marks-accent`. The names, edges and strongest step clear the slide on every built-in palette in both modes; a source under `pigment` or `etch` does nothing, and `lint:deck` says so (`marks-source-needs-tone`). Decks that name no source render exactly as before.
- **Etch on a dark slide changed look:** its chart bodies now clear 3:1 against the slide (they measured 1.14 to 1.57:1, so the outline carried the bar alone). The dark body is the category's lighter ink at 78% instead of its hue at 40%, so etch bars on dark read stronger and the doubled edge reads quieter. The bullet measure follows. Light slides do not change. This intentionally changes every etch chart on a dark slide (a `dark` slide, a `*-dark` theme or a dark color mode); the shipped example decks and goldens use etch only on light slides, so none of them moves.
