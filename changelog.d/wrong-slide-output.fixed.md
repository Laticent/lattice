- A split-panel point whose title carries its own bold run (`- **6** again`) no longer leaves a `<strong>` open on the split page. The split now reads the title as a balanced element, so later slides stop rendering bold, the export finds every slide again, and the title keeps its trailing words ("6 again", not "6").
- A `<style>`, `<pre>`, `<textarea>` or `<script>` block closed with a space before the bracket (`</style >`) now ends at that tag. It used to run on, swallow the next `---`, and merge two slides into one, so a lens export could show a withheld slide's body under the next slide's lens tag.
- A front-matter key nested under a block, such as a lens named `footer` under `lenses:`, is no longer read as the deck's directive. That lens registry used to print in every slide's footer and could overwrite the real `footer:`.
