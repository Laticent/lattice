# <Title: the decision as a sentence>

**Symptom.** What went wrong or what prompted this, with a link or number.

**Cause.** Why it happened. Say whether you measured it, observed it, or are arguing it.

**Decision.** What we will do, and what we considered and rejected.

**Enforced by.** The check that holds this, or "discipline only".

**Retest when.** The condition under which we check whether this still holds.
