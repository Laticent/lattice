Make a Lattice deck.

Lattice is a Markdown slide-deck engine. Below are twelve slides. Each has a body in Markdown and a
list of settings that slide needs. Write the twelve slides as one Lattice deck, in Markdown, with each
slide's settings applied the way Lattice expects them. Keep every body exactly as given. Separate slides
with a line that holds only `---`. Do not number the slides in the output.

Output only the deck: no explanation, no code fence around it.

### Slide 9

Body:
```markdown
`Do this first · 8 minutes`

## Handle both pairs before anyone says a word about them.

1. Handle, don't judge
   - Pick each one up. Flex the sole. Look inside. Say nothing to anyone yet.
2. Write ten differences
   - Use your own words. You don't have the technical vocabulary yet, and it wouldn't help.
3. Mark the three a buyer would spot
   - Ninety seconds in a shop, no tools, and nobody to ask.
4. Name what you can't check
   - One difference you suspect but cannot prove by looking. This is the one that matters.
```

Settings for this slide:
- Layout: list-steps
- Speaker notes (2 paragraphs): "Set a real timer. Silence is the whole point — the moment they talk, the
loudest person's categories become everyone's categories, and you lose the
independent observations you need for the debrief." / "Step 4 is the hinge of the session. Most groups produce nothing for it on the
first pass. That blank is the lesson: they have no vocabulary for the part
that matters, because nobody ever showed it to them. Don't rescue them."
