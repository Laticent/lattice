# Checks: one name and one owner for the six answers (2026-10-18)

Status: owner decisions recorded in §12. Earlier positions are in §3. No code changes. Parents: `2026-09-24-six-state-marks.md` (the six answers and
their one meaning each), `2026-10-17-pills-plugin.md` (PR #2755: the embedded pills plugin, the token rule in §3 and the
plugin classes in §4.5), `2026-08-25-typed-glyphs.md` (HARD RULE #29: we draw the shape), and
`2026-09-29-inline-icons.md` (the icons plugin).

## 1. The answer

1. **Name it what it is.** The six answers are the outcomes of a check: yes, partly, no, unknown, not yet, does not
   apply. The author-facing docs already call the family `checks-*` and the component `checklist`. We adopt **check** and
   retire "mark" and "state" for this sense (§5).
2. **One owner.** One piece owns the vocabulary (six rows), the six drawings, the `[x]` grammar, the words and narration,
   autocomplete and lint. Components and pills never re-implement any of it.
3. **Containment, not flags.** A check sits inside a container: a pill, a badge, a table cell, a list row. The container
   holds whatever it is handed and does not know what a check is. The pill plugin gains no check attribute (§6).
4. **Plugin or host-level is a later call.** Staging comes first: name, registry, composition proven on `verdict-grid`.
   Whether the owner becomes an embedded plugin is decided after that (§7).

## 2. What exists, measured

Counted with `grep` on 2026-10-18; the counts are approximate and only the order of magnitude matters.

**"Mark" means seven things.**

| # | Sense | Example | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The six checkbox answers | `[x]`, `[-]`, `[!]` | `lib/core/state-marks.js`, about 17 files |
| 2 | A color role | `--cat-3-mark`, `--muted-mark` | about 55 stylesheets |
| 3 | Slide decoration | `mark-orbit`, `mark-micro` (11 classes) | `base.treatments.css` |
| 4 | The drawn elements of a chart | a chart's bars and points (`kernel.marks`) | about 24 chart manifests |
| 5 | A brand logo | `logo-mark` | the logo wall, about 6 files |
| 6 | A plugin's icon | the manifest's `icon` ("a plugin's mark") | `lib/plugins/README.md` |
| 7 | The drawing technique | `--mark-*` and `--shape-*` mask tokens | `base.tokens.css`, 22 tokens |

**One idea, seven names.** The six answers are called a *marker* (the typed `[x]`), a *state* (`state-marks.js`, `.state`,
`--state-color`), a *mark* (`--mark-check`), a *disc* (the docs), a *check* (`checks-bold`, `checklist`), an *answer* (the
meaning), and a *badge* (the chip that wraps one). "State" also names a state-chart's finite-state-machine states.

**Who reads the checks.**

- Twelve stylesheets read the mask tokens: `checklist`, `obligation-matrix`, `verdict-grid`, `roadmap`, `state-chart`,
  `line`, `bar`, `stacked-bar`, `slope`, `hub-spoke`, `chart-family` and `base.modifiers.css`.
- Three transforms build a `.badge` span from a leading `[x]`: `verdictGridBadges` (which also covers `pricing`) and
  `obligationMatrixBadges` (which also covers the universal `state-cells` modifier) in
  `lib/integrations/markdown-it/plugins.js`, and a DOM mirror in `lib/runtime/index.js`. Each builds the same
  chip-with-a-check, by hand.
- The grammar is read by Segno's list-text grammar (`leading-marker.js`, `list-text-grammar.js`) for a leading `[x] Senior`
  in list items and cells, and by the host's `state` inline-code kind for `` `[x]` `` (`HOST_INLINE.kinds` lists `state`).
- Compose edits them (`docs/e2e/compose-state-markers.spec.ts`), and `lint-core.js` coaches authors toward them
  (the typed-glyph rule, HARD RULE #29).

**A second meaning vocabulary sits beside the six.** `lib/core/chart-status.js` holds ten status words (`on-track`, `done`,
`live`, `at-risk`, `warn`, `blocked`, `fail`, `pilot`, `decision`, `deferred`), painted by `.chart-status` in
`chart-family.styles.css` and written as pill-shaped records. They describe how a piece of work is going. The six checks
describe whether a criterion is met. `warn` and `fail` appear in both lists.

## 3. Owner positions, 2026-10-18

Recorded as stated. Nothing else in this note is decided.

- "Mark" means several things in Lattice. Call each what it is and find a better name for this one.
- A pill is not stuffed with another attribute. The owner holds to the Unix philosophy: small tools that each do one
  thing, and accept a higher cost up front for less confusion and room to evolve.
- A component does not write its own semantics and translate them into a pill. It builds on the plugin's semantics
  (`2026-10-17-pills-plugin.md`, §3).
- Checks get their own design note and their own line of work, separate from the pills PR (HARD RULE #17).

## 4. The model

Four primitives, each with one job:

| Primitive | Its one job | Vocabulary | Knows about |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pill** | A labeled container: shape, color slot, size, room for content | Open labels, 12 ordinal colors | Nothing about checks, statuses or icons |
| **Icon** | One drawn glyph, by name | Open (a library) | Nothing about pills |
| **Check** | One of six answers, drawn as a disc | Closed, six rows | Nothing about pills |
| **Status** | A health word for a piece of work | Closed, ten rows | Nothing about pills (§8) |

Five rules keep them apart:

1. **Meaning lives in a check or a status, never in a pill or an icon.** A pill's color slot picks contrast and never
   meaning, as the pill docs already say.
2. **Components read checks, never pill labels or colors.** `verdict-grid` accepts `[x]`, the shared concept.
3. **A component composes, it does not interpret.** It places one primitive inside another.
4. **The vocabulary is data.** A check row holds its id, its marker character, its meaning, its drawing, its tone token, its
   default words, its narration words and its autocomplete entry. Adding an answer is adding a row, with no new syntax and
   no per-component edit. Today six rows exist across `state-marks.js`, the label sets and the mask tokens; the work is
   to make them one.
5. **One parse.** `2026-09-24-six-state-marks.md` §6.1 already requires it (HARD RULE #1).

**An enforceable test of the Unix rule:** a unit test that fails if `lib/plugins/pills/pills.inline.js` mentions "check",
"status" or "mark". The pill's existing `icon=` word is the one known exception, and it is called out, not copied.

## 5. The name

| Candidate | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **check** | Already author-facing (`checks-bold`, `checks-outline`, `checklist`); CI vocabulary, where a check ends pass, fail, skipped | `npm run check` and `build:check` use the word for tooling, a different audience |
| state | The host's inline kind is already `state` | Collides with a state-chart's states and the `.state` classes |
| verdict | Natural for `verdict-grid` | `verdict-grid`'s verdict is the whole recommended card |
| status | Plain English | Collides with the ten chart statuses |
| answer | Says what the six are | Too generic to search for |
| mark(er) | The current name | Seven meanings already |

**Recommendation: `check`.** It matches what authors already read. The typed `[x]` stays as the bracket form of a check.
The final name is the owner's call. A rename lands in stages: docs and new code first, file and token names later, with the
old names kept as aliases until each importer has moved.

## 6. How a check goes inside a pill

| Option | How it works | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A. A check word on the pill | `{Residency, check=pass}` | Rejected by the owner (§3). Each new meaning would add another flag. |
| B. Siblings | `` `[x]` `{Residency}` `` side by side | Works today. The check cannot sit inside the pill's edge, so the chip look is impossible. |
| **C. Containment** | The pill renders the content it is handed. A check draws itself inside. | Recommended. Needs the unknowns below answered. |
| D. The component owns the badge | Status quo: three components build their own | Rejected: it is the duplication this note exists to remove. |

With C, `verdict-grid` keeps `[x] Residency` as its authoring syntax, since that syntax is the shared concept and
`checklist` and `obligation-matrix` use it too. The component asks the pill renderer for a pill whose content is a check
followed by the text. The `.badge` geometry in `verdict-grid`, `pricing` and `obligation-matrix` is deleted, and the runtime
mirror shrinks to the same call.

**Unknowns, to answer with a spike before anything lands:**

- A pill's label with a `[` or `]` must be quoted today (`` `{"[x] Residency", tag}` ``), so an author writing the composed
  form directly gets an awkward spelling. Components compose it, so authors rarely write it, but a small Segno rule may be
  needed.
- Whether the pill's text path can run the leading-check reader without special cases in the pill.
- Whether narration, lint and Compose still read the composed result.

## 7. Is it a plugin, and which class

| Option | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Stay host-level; rename and build the registry | Smallest | Step one on the way to the embedded plugin |
| **Embedded plugin** (`2026-10-17-pills-plugin.md` §4.5) | The grammar lives in Segno's list-text grammar, deeper than the inline door pills use. About 17 files depend on it. | **Decided by the owner, 2026-10-20 (§12).** Staging below still starts host-level, then moves in. |
| Essential plugin built on icons | The icons plugin can be switched off, and a check that vanishes with it breaks the color-blind-safe shape channel | Rejected |

## 8. Statuses

The ten chart statuses and the six checks are different questions (is the work healthy, versus is the criterion met), but
they share a shape: a closed word, a drawing, a tone, words and narration. The proposal is **one registry shape with two
sets**, answers and progress, and a draft overlap to test, not to decide: `warn` and `fail` appear in both; `done` is close to
yes; `deferred` is close to not yet. Nothing changes in `chart-status.js` or `.chart-status` now. The question is decided
after §6, because it decides whether `.chart-status` is the same job as a check.

## 12. Owner decisions, 2026-10-20

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name | **check** |
| 2 | Typed forms | The brackets `[x] [-] [!] [?] [ ] [/]` stay as shortcuts. `@check{…}` is the full form. |
| 3 | Owner | An **embedded plugin** (§7, now decided). It needs its own note and PR for the grammar work. |
| 4 | Statuses | They **join** the registry: one shape, two sets, answers and progress (§8). |
| 5 | Autocomplete | Offers the six answers through `@check{…}`. |
| 6 | The `checks-*` style family | Keeps its name, since the concept keeps its name. |
| 7 | A pill with a label | `@check{…}` takes the answer as an **enum value** and the label as **its own parameter**. The shortcut is not used for this. |

Item 7 removes the first unknown in §6: the label never goes through bracket quoting, because it is a named parameter and
not text after a `[x]`. The pill still renders the content it is handed (option C), and a check inside it draws itself.

## 14. Spike results, 2026-10-20

Staging step 2. A throwaway script in `.scratch/` (not committed) reads the pill with the real `resolve`, splits its label
with the real `readLeadingMarker`, and rewrites a rendered `verdict-grid` so each `.badge` becomes a pill holding a drawn
check. Measured on the indaco theme at 1280 x 720; the before and after PNGs went to the owner in chat.

| Unknown (§6) | Result |
|---|---|
| Bracket quoting | Confirmed. `{[x] Residency, tag}` does not read as a pill and stays literal; `{"[x] Residency", tag}` reads, with `value` `[x] Residency`. Decision 7 (§12) removes the need: `@check{…}` carries the label as its own parameter. |
| Can the pill's text path run the leading-check reader | Yes, with no pill special case. `readLeadingMarker(pill.value)` returns `{marker:'x', rest:'Residency'}` for all six markers, a label with a comma included. The composer lives in the caller, as option C says. |
| Narration | **Does not carry over as is.** Today's grid speaks "Residency: yes, Adoption: not applicable, Calibration: not assessed". The composed grid speaks "yesResidency, does not applyAdoption, openCalibration": the layout's own words are lost because narration keys on `.badge`, and the disc's `aria-label` joins the label with no space. Step 5 owes a narration change: read a check inside a pill through the layout's word map, and put a space after it. |
| Lint | Clean. No rule reads a check inside a pill, so the composed grid also drops the `density-crowd` hint the shortcut form earns. Step 5 owes a rule that reads the answer from either spelling. |
| Compose | Better, not worse. The shortcut `- [x] Residency` locks the slide in the Studio (`hasLossyConstruct` is true); the composed form is ordinary inline code and round-trips (false). |

Two more findings the renders showed:

- **The badge shift is real.** The pill is 23 px tall against the badge's 26 px, and its type is smaller. This is the shift
  §10 expects, and step 5's before and after renders will carry it.
- **A pill that holds a check needs a gap rule** between the disc and the text. The pill has none today, so the disc touches
  the first letter. The fix belongs in `pills.styles.css`, as a rule for a leading `.lat-state`, and not in a component.
- **The shape channel survives.** All six drawings (check, cross, dash, question ring, empty ring, slash) stay distinct
  inside the pill, without color.

Proposal for the open spellings in §13: the answer is the first bare word and is one of the six ids (`pass`, `warn`,
`fail`, `unknown`, `todo`, `skip`); the label is `label=`, for example `@check{pass, label=Residency}`. The brackets stay
the shortcut for the six. The owner confirms.

## 15. Staging step 3 landed, 2026-10-20

`lib/core/state-marks.js` now holds `CHECKS`, one frozen table of six rows: `id`, `marker`, `shape`, `label`, `mask` and `tone`. `STATE_CLASSES`, `MARKER_LABELS` and `MARKERS` derive from it and keep their names
and shapes, so no reader changed. Unit tests pin each row against the places that cannot import it: `mask` against the
`--state-mark` rules in `base.modifiers.css` and the `--mark-*` tokens, `tone` against the `.lat-state` rules, and the ids
against the keys of every `WORD_MAPS` row in `prose-projection.mjs` (which imports no kernel and stays the one home of the per-layout words; carrying them in the registry too put 71 bytes over the getting-started engine ceiling for data no engine code read). No behavior change: the whole-corpus render
(2,248 decks, HTML plus CSS length) hashes identically before and after.

Not folded in yet: the roadmap's own vocabulary (`state-shipped`, `state-wip`, …) and the label sets in manifests. They are a
different word set for the same six answers and move with step 5.

## 16. Staging step 4 landed, 2026-10-20

- **The pill accepts content.** `pillHtml(text, off, contentHtml)` and `pillElement(doc, text, off, content)` take what the pill
  holds in place of its label (§6 option C). The pill source does not look inside it, and says nothing about checks.
- **The gap is a flex row** (owner decision, 2026-10-20): a pill that holds a `.lat-state` is `inline-flex` with `align-items:center`
  and `gap:0.4em`, and the component wraps its words in `.lat-label` so `text-box` has a real flex item to trim. Measured at
  1280 x 720 on indaco in Chromium: disc to label 2.48 px before, 4.52 px after; disc and label centers equal; pill height
  20.1 px against 20.7 px. No margin declaration is added, the `SANCTIONED_MARGINS` entries stay at three with their plain values,
  and the gap sits inside the pill's own box, where a measuring layout sees it. A first version routed the gap through a custom
  property on the disc's margin; it is gone.
- **Narration.** `prose-projection.mjs` reads a disc inside a pill through the layout's word map and puts a space after it. A
  composed `verdict-grid` now speaks "Residency: yes, Adoption: not applicable, and Calibration: not assessed", equal to
  today's grid (a unit test builds both and compares them).
- **Not done here:** the lint rule that reads a check from either spelling (the `density-crowd` hint) moves with step 5, when a
  component first emits the composed form. No component composes a pill yet, so no deck renders differently (the corpus hash is
  unchanged).

**Checker findings, 2026-10-20.** The gap and the narration selectors match the disc anywhere among the pill's direct children, so an
icon before it does not break them. Known and unreachable today: a state word from the element itself and a pill disc of a different
answer in one element.
