# R1-R4 audit: per-component matrix

Method: every component gallery (`lib/components/*/*/*.gallery.md`, `lib/plugins/chart-family/kinds/*/*.gallery.md`), every slide with a list, rendered to PNG through the CLI (`dist/lattice.js deck.md deck.png`, theme from the gallery) in five variants: `orig`; `flipTop` (top-level `-`<->`1.`, children re-indented); `flipAll` (every level flipped); `boldOff2`/`boldOn2` (the leading run of each top-level item wrapped in `**...**` or not, with `section strong{font-weight/color/style:inherit}` injected into BOTH so only STRUCTURAL effects of bold show). Pixel diff at 1280x720, channel delta >24, reported as % of pixels differing. Every gallery ends with a `cards-grid` "When NOT to" slide; those pages are excluded from other components' rows (they are cards-grid's R2 behavior). Scripts: gen.js, gen3.js, cmp.py, htmldiff.js in this folder; raw data pixdiff.json, htmldiff.txt.

Caveats: `bold` = "not rendered" means that deck was outside the 33-component bold subset; "identical" for bold where the gallery items already start with `**` or code means the generator could not add bold (inventory, matrix-2x2, pricing, redline, statute-stack, verdict-grid, checklist, rows) - those rows rely on static evidence. Tiny diffs (<0.1%) on citation-card/decision/split-compare flipAll were not inspected (UNVERIFIED cause).
