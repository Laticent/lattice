`Feature demo · colour-vision deficiency`

# Meaning that survives colour blindness.

One theme — `theme: a11y-deuteranopia` — and the deck stops relying on hue
alone. Status reads by shape, categories by texture.

---

<!-- _class: silent -->

`The problem`

## ~8% of men can't separate your categories by colour.

Under deuteranopia, red and green collapse; the pale categorical fills a deck
leans on drop to one or two distinguishable colours. A palette can't fix
that — the information has to ride a second channel.
