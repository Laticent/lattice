## The tile takes the deck's colors, sizes and shapes; the face keeps its own.

- Sizes: `!{Kenji Sato, sm}` `!{Kenji Sato}` `!{Kenji Sato, lg}` `!{Kenji Sato, xl}` — 1.1, 1.45, 2.4 and 4 times the text.
- Shapes: `!{Sofia Marchetti, lg}` circle, `!{Sofia Marchetti, lg, rounded}` rounded, `!{Sofia Marchetti, lg, square}` square, and `!{Sofia Marchetti, lg, bare}` bare.
- Colors: `!{Nia Bello, lg, c1}` `!{Nia Bello, lg, c3}` `!{Nia Bello, lg, c5, border=c5}` `!{Nia Bello, lg, c7, border=c7}` — the tile from `c1`–`c12`, a ring with `border=`.

## `gender=` changes which defaults a name draws from; it never locks a trait.

- `!{Sam Lee, lg}` `!{Sam Lee, lg, gender=woman}` `!{Sam Lee, lg, gender=man}` — one name, three presets: the hair, beard and brows change, nothing else.
- `!{Sam Lee, lg, gender=man, hair=long}` `!{Sam Lee, lg, gender=woman, hair=buzz}` — a trait you write always wins over the preset.
- Skin, hair, eyes and clothes keep their colors in every theme, the way a photograph does.
