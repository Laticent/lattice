# Keep the pattern off the chrome

`backdrop: clear-hero · deck-wide`

The strata dots run behind the words and step aside for the footer and the hero number.

---

`Deck-wide · clear-hero`

## The pattern stays; the footer reads clean.

Strata is a dot finish. `clear` empties the whole content box, which throws the pattern away. The keep-outs leave it behind the content and clear only what a reader decodes letter by letter.

- The footer text, the section rail and the page number sit on clean canvas
- The dots fade back in above the footer band, never through it
