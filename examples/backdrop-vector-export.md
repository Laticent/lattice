# Backdrops, drawn

`Export · PDF`

A finish's glow and texture reach the PDF as drawing, not as a low-resolution picture.

---

<!-- _class: statement finish-loom -->

`Glow and weave`

## The weave stays sharp, and the glow stays smooth.

Zoom in: the lattice is lines, and the glow under it has no blocks or color patches.

---

<!-- _class: statement finish-nimbus dark -->

`Dark canvas`

## A dark glow keeps its shape.

Four blooms and an edge vignette, as the Studio shows them, not the flatter export face.

---

<!-- _class: statement finish-strata -->

`Dots and bars`

## Each dot is a disk.

The dot grid, the corner mark and the bar along the top are drawn as shapes.

---

<!-- _class: statement finish-atrium backdrop-clear dark -->

`backdrop: clear`

## The clear panel fades without a seam.

Its edge is one smooth falloff on each side, multiplied at the corners.

---

<!-- _class: statement finish-halo backdrop-spot-tr -->

`backdrop: spot-tr`

## A spotlight hides the rings outside its window.

The scrim is a smooth fade, drawn over the rings it covers.

---

<!-- _class: statement finish-gallery -->

`frame`

## The frame keyline is a line.

Gallery's mat and keyline are drawn as rings, sharp at any zoom.

---

<!-- _class: statement finish-meridian ghost -->

`What stays in the photo`

## A ghost numeral is text, so it stays a picture.

The finish keeps its export face on this slide, and the stripes under the numeral are still drawn.
