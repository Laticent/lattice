# A card tag can sit anywhere on its card.

Five placements and three alignments, on every tagged layout, set deck-wide by `tag:` or per slide.

---

<!-- _class: decision tag-foot -->

## tag-foot moves every tag to the bottom corner.

- Build
  - Owns the scoring policy, the calibration loop and the pager.
- Why not buy either
  - Neither vendor exposes the calibration weights.
- Why not delay
  - The competitive window closes in 18 months.

---

<!-- _class: cards-grid four tag-notch -->

## tag-notch straddles each card's top edge.

1. Signal Intake
   - Weekly structured collection, one schema.
2. Scoring Model
   - Confidence, recency and relevance.
3. Decision Log
   - Every decision with its signals.
4. Calibration Loop
   - A monthly look at the weights.

---

<!-- _class: decision tag-band tag-center -->

## A band may wrap to two lines, and every band matches it.

- Build
  - Owns the scoring policy, the calibration loop and the pager.
- Why not buy from either shortlisted vendor
  - Neither exposes the calibration weights to the customer.
- Why not delay
  - The competitive window closes in 18 months.

<!-- _class: compare-prose transition tag-inline -->

## tag-inline sets the label above the text as a chip.

- Before
  - Every prioritization debate argued from first principles, average close four hours.
- After
  - Decisions resolve against logged weights, average close 18 minutes.

## tag-end sets the text at the far end of the band.

   - Signals collected, decisions unlogged. Above P2, the log is required.
