# One recipe for every card tag.

Numbers, slot labels, the verdict and the step label now share one kernel.

---

<!-- _class: cards-grid -->

## Numbered cards carry a flush corner tag.

1. Signal Intake
   - Weekly structured collection, normalized into one schema before scoring.
2. Scoring Model
   - Each signal scored on confidence, recency and strategic relevance.
3. Decision Log
   - Every decision recorded with the signals and criteria behind it.
4. Calibration Loop
   - A monthly retrospective that adjusts the scoring weights.

---

<!-- _class: cards-stack -->

## A numbered stack uses the same tag.

1. False signal amplification
   - One loud voice dominating the decision. The model caps any source at 30%.
2. Signal hoarding
   - Signals collected, decisions unlogged. Above P2, the log is required.
