# A split slide reads as a carousel.

The cover carries the title and the swipe cue. Every page after it carries the content and nothing else.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, footer=cards-grid} -->


## The framework has four parts.

- Signal intake.
  - Every source lands in one queue.
- Scoring model.
  - Each signal is weighed on three axes.
- Decision log.
  - Every call is written with its signals.
- Calibration loop.
  - Outcomes are checked against predictions.

> Signals without decisions are noise.

## Three jurisdictions.

- Federal `{15 U.S.C. §6501}` `{In effect since 2000}`
  - Verifiable parental consent for under-13 personal data.
- State `{Cal. Civ. §1798.120}` `{Enforced 2023}`
  - Opt-in for selling or sharing under-16 data.
- Local `{NYC §22-1201}` `{Effective 2023}`
  - Bias-audit obligation for automated hiring tools.
