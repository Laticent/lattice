# A split slide reads as a carousel.

The cover carries the title and the swipe cue. Every page after it carries the content and nothing else.

---

<!-- _class: cards-grid -->
<!-- _footer: "cards-grid · one card a page, no repeated title" -->

## The framework has four parts.

- Signal intake.
  - Every source lands in one queue.
- Scoring model.
  - Each signal is weighed on three axes.
- Decision log.
  - Every call is written with its signals.
- Calibration loop.
  - Outcomes are checked against predictions.

> Signals without decisions are noise.
