# `Bn` and `BN` now read as billions

Finance decks write a billion four ways, and every chart draws all four at the same height

---

<!-- _class: bar -->

`Revenue by segment · $ billion`

## Four spellings of one figure draw four equal bars.

- Retail `$5B`
- Wholesale `$5bn`
- Services `$5Bn`
- Licensing `$5BN`

Before, Services and Licensing drew at five dollars, a billion times too short.

---

<!-- _class: bar row -->

`Capital plan · $ billion`

## A deck pasted from two models still shares one axis.

- Network upgrade `$2.4Bn`
- Data centers `$1.8BN`
- Retail refit `$950M`
- Fleet renewal `$0.6bn`

---

<!-- _class: line -->

`Group revenue · $ billion`

## Revenue crossed five billion in the third quarter.

- Q1 2026 `$4.1BN`
- Q2 2026 `$4.6Bn`
- Q3 2026 `$5.2bn`
- Q4 2026 `$5.8B`

---

<!-- _class: bar diverging -->

`Variance to plan · $ billion`

## A negative billion reads as negative in every spelling.

- Americas `$1.2Bn`
- EMEA `-$0.8BN`
- APAC `($0.4Bn)`
- Corporate `$0.3bn`

The narration says "down eight hundred million dollars" for EMEA, as it does for `-$0.8bn`.
