# The key matches its chart

On a dark canvas a legend swatch could paint a different color from the mark it labelled. The 82% wash had no token, so ten call sites retyped it and five drifted onto the canvas instead of the base. This deck is dark on purpose — it is the only canvas where the two ever differed.
