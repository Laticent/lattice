# Hue has two jobs

A categorical hue **binds** a mark to a name that sits somewhere else, or it **separates** marks that touch. A mark with its own label beside it and clear space around it needs neither — so `funnel` and `timeline-list` now draw in one hue instead of six.

`The rule`

## Spend hue where it does a job.

- Bind
  - The mark and its name are apart — a legend rail or a leader line.
- Separate
  - The marks touch or overlap, so position cannot tell them apart.
- Neither
  - One hue, slot 1; identity moves to the label already beside the mark.
