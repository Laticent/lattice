# A chart key sizes its value column to what it holds

`Charts · map and piechart keys`

---

<!-- _class: map world highlight -->

`Field offices · 2026`

## Each office names its region in the key.

- Kenya `East Africa`
- Nigeria `West Africa`
- India `South Asia`
- Brazil `Latin America`

— Each region sets on one line; the column grew to fit "Latin America".

---

<!-- _class: map world highlight -->

`Global South · by continent`

## A long region name wraps inside its own column.

- Global South — Africa `Africa`
- Global South — South America `South America`
- Global South — North America `Caribbean / Central America`
- Global South — Oceania `Pacific`

— Before: "Caribbean / Central America" printed across "Global South — North America".

---

<!-- _class: map world highlight grouped -->

`Partner tiers · 2026`

## A short tag keeps its line.

- European Union `Tier 1`
- ASEAN `Tier 1`
- Sub-Saharan Africa `Tier 2`
- Latin America `Tier 2`

`Revenue mix · FY26`

## A numeric key is unchanged.

- Licenses `46%`
- Services `31%`
- Support `23%`

— Values of five characters or fewer keep the old column, so this key renders as it did.
