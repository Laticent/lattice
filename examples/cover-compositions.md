# Seven ways to compose a cover.

`cover-layout: <composition>`

Each composition reads in dark, on the brand panel, and in light, on the canvas.

---

## The register takes one word

- `cover-layout: column`
  - The word picks the composition. The color comes from `color-mode:` and a slide's own `light` or `dark`, as on every slide.
- Seven compositions
  - `masthead`, `ledger`, `column`, `label`, `stage`, `frame` and `poster`, on title and closing alike.
- `cover-plain` on one slide
  - Keeps today's centered block on that cover inside a deck that sets a composition.
