# Cuoio's inks now step down

Body text, secondary text and the frame used to share one ink. Now each reads a step quieter than the one above it.

`The programme · Part one`

## What a visitor walks through

- Twelve installations along four kilometers of the east bank
- A floating stage at the old customs house, open until two
- Night walks led by the artists themselves, free on weekdays

`Three ways in`

## Come once, or every night

- Walk
  - The riverside route is free and needs no ticket.
- Stay
  - The floating stage sells nightly passes from March.
- Make
  - Twelve workshops with the artists, for ages nine and up.
