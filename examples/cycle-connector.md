`Weekly reimbursement`

## We turned $200,000 into revolving working capital.

- Help families
  - Rent assistance goes out first.
- Get reimbursed
  - The County repays us weekly.
- Help more families
  - The same dollars go back out.

<!-- _class: cycle -->

`The water cycle`

## Nothing is spent — only moved.

- Evaporation
  - The sun lifts water into vapor.
- Condensation
  - Vapor cools into cloud.
- Precipitation
  - Rain returns it to the ground.

---

<!-- _class: cycle -->

`Product loop`

## Every release feeds the next one.

- Ship
  - Put the change in front of users.
- Measure
  - Read what they actually did.
- Learn
  - Decide what the numbers mean.

  - The County repays the advance.
- Reinvestment
  - The funds return to families.
