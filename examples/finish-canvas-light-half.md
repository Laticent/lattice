# A finish mixes toward the surface the slide really paints

The dark half shipped first. Every other mode carried the same mismatch, and the gate was holding it by name.

---

<!-- _class: content -->

## What `--fin-canvas` is for

- Every finish layer is palette-blind
  - Wash, export face and clearance mask all mix toward one token, so a preset works on all 33 palettes.
- That token has to be the real surface
  - Name the deck ground on a slide painting its own panel and the mask paints a pale ellipse across it.

## The covers are fixed in the same change, and no slide here can show them

- What the cascade does
  - An accent cover paints `var(--accent)` in every mode. Six of them re-point the canvas to match.
- Why you cannot see it
  - A split drops the `finish` class when it builds a cover, so a cover gets no backdrop. Filed separately.
