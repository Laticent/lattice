# A split run keeps the deck's finish on every page it emits

The cover used to lose it. Every page after this one is a split run, and every page wears the finish.

---

<!-- _class: content -->

## The plain split: a cover, then one point per page

- The cover keeps the deck's registers
  - Finish, stamp, corners and the rest of the surface registers ride across the class swap.
- The body pages always did
  - They add a class rather than replace the list, so they never lost anything.

---

<!-- _class: split-panel -->

`Carousel · split-panel`

## Every page of a carousel run is re-authored, so every page was bare

- The cover uses its own ink
  - Accent on an accent field vanished. The cover's ink reads.
- The points pages sit on the canvas
  - They draw it in the accent, like any slide.

---

<!-- _class: split-panel cat-3 -->

`Carousel · a tinted panel`

## A category tint is a canvas too, and names its own ink

- The canvas is the tint
  - The finish mixes toward the panel fill, not the deck ground.
- The ink is the tint's text color
  - The same ink the panel's headline already uses.

---

<!-- _class: decision finish finish-strata -->

`Decision · a per-slide finish`

## A slide's own finish wins over the deck's, across the split too

- Carry the slide's preset
  - This run is strata, not atrium. The cover and both points pages agree.
- Drop the deck's preset
  - The same slide-over-deck rule the engine applies before the split.
