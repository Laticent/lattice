## Long items take more room, so fewer fit a page.

- Signal intake lands every source in one queue, normalized to one schema before anyone scores it.
- The scoring model weighs each signal for confidence, recency and strategic fit, with weights each team sets once.
- The decision log records every call with the signals behind it, the options weighed and the criteria applied.
- The calibration loop checks outcomes against predictions every quarter and moves the weights that missed.
- Escalation routes a contested call to the steering group within a week, with both readings written down.
- The archive keeps every retired signal and the reason it was retired, so the next team can see what was tried.
- Reporting rolls the log up into one page a month, so the board sees the calls and not the machinery.
- Access stays with the owning team, and every read outside it is logged for the audit.
