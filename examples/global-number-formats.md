# Money as global clients write it

A reinsurance deck carries `EUR 4.2M`, `4,2 Mio. €` and `USD 250mn`. Each of those used to stop the chart from drawing. Every slide here is written in one of them.

- 2021 `EUR 1.2B`
- 2022 `EUR 1.4B`
- 2023 `EUR 1.7B`
- 2024 `EUR 2.1B`
- 2025 `EUR 2.4B`
