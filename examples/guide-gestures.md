`Feature demo · Guide`

# A pointer that says more than "over there."

Turn Guide on in Present and read this deck. Each slide is a different shape, and the hand changes what it does to suit it.

---

<!-- _class: content -->

`The problem`

## A pointer with one verb can only say "this" by going somewhere.

Guide used to move on every sentence, so the eye was dragged on every full stop.

- A hand rests, then names one thing.
- So the gesture fires on a block change.

---

<!-- _class: list-criteria -->

`The vocabulary`

## Five gestures, chosen by the shape of the thing being named.

1. Underline
   - One wide, short line of prose. The workhorse, and it lives in the descender gap.
2. Ring
   - Compact and roughly square: a stat, a chip, a table cell.
3. Wash
   - A phrase inside a longer block, named without naming the block.
4. Bracket
   - A whole card or a block read several lines at a time.
5. Tap
   - Something small and discrete, where a ring would be a dot.

---

<!-- _class: content -->

`Underline`

## This heading is one wide, short line, so the hand sweeps along it.

Watch the stroke: it sits below the baseline, in the descender gap, and the pointer rides it out past the right-hand edge of the column. It never comes to rest on the words it is asking you to read.

`Wash`

## A paragraph read a clause at a time gets a highlighter, not a circle.

Growth held across every region. Spend stayed disciplined through the second half. The quarter landed on plan with room to spare. Each of those sentences is a phrase inside this block, so the hand washes the words being spoken rather than naming the whole paragraph four times over.

<!-- _class: cards-grid -->

`Bracket`

## A card is outlined from just outside it.

- Rests, then moves
  - The gesture fires on a new block, never on a full stop.
- Fits the shape
  - Five verbs, picked by geometry rather than at random.
- Clears the text
  - The pointer rides the stroke, so it stops outside what it named.
- Follows the deck
  - Where a slide declares focus, that is what gets named.
