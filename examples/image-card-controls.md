## image-card-sm, top right.

The small card steps the type down, so the photo carries the slide.

![A figure by a lake under a low sun](sample:photo-wide.jpg)

<!-- _class: image spotlight image-card-bottom image-card-soft image-card-light -->

## A soft dark card, centered low.

`image-card-soft` thins the panel to 80%; `image-card-light` sets white words on a dark card.

![A lone figure on a wide shore at dusk](sample:photo-pano.jpg)

<!-- _class: image spotlight image-card-top-left image-card-sheer image-card-dark -->

## A sheer light card, top left.

`image-card-sheer` drops the panel to 55%; `image-card-dark` sets near-black words on a light tint.

![A figure by a lake under a low sun](sample:photo-wide.jpg)

---

<!-- _class: image spotlight image-card-center image-card-lg -->

## image-card-lg lets the message lead.

A wider card and a larger heading, centered on the slide.

![A figure by a lake under a low sun](sample:photo-wide.jpg)

## The scrim flips to the right.
