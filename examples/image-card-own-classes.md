## A deck-wide compact card.

`image-card: compact` tightens the card on every image slide. It no longer tightens the slide's spacing.

## Placement and size.

`image-card-top-right` moves it.

`image-card-sm` steps its type down.

![A figure by a lake under a low sun](sample:photo-wide.jpg)

<!-- @meta{layout=image, class=spotlight image-card-top-left image-card-sheer image-card-dark} -->

## Backing and ink.

`image-card-sheer` thins the panel to 55%.

`image-card-dark` sets dark words on a light tint.

![A figure by a lake under a low sun](sample:photo-wide.jpg)

<!-- @meta{layout=image, class=statement image-card-bottom image-card-band image-card-65 image-card-light} -->

## A band at a 65% backing.

`image-card-band` stretches the card along the bottom edge.

`image-card-65` sets its backing to 65%.

![A lone figure on a wide shore at dusk](sample:photo-pano.jpg)

<!-- @meta{layout=image, class=spotlight image-card-center image-card-lg} -->

## A large card, centered.

`image-card-lg` widens the card and lifts the heading.

`image-card-center` puts it in the middle.

![A figure by a lake under a low sun](sample:photo-wide.jpg)

<!-- @meta{layout=content, class=tint-corner at-tr} -->

## `at-tr` places a tint, not a card.

- The corner tint sits top right, as it does on any slide.
- The tint words keep their meaning on every layout.
- The next slide puts a card in the same corner with its own class.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=image, class=spotlight image-card-top-right} -->

## The card has its own corner word.

`image-card-top-right` puts the card where slide 7's `at-tr` put the tint: one word, one job.
