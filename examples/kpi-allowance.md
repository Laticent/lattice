# How much a KPI ledger holds, and what it does when you ask for more.

The briefing grid used to demand three support rows whether you authored three or one, so an ordinary three-metric slide over-subscribed its own stage. It now sizes to the metrics you wrote, and yields to the stage instead of spilling past it.

---

<!-- _class: content -->

## The allowance is a trade, not a single number.

A `kpi` slide is a hero metric spanning the stage and a column of supports beside it. The supports divide whatever room the title and eyebrow leave them.

- Three metrics is the allowance, up to a two-line title.
- A fourth needs everything terse: one pill, no eyebrow, one title line.
- A fifth never fits, at any label length.

## When you do exceed it, the slide says so — it never quietly shrinks.

The ledger yields down to the height its content genuinely needs and no further. Past that it spills over the stage edge, the stage clips it, and the export names the page in its overflow report rather than compressing rows until the metrics overprint each other.

- `npm run lint:deck` warns before you render, when a slide is past the stated budget.
- The export's overflow report names the clipped page, and tags it when a text line is actually cut.
