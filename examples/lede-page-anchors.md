## What the second half covers.

The first half named the problem. The second half prices it: what each option costs this quarter and next, who carries the risk while it runs, which of the three options the finance team can fund from the existing budget without a reforecast, and the one decision the board has to make before the next meeting so that the work can start on time. It closes with the plan for the quarter after, because a decision that only covers this quarter is a decision the board will be asked to make again in ninety days, with less room and a shorter list of options than it has today. The appendix carries the numbers behind every answer.
