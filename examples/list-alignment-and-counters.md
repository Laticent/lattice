# A list sits where `cards:` puts it

`list · cards register · one counter scale`

Top, center and stretch now place a list's rows, every counter follows one scale, and a wrapped row stays inside its pill.

---

<!-- _class: list -->
<!-- _footer: "No setting · the rows sit centered at their content height" -->

## Three points no longer stretch to fill the slide.

- Each row is as tall as its text.
- The set sits in the middle of the stage.
- The spare height stays outside the pills.

---

<!-- _class: list cards-top -->
<!-- _footer: "cards-top · the rows sit under the headline rule" -->

## Top packs the rows under the rule.

- The first row starts under the headline rule.
- The spare height collects at the bottom.
- Use it when a list is followed by nothing.

---

<!-- _class: list cards-stretch -->
<!-- _footer: "cards-stretch · the old full-height bands" -->

## Stretch hands the spare height to the rows.

1. Every row grows by the same amount.
2. The set fills the stage edge to edge.
3. This is what every list did before.

---

<!-- _class: list takeaway numbered -->
<!-- _footer: "takeaway numbered · counter 1.4× the text, one gap" -->

## Counters sit one gap from their text.

1. Every counter is 1.4 times its row's text.
2. Every counter sits in the same box.
3. The text starts at the same x on every row.

---

<!-- _class: list takeaway numbered -->
<!-- _footer: "takeaway numbered with a gloss · the lead outranks its gloss" -->

## A lead keeps its size over its gloss.

1. The lead is the row's full size
   - The gloss sits one step below it, in regular weight.
2. The counter is the same box
   - It lines up with the counters on rows that have no gloss.
3. The pair is centered on the counter
   - However many lines the gloss runs to.

---

<!-- _class: list principles -->
<!-- _footer: "principles · display weight, same 1.4× counter" -->

## Principles use the same counter rule.

1. State each rule as an imperative.
2. Keep each rule under ten words.
3. Retire a rule you keep breaking.

---

<!-- _class: list -->
<!-- _footer: "Five rows, one wrapping · the wrapped row keeps its text inside, the others give up air" -->

## A wrapped row stays inside its pill on a full slide.

1. A long line may spend twenty words, and this one spends them to show where the wrap lands.
2. Short lines give some air back.
3. The rows share what the stage has left.
4. No row ever shrinks below its text.
5. Five is a comfortable wide list.
