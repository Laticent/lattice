## Onboarding has no fixed order.

- Meet the team
  - A coffee with each lead in the first week.
- Read the playbook
  - The decisions we made, and why.
- Ship something small
  - A real change, reviewed and live.

## Release runs in three steps.

1. Freeze
   - Nothing merges after Thursday noon.
2. Verify
   - The full suite runs on the release branch.
3. Publish
   - Tag, build, and announce.

## Three gates every proposal clears.

1. It names the customer
   - Who is better off, and how we will know.
2. It names the cost
   - People, money, and what we stop doing.
3. It names the owner
   - One person signs for the result.
