# The logo picks its berth

The deck sets `logo-at: bottom-center`. The footer text stands down in that band.

---

## Header, page number and logo coexist

- The header stays at the top
- The page number stays at the bottom right
- Only the footer text yields to a bottom-center logo

---

## Change one line to move the brand

- `logo-at: top-left` hides the running header
- `logo-at: bottom-right` hides the page number and the rail
- `logo-x` and `logo-y` still place it freely
