# One line recolors the logo

`logo-color: accent 60%, ink` mixes two theme colors. The mix follows light and dark.

---

## Light slide

- The mark takes the mix on a light canvas
- No filter chain to tune by hand

---

<!-- _class: dark -->

## Dark slide

- The same line resolves against the dark palette
- The mark stays readable with no per-mode setting

---

<!-- _class: divider -->

## Divider

The mark follows the divider's own canvas.

---

## Change the mix

- `logo-color: accent` is one role color
- `logo-color: brand 70%, canvas` softens the brand color
- `logo-color: #c8a040` pins an exact color
