# A logo wall takes the columns its captions need.

`Feature demo · logo wall, measured columns`

---

<!-- _class: content -->

## What this deck shows

- Slide 3: short captions fit four across, so the wall keeps its layout.
- Slide 4: one wide pill sits inside its share of the column gap and touches nothing, so it keeps four across too.
- Slide 5: eight long pills cannot fit four across on a phone. The wall measures them and drops to three columns, with tighter spacing, so every row stays on the slide.
- Slide 6: the same wall without captions keeps four across.
