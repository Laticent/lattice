# Five marks you can now see, and an orbit that leaves the page number alone.

`mark-brackets`, `mark-threads`, `mark-ticks`, `mark-seeds` and `mark-asterisks` are larger or heavier. The paint stays at 28%.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, class=mark-brackets} -->

## mark-brackets

- Edge
  - Two heavier brackets in the right margin.
- Paint
  - 28% accent, unchanged.
- Slot
  - Right margin, clear of content.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, class=mark-threads} -->

## mark-threads

- Stroke
  - Two px, was one.
- Slot
  - Top-right header band.
- Dark
  - Heavier strokes read in light and dark.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, class=mark-ticks} -->

## mark-ticks

- Length
  - About 31 px, was 18.
- Weight
  - About 2.8 px, was 1.9.
- Slot
  - Right margin.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, class=mark-seeds} -->

## mark-seeds

- Size
  - Larger specks, at or under the 28% ceiling.
- Slot
  - The four corners.
- Mode
  - Now paints on dark slides and under a finish.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, class=mark-asterisks} -->

## mark-asterisks

- Size
  - About a third larger, heavier strokes.
- Slot
  - Top-right and bottom-left.
- Read
  - Asterisks, not dots.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, class=mark-orbit} -->

## mark-orbit

- Position
  - Moved left, out of the page number's column.
- Slot
  - Bottom-right.
- Paint
  - Unchanged.
