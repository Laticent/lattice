# The corner held four things. Now it holds two, and the markers are one.

`Marker corner · stamp, logo, and one capsule at the foot`

Four absolutely-positioned boxes wanted the slide's top-right, and the arithmetic that kept them apart shipped wrong five times. This deck renders the answer: the two transient claimants left, then merged.

---

<!-- _class: content -->

`The occupants`

## Two are permanent. Two were passing through.

- The status stamp
  - Paints on the section's own `::before`, flush to the corner. It ships in the export.
- The deck logo
  - The author's mark, at the frame inset. Its geometry belongs to them.
- The clip marker
  - Drawn because a slide is broken. It leaves when the slide is fixed.
- The type-floor marker
  - The second segment of the same capsule. Author-only.

The first two keep the corner. The last two are one pill at the foot.

<!-- _class: content -->

`Why merging ended it`

## Five fixes asked how far the marker should drop. The sixth stopped asking.

Each earlier answer was arithmetic: 21 stamp class names pushed a fixed row when the shapes sat at three different heights; a unitless `calc()` that discarded the whole transform and put both pills back in one band; a logo reserve that landed a pill inside the mark it was written to clear.

Two segments in one flex row cannot collide with each other, and a single centered box has one neighbor set to clear instead of four. So the reserves are deleted rather than re-derived.

## Thirteen shapes, and none of them has to be cleared.

- Anchored right
  - `tab`, `flag` and `pin` at the corner; six more sit lower. All of them top.
- Full width
  - `notch` is a hairline band across the top edge. Also top.
- Full bleed
  - `mark` and `veil` cover the capsule on purpose, from the plane above.

A stamp paints along the top. The capsule berths at the foot. There is nothing left to reserve.
