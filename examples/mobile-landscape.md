## Fixing SMB costs $2.1M; exiting frees nine sellers for mid-market.

- Fix the segment
  - A guided setup, a lighter tier and pooled support: about $2.1M next year for a 20-month payback.

  - Move 640 accounts to a partner and nine sellers to mid-market, where payback is 17 months.

`Where it leaks`

## SMB pays back in 31 months.

Three findings from the cohort review.

- Stalls before the proposal
  - Half of SMB deals stop between the demo and the proposal stage.
- Onboarding assumes staff
  - Our onboarding needs an admin that most SMB accounts do not have.
- Churn gives a third back
  - Churn returned a third of the new ARR the segment added this quarter.

---

<!-- _class: list-steps -->


## How the exit would run.

1. Announce — November, with twelve months' notice to every SMB account.
2. Migrate — the certified partner onboards accounts from January to June.
3. Redeploy — nine sellers join mid-market on 1 December.
4. Report — churn impact at every board meeting until the last account moves.

---

<!-- _class: decision -->


## We recommend exiting SMB by June 2027.

`Decision requested · Q3 FY26`

- Exit SMB
  - Payback back to 14 months by Q3 FY27; nine sellers move to the segment that converts.
- Why not fix it
  - $2.1M to reach a 20-month payback on a segment that brought in $0.5M of new ARR this quarter.
- Why not wait
  - Every quarter we wait adds roughly 60 accounts we would later have to migrate.
