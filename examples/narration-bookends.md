# Narration that says hello.

`Feature · narration bookends`

A narrated deck can open with a greeting and end with a closing, set once in front matter.

---

## The greeting follows the listener's clock.

- Morning from four, afternoon from noon, and evening from five.
- The time is read where the deck plays, not where it was written.
- A video has no listener, so it says hello.

---

## Two keys, set once in front matter.

- The greeting key takes true, or your own words around the salutation.
- The closing key takes true, or the exact line to end on.
- Leave either out and the deck plays exactly as before.

---

## Each line plays once, and never twice.

- The greeting plays only when narration starts on the first slide.
- The closing plays when narration reaches the end by itself.
- Pausing, rewinding or pressing play again never repeats either.

---

## One rule on every surface.

- Present in the Studio speaks both lines live.
- An exported player carries every greeting and picks one when you press play.
- Captions show both lines, like any other sentence.
