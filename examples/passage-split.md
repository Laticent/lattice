# A long quote keeps every word.

`Feature demo · passage split`

On a phone, a quote that does not fit moves its notes to the next page. A quote longer than a page continues at the end of a sentence.

## A short card stays one page.

`15 U.S.C. §6502(a)(1)`

> It is unlawful for an operator of a website directed to children to collect personal information from a child in violation of the regulations.

- Applies to sites aimed at children under 13.

## The notes move to their own page.

`Cal. Civ. Code §1798.140(o) · CCPA/CPRA`

> "Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular consumer or household.

- In plain English: any data tied to a household or device, not just a named person. IP addresses, cookie IDs and device fingerprints are all in scope.
- **What we must do.**
  - Treat household identifiers as personal information in our notice, retention and access-request workflows.

## A long quote continues at a sentence.

`Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100(a) · CCPA/CPRA`

> A business that controls the collection of a consumer's personal information shall, at or before the point of collection, inform consumers of the categories of personal information to be collected. It shall also state the purposes for which each category is collected or used. A business shall not collect additional categories without notice. It shall not use collected information for a purpose incompatible with the disclosed one. The notice shall say whether the information is sold or shared. It shall state how long the business intends to keep each category.

- The cite stays above the quote on each page.

## One amended clause, its why on the next page.

`Cal. Civ. Code §1798.135 · amendment SB-362 (2024)`

> A business shall provide <del>two or more</del> <ins>at least one</ins> designated method for opt-out requests. The method shall include a clear and conspicuous link on the homepage. The link shall be titled "Your Privacy Choices."

- **Why this matters.** SB-362 pins a single link title, so the homepage and the request workflow need one shared label.
- **What changes.** The footer link is renamed in every market, not only in California.
