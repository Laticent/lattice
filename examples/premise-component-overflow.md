# Rows that grow

`premise` cut any term past about eleven characters — `Communication` rendered as `Communic…`. The term column now sizes itself to the longest term, the split gives the ledger room, and the gap between rows follows how many there are.

## Senior engineers multiply, not add.

Five skills separate an engineer who ships from one who lifts the team around them.

1. Communication
   - Say the hard thing clearly and early.
   - Who needs to know?
2. Prioritization
   - Choose the work that compounds.
   - What matters most now?
3. Mentorship
   - Grow the people beside you.
   - Who can do this next?
4. Architecture
   - Shape systems that outlast you.
   - What will this cost later?
5. Accountability
   - Own outcomes, not tasks.
   - Did it actually land?

`What changed`

## Three moves, one component

- One term column
  - The ledger is a single grid and each row is a subgrid of it, so the term column is sized once, to the longest term, instead of a fixed 140px per row.
- A flexible split
  - The claim rail takes spare width first up to 34%, and gives it back to the ledger down to a 26% floor when the rows need it.
- Rhythm by count
  - Three rows get a loose gap, four or five a medium one, six or seven a snug one, and eight the tight rhythm the layout always had.
