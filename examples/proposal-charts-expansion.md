# Seven charts that plot a value.

Bar · stacked bar · line · waterfall · scatter · slope · bullet

---

<!-- _class: bar -->

`FY26 plan`

## Growth is concentrated in two regions.

- North America `$4.2M`
- EMEA `$3.1M`
- APAC `$1.8M`
- LATAM `$0.6M`

*The value sits on the canvas above each bar, so no axis is needed to read it — and one series takes one hue, because color that encodes nothing is the amateur tell.*

<!-- _class: waterfall -->

`FY26 plan · actual`

## Price paid for the volume we lost.

- Plan `12.0M`
- Price `+1.4M`
- Volume `-0.8M`
- Mix `-0.6M`
- FX `-0.3M`
- Cost base `-1.9M`
- Actual `9.8M`

*The sign is the syntax: a signed value floats as a step, a bare one anchors to zero as a level. The connectors are what make it a bridge rather than a row of boxes.*
