## How to write a risk matrix.

- One bullet per risk: its name, then `{likelihood, impact}`, each a whole number from 1 to 5.
- Add a second `{likelihood, impact}` for where mitigation takes it; the chart draws an arrow there.
- Bands come from likelihood × impact: 1–4 low, 5–9 medium, 10–14 high, 15–25 critical.
- Indent bullets under a risk for its owner and plan; they open on tap in Present.
