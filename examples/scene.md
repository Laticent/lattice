# The scene layout — motion, frozen for paper

`Imagery · Anima poster still`

A `scene` puts an Anima motion scene on a slide as its poster still. The PDF shows the still; the live surfaces animate it. Like `image`, it reads the poster's shape and resolves the composition for you.
