# Split panels hold their frame

`Fix · split-panel · split-compare`

The running chrome stays on its field, and the finish covers both sides.

---

<!-- _class: split-compare -->

`Why Lattice`

## Drag-the-box tools vs. a deck that is already designed.

Both put pixels on a slide. Only one keeps its taste when the content changes.

- Slide editors
  - You place every box, line, and color by hand
  - Restyling means re-touching slides one by one
  - Consistency depends on whoever edited last
- Lattice
  - You write the content; the engine composes the slide
  - One theme restyles the whole deck, every slide at once
  - The design system holds the line on every slide

> Chrome ends at the rail; the finish runs across both sides.
