# A price list you can read on a phone

`Auto-split · pricing`

Three tiers side by side need a wide page. On a tall one they used to lose two of the three. Now the comparison pages instead.

## What this deck shows

A cover leads in, then each tier gets a page of its own, keeping the component's
own card — the featured flag, the accent border, the tinted fill.

Every page carries `Option N of M · comparing …`, derived from the tiers rather
than authored. The `two` and `four` variants follow the same rule, and a wide
page still renders every tier at once.

## Why a comparison is allowed to page

Tiers on separate pages do read weaker than tiers side by side. That cost is
real, and it is smaller than the one it replaces: unsplit at this size the slide
showed one tier of three, cut mid-word, on a two-thirds empty page.

A comparison you page through beats a comparison two thirds of which is off the
page — and the forward signal is what holds the run together.
