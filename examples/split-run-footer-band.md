# The band a run needs

`Auto-split · the footer Cell`

---

<!-- _class: content -->

## A run is not a slide

- A standalone sovereign slide claims the canvas
  - It suppresses the footer band on purpose: one statement, and no furniture around it.
- A split run is multi-page
  - It cannot be read without wayfinding — which page is this, and is there more to come.

---

<!-- _footer: "split-panel pullquote · said once, on the page that opens the run" -->
<!-- _class: split-panel pullquote -->

> The marks belong in the band, not nailed to the section that happens to hold them.

`the forward pointer`

- Docked, never appended
  - Without the Cell the pointer was appended to the section, and on this layout the section is
    the panel row itself, so the mark became a third column and squeezed the content panel.
- The band is reserved on the section
  - That is what makes the content panel end above it, which is what makes content exceeding
    the panel read as ordinary overflow the engine already measures.

---

<!-- _class: premise -->

## The same band, a different sovereign layout

- Before this change
  - The forward pointer floated in the middle of the slide, far from the rail and the number,
    because the layout had no Cell for it to dock into.
- After
  - All three marks read as one line at the foot of the page, in the band.

---

<!-- _class: content -->

## What did not change

- The caption rule
  - A run's own `_footer:` is still said once, on the page that opens the run.
- The page number and the rail
  - Both still ride every page; they simply have a home now.
