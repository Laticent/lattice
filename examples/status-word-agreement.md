## A capitalized status now tints its card.

- Backlog
  - Lowercase control `S`
    - platform `at-risk`
  - Upper case `M`
    - platform `AT-RISK`
- In progress
  - Title case `M`
    - data `Blocked`
  - Upper pilot `S`
    - data `PILOT`
- Review
  - Mixed case `S`
    - infra `On-Track`
  - No status `S`
    - infra

*Capitals used to paint an untinted card.*
