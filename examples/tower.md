# A reinsurance program, layer by layer

## How to write a tower.

- One bullet per layer: its name, then its size as `$40M xs $50M` (limit, then attachment). `x/s` and `excess of` work too.
- A size with no `xs` is the retention, the part the client keeps.
- Under each layer, one bullet per reinsurer with its share, such as `Acme Re 30%`. What is left over shows as Open.
- Layers are drawn at the same height so small bottom layers stay readable. Their sizes are printed beside them.
