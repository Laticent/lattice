# Write for the room

`Typography · venue: conference`

Name where the deck will be seen. The engine sizes the type for the back row.

---

<!-- _class: table table-fill -->
<!-- _footer: "The four venues · one per rung of the scale" -->

`Four venues`

## Pick the room, not the font size.

| Venue | Who is watching | Back row | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `laptop` | You, reading on your own screen | 3 screen-heights | 1x |
| `huddle` | 4–6 people around a TV | 4.5 | 1.15x |
| `conference` | 10–30 people | 5–7 | 1.3x |
| `hall` | 50–2,000 people | 7–10 | 1.5x |

`What a venue sets`

## One line of front matter, three effects.

- Type size
  - Body text at 1.3x, the rung for a conference room.
- Label lift
  - Eyebrows, captions and page numbers grow a little more.
- Word budgets
  - `lint:deck` warns when a slide is too full for the room.

---

<!-- _class: list takeaway -->
<!-- _footer: "One size per deck · the engine never mixes sizes" -->

`One size per deck`

## A slide too full for the room sets the size for the whole deck.

- Every slide renders at the rung all of them fit.
- The export names the slides to trim for the full size.
- Nothing is clipped, and nothing changes size between slides.

---

<!-- _class: list takeaway -->
<!-- _footer: "At venue: hall · fewer words, not only bigger ones" -->

`Venue: hall`

## In a hall, bigger type is not enough.

- Even 1.5x falls short of comfortable from the back row.
- Say less on each slide: a statement or a number.
