## When NOT to reach for title.

- Mid-deck statements
  - Use `big-number` or `content` for emphatic statements inside a deck. Reaching for the title chrome mid-deck breaks the bookend signal.
- Multi-line h1
  - Keep the h1 to one editorial line. The layout is centered and large — two-line titles get cramped and lose impact.
- Header or footer overrides
  - Don't blank the footer with an empty `footer=""` in a title slide's record. The footer is the deck's standing note (a "Confidential", a copyright), and a cover is where a reader most expects it. Drop it on purpose with `no-footer`, or every band with `silent`.
