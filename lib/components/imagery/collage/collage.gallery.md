## When NOT to reach for collage.

- More than six photos
  - Past six, tiles get too small to read from the back of the room, and the layout falls back to a plain even grid. Split the photos across two collage slides.
- Photos used as decoration
  - A collage of generic stock images fills space and says nothing. Use photos the audience should look at.
- Captions on every photo
  - There are no per-photo captions. Say what the photos show once, in the description paragraph, and let the alt text carry the rest.
