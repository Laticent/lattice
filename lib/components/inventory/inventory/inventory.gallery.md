## When NOT to reach for inventory.

- More than five items
  - Past five rows at 16:9 the looks lose scannability (cards and timeline past four). Escalate to list-tabular or split across slides.
- Ordered steps
  - If sequence carries meaning, use list-steps or `list takeaway` over a `1.` list. inventory entries are parallel, of similar weight.

---

<!-- @meta{layout=cards-grid, class=claim-hero, footer=Anti-patterns · inventory · 2 of 2} -->

## When NOT to reach for inventory.

- Inline bold lead
  - Nest the description under the name (`- Name` / `  - description`). A one-line `- **Lead.** detail` is a lint error; `lint:deck --fix` rewrites it.
- Lopsided density
  - Equalize the prose when one entry has three sentences and the rest have one — uneven density unbalances every look.
