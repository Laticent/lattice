`The water cycle`

## Nothing is spent — only moved.

- Evaporation
  - The sun lifts water from ocean and leaf into vapor.
- Condensation
  - Vapor cools into cloud, a reservoir held aloft.
- Precipitation
  - Rain and snow return the water to the ground.
- Collection
  - Rivers gather it back to the sea, and it begins again.

## Criteria are gates: clear them in order.

1. State the bar
   - Each criterion is a pass-or-fail line, not a preference.
2. Order by veto power
   - The criterion most likely to kill goes first.
3. Keep the list short
   - Three gates decide; six gates stall.

## Steps promise an order and keep it.

1. Name the step
   - A verb-first title and one clause of body.
2. Keep steps parallel
   - Same shape every step, or sequence breaks.
3. Number honestly
   - Order is the promise this layout makes.
4. Stop at five
   - Six fits; seven is two slides.
