Does status still read at a glance from the back row, on a real projector? Renders a deck (or takes a rendered `.html`), then puts each slide through the two losses a boardroom adds: projector washout (stray room light lifts the blacks; `--room=lit` is about 20:1, `dim` about 100:1, `none` skips it) and viewing blur (a Gaussian sized from the venue's back-row distance, `VENUE_BACK_ROW` in lib/core/resolve-venue.js, for a 20/40 viewer, plus a 20/200 "squint" view). Then it finds every status mark in the DOM (chart `data-s` members, state discs, checklist discs, kpi pills) and measures each on the simulated slide with its glyphs hidden: SEPARATION, the better of fill-vs-surround and edge-vs-surround, against a 3:1 back-row floor; and DISTINCTNESS, the closest two statuses on a slide by OKLab distance, flagged below 0.08 (report-only, proposed). Writes a contact sheet per deck (as painted, then each venue's back-row and squint views, failing marks boxed red). Report by default; `--strict` exits 1 on a mark below the floor. `--venues=conference,hall` (default), `--palette=`, `--out=`, `--json`. Exists because every contrast gate here scores the pixels as painted, and a pale pill held off the slide by a 2px border passes all of them while vanishing at the back of a hall.
