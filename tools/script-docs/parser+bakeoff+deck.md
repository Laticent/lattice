The numbers the deck-parser pipeline (engineering/decisions/2026-10-22-segno-deck-pipeline.md) is held to. `--audit` (the default) times, on three decks, markdown-it's parse against a full render, one lint, and counts how often lint calls `parse` and re-runs the `\r\n?` normalization. `--census` counts the modules that read deck structure themselves: a syntax-tree walk of lib/, docs/src/ and tools/ for private markdown-it builds and fence, slide-boundary and directive regular expressions (classified by what each pattern matches, not how it is spelled), joined to V8 precise coverage of one render and one lint of the gallery. `--arms` is the corpus differential harness: markdown-it's block structure against the staged arms (hand-written and Segno-generated line pass) on every tracked deck and doc, counted only at exact parity. `--runs N` repeats `--audit` or `--arms` in N separate processes and prints each figure's median and range with the CPU model; `--json` for machine output. On-demand, not a gate.
